## Summary

**Follow-up change (20 October 2026):** the online updater now excludes all 58 NJT law records and the NJT catalogue. Use the current NJT consolidated page as a human-reviewed official reference, record its displayed date, and consult a Gazette item only for a specific material later change or commencement issue. This is a workable source lane without a Gazette-by-Gazette reconstruction. Automated NJT capture remains unavailable under the recorded portal terms. The OIF/NAIH/AJBH maintenance lane remains separate and still has the operational limits below.

The OIF rule is **fragile**: it uses official parent pages and stable-looking direct links, but many records lack source-specific identity checks, PDFs receive no OCR quality review, and each ordinary run makes up to 128 requests across the pack without request timeouts, backoff, or source-specific host restrictions. NAIH and AJBH rules have similar identity and access uncertainties. Court discovery remains a lead only, with weak automatic coverage of query-driven results.

This is a source-rule assessment, not a finding on legal force or current law. It does not assess GitHub mirror synchronization.

## Cross-cutting design risks

- Online check/sync now covers 41 non-link-only OIF/NAIH/AJBH records; NJT and the three AJBH link-only records are excluded. The online lane still needs ordinary request pacing and timeout improvements. It has no request timeout, retry/backoff, 429 handling, or inter-request delay.
- `fetchOfficial` permits redirects to any hostname in the pack-wide list of ten official hosts instead of requiring the source's expected host or an explicit alias set.
- `TextDecoder` is non-fatal by default, so malformed UTF-8 can be replaced silently. The HTML decoder supports only a selected set of named entities.
- The sync path saves successful records if another record fails and reports partial success; the blocked AJBH record is nevertheless included in the active set, so it makes a clean full-source run impossible until reclassified or resolved.
- `--discover` now excludes the prohibited NJT route and scans only permitted non-NJT catalogues; query-driven court coverage remains human-led.

## Repair priorities

1. **Use the official NJT consolidation as a manual source reference.** The online updater excludes NJT law and catalogue requests. Record the displayed version; use an exact Gazette amendment only for a material post-cutoff change, commencement issue, or discrepancy. No complete amendment chain is required.
2. **Keep automation separate from the legal text reference.** The existing OIF/NAIH/AJBH updater remains independent of NJT. Improve its pacing, timeouts, redirect checks, and source identity only where needed for recurring maintenance; ordinary research does not depend on those updater fixes.
3. **Decouple adapters and run modes.** Allow OIF/NAIH/AJBH checks and catalogue discovery without requesting NJT sources; add per-publisher pacing, finite request timeouts, 429 handling, expected-host redirect rules and strict character decoding. Acceptance condition: a failure or blocked publisher does not trigger requests to unrelated publishers or mark a fresh check.
4. **Strengthen source identity and PDF conversion.** Add an expected title, publisher, record number and visible edition/date marker for each active page/PDF. For scanned PDFs, record OCR confidence and require reviewer inspection below a threshold. Acceptance condition: wrong-page, wrong-document, partial-page, and malformed-encoding fixtures fail closed; reviewer comparison confirms tables, article numbering, footnotes and annexes.
5. **Complete AJBH route review.** Current Ombudsman, equal-treatment, and public-interest reporting routes, forms, deadlines, and competent units remain unverified. The three identified PDFs are link-only and do not block other records. The whole-site offer is in `references/human-review-offers.md`.
6. **Recheck the Act CL/2016 legacy conversion.** The manifest and filename now use `hu-act-2016-150`; compare the historical converted text with the official dated consolidation before accepting any local copy.
7. **Continue family-status legal mapping.** Act I/2010 is now recorded as an official link-only reference. Recognition, translation, authentication, and evidentiary rules for foreign records remain open; the broader topic is not covered by this link alone.
7. **Recheck the prior Act CL/2016 conversion finding.** The 15 October register noted navigation artifacts in its converted text. The current converter targets NJT's legal-content container, but no saved-output comparison or replay was performed in this audit. Acceptance condition: compare the full converted text against the official human-viewed source and retain only the intended body.
