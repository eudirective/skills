## Scope

This review assesses the official link routes and the historical local-capture results separately. A reviewed government link can be used for ordinary reference with its publisher, identity, scope, version date, and caveats recorded. It does not require a local capture, hash, conversion, or Gazette-chain reconstruction. Link acceptance does not establish complete legal topic coverage or country completion.

## Dependency review

The Bulgarian EU-citizen Act's Article 10 and definitions still refer to Regulation (EU) 2019/1157. The Court of Justice declared that regulation invalid in C-61/22 while maintaining its effects until a replacement entered into force. Council Regulation (EU) 2025/1208 entered into force on 10 July 2025 and governs current EU identity/residence-document security and format standards. The national Act's text is not altered; the relationship is recorded separately, with the relevant EU sources and a follow-up question in source-dependencies.json.
