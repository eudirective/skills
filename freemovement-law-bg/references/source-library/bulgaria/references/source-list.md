# Bulgaria source discovery register

Research date and latest official-reference reviews: 29 October 2026; original capture pass: 19 October 2026
Scope: official national sources relating to EU/EEA/Swiss family mobility, entry and residence, visas, administrative procedure, review, privacy, and oversight. Bulgaria is an EU Member State; each national route was checked independently. The comparison leads were the Denmark, Sweden, Czechia, Hungary, and Romania source reviews.

This is a bounded register, not a complete inventory of Bulgarian law. It contains 45 catalogue entries. Twenty-nine official-reference locators have been reviewed for use within stated scope or cutoff; locators are not counted as retained source texts. The acceptance register records 21 accepted local artifacts: 19 retained files in `documents/` and two independently authored scope notes that do not reproduce the cited legal texts. The 19 October capture run converted 24 of its original 35 leads and recorded 11 failures or blocks; those are historical retrieval outcomes, not current source-availability blockers. Source acceptance, local-copy status, topic coverage, and country completion are separate. This table is the current source catalogue; individual reference-use decisions and accepted local-file paths are in source-acceptance.json, material cross-references are in source-dependencies.json, and discovery, disposition, retrieval, and capture working files are in the EU Source Update Monitor's local-only archive.
