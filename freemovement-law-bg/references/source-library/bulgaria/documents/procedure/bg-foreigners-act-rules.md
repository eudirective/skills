## Source identity and scope

The MVR Migration Directorate index identifies the Rules for implementation of the Foreigners Act as a separate national instrument. The MVR 2026-labelled PDF recorded in the official source register is the implementing instrument adopted by Council of Ministers Decree No. 179/2011, with its amendment history shown through State Gazette issue 7/2026 (20 January 2026).

The Rules govern implementation of the Foreigners Act, including entry, residence, and departure procedures for third-country nationals. Article 1 of the recorded text includes specified family members of Bulgarian citizens who are not nationals of an EU Member State, an EEA State, or Switzerland. This is the general third-country-national regime. It does not replace Bulgaria's separate free-movement regime for EU citizens and eligible family members.
