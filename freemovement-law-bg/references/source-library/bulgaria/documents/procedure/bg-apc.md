## Subject and source role

The Code governs administrative decision-making, challenges to individual administrative acts, and judicial review of administrative acts and subordinate legislation. It is a general procedural statute, not an immigration or residence-rights instrument. It may supply procedure for challenges involving immigration decisions, but the competent forum, applicable special rules, deadlines, and suspensive effects must be checked in the governing legislation and the decision itself.

The MVR-hosted consolidation is a government-published official reference. The Ministry of Justice normdoc is a useful second official route. This file identifies and scopes those texts; it does not reproduce the Code or certify every provision as current beyond the printed/indexed cutoff.
