## Historical operational content

The preserved official-page capture explains that applicants cannot access an online case-status account and that the Directorate’s waiting-time page identifies the application month currently being processed. It says applicants whose cases remain in the queue should not expect status updates by phone, email or chat.

The captured page says that in 2025 the average time for approved EEA/EFTA family-card applications was seven months. It explicitly warns that an exact individual decision date cannot be predicted. It also describes how to withdraw an application, submit additional documents, notify the Directorate of changed circumstances, and the point at which spouses may work while an application is pending.

The seven-month figure is historical 2025 information, not a current estimate. The Directorate’s separate current waiting-time page is the live source for current aggregate status and expressly cautions that averages do not predict an individual pending case.
