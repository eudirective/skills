# Iceland: moving guidance and return by Icelandic citizens

**Source ID:** is-16  
**Publisher:** Ísland.is / Registers Iceland  
**Official page:** <https://island.is/en/life-events/moving>  
**Checked:** 29 October 2026  
**Language:** English

## Page role and summary

This is a general government moving-services hub, not an immigration-status decision. It directs people moving to Iceland or returning from abroad to Registers Iceland and describes address-registration steps. The page says moves generally must be registered within seven days, and describes identity-document and in-person steps for people moving back to Iceland. It directs Nordic movers to their separate form and procedure.

The page also covers domestic moves, moves abroad, minors’ domicile, couples, schools and practical matters. Those topics are outside the present residence-source scope and are not summarized here. For foreign nationals, the page directs readers to distinct rules based on Nordic, EEA/EFTA or other nationality; it does not merge those routes.
