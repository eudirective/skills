# Iceland: residence card for EEA/EFTA family members

**Source ID:** is-08  
**Publisher:** Directorate of Immigration / Ísland.is  
**Official page:** <https://island.is/en/residence-right-of-family-members-of-eea-efta-citizens>  
**Checked:** 29 October 2026  
**Language:** English

## Scope

The Directorate’s page describes a residence-card route for non-EEA/EFTA immediate family members whose residence derives from an EEA/EFTA citizen residing in Iceland. The displayed family categories include spouses (marital or cohabiting), specified descendants and dependent relatives in the ascending line, and certain custodial parents of Icelandic EEA/EFTA children. The page limits the student sponsor route to spouses and descendants under 21.

The page describes a special return route for close relatives of Icelandic citizens where the Icelandic citizen previously exercised residence rights in another EEA/EFTA state and the pair lived together there immediately before returning. Where that route does not apply, the page directs non-EEA/EFTA relatives of Icelandic citizens to the ordinary residence-permit route.

The page says the application is made on paper to the Directorate and displays a fee of ISK 8,000. It also describes where an application may be delivered and how an applicant may request confirmation of submission.
