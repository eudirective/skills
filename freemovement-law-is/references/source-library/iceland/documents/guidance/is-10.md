# Iceland: rights and obligations of EEA/EFTA family residence-card holders

**Source ID:** is-10  
**Publisher:** Directorate of Immigration / Ísland.is  
**Official page:** <https://island.is/en/residence-right-of-family-members-of-eea-efta-citizens/residence-card>  
**Checked:** 29 October 2026  
**Language:** English

## Rights described by the Directorate

The page states that a family-member residence card is valid for five years, or for a shorter period if the EEA/EFTA sponsor’s right to reside ends sooner. It says a card may expire following more than six months outside Iceland in a year, subject to special reasons.

The page distinguishes work before and after issuance. It says marital and cohabiting spouses may begin working once their residence-card application is submitted. It describes a system ID application route for a marital spouse who has found employment, and says cohabiting spouses cannot use that system-ID route. After issuance, it says family members may work without a work permit.

The page also states that family members acquire a permanent right of residence after five years of continuous legal residence.
