# Iceland: permanent residence right for EEA/EFTA family members

**Source ID:** is-11  
**Publisher:** Directorate of Immigration / Ísland.is  
**Official page:** <https://island.is/en/permanent-residence-for-family-members-of-eea-efta-citizens>  
**Checked:** 29 October 2026  
**Language:** English

## Route described by the Directorate

The page states that an EEA/EFTA family member acquires a permanent right of residence after five years of continuous lawful residence in Iceland while holding the relevant family-member residence card. It says the right is cancelled after more than two continuous years outside Iceland.

For continuity, the page lists absences totaling less than six months in a year, military service, and an absence of up to one year for compelling reasons (such as pregnancy, childbirth, serious illness, education or training, or certain seconded employment) as not interrupting residence. It describes exceptional circumstances in which the five-year period may not be required, referring to provisions of the Foreign Nationals Act concerning a sponsor’s earlier permanent status or death.

The page says applications are submitted in original paper form, describes delivery routes and an ISK 8,000 registration fee, and states that expiry of the five-year residence card does not itself require a new application for the permanent right; a replacement card requires a photo appointment and fee.
