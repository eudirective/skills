## Guidance and recorded discrepancy

The official page concerns registration of EEA/EFTA citizens with Registers Iceland, including legal domicile and an Icelandic ID number. It separates Nordic nationals to a distinct registration route and gives processing information after required documents are received.

The page currently presents inconsistent duration language: its heading and form describe registration for stays exceeding six months, while its body says EEA/EFTA citizens staying longer than three months must register. The Directorate’s separate entry-requirements page says six months. The official consolidated legal sources and the responsible authorities must be consulted to resolve the applicable threshold; this service page cannot settle it.
