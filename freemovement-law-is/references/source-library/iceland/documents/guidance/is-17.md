# Iceland: entry requirements guidance

**Source ID:** is-17  
**Publisher:** Directorate of Immigration / Ísland.is  
**Official page:** <https://island.is/en/entry-requirements-to-iceland>  
**Checked:** 29 October 2026  
**Language:** English

## Guidance summarized

The page separates entry information by nationality. For EEA/EFTA citizens it describes the travel-document requirements and says that registration is required when residing in Iceland longer than six months. It gives Nordic citizens a border-crossing exemption for travel between Iceland and other Nordic countries.

For non-EEA/EFTA visitors it describes passport-validity conditions, the Schengen short-stay limit, visa or residence-permit conditions and general admission requirements. For non-EEA/EFTA nationals intending to reside longer than 90 days, it directs readers to apply for a residence permit. Those visitor rules are outside the narrower EEA family-card route and should be checked against current Schengen and Icelandic law.

## Registration-threshold conflict

This page’s six-month statement differs from the body of the Registers Iceland EEA/EFTA registration page, which says three months even though that page’s heading and form say six months. The discrepancy remains open in the Iceland source register. This summary records what each official service page displays; it does not resolve the legal threshold.
