## Document guidance summarized

The Directorate page places responsibility on the applicant to provide satisfactory evidence and warns that inadequate evidence may delay or defeat an application. It describes evidence categories including:

- Passport identity and signature pages.
- Proof of the qualifying family relationship, such as marriage or birth records; cohabiting partners are directed to provide civil-status and cohabitation evidence.
- Custody evidence where relevant.
- Evidence of dependency for adult descendants or dependent relatives in the ascending line, together with evidence of the EEA/EFTA citizen’s ability to support them.
- Evidence of sufficient support, which may include employment or self-employment income, recent payslips and tax information, regular payments, bank funds, or grants and study loans.

The page also explains document form requirements. Foreign originals generally require legal authentication by apostille or chain authentication, while Icelandic originals do not. It distinguishes certified copies from ordinary copies and gives translation requirements for documents outside English or a Nordic language. It describes when a certified, authenticated declaration may be requested.
