## Source role and currentness

The official record identifies Regulation No. 540/2017, titled *Reglugerð um útlendinga*, and lists amendments that link back to the base regulation. The record is useful for confirming the instrument identity and following its amendment chain.

The linked base text is not accepted here as a current consolidated text. The record lists later amendments to it, including Regulations No. 669/2026, 724/2026 and 725/2026. Their reviewed effect is summarized below. Consult each amendment and the currently applicable law; do not read the old base text alone as the law in force.

## Verified 2026 amendments

- **Regulation No. 669/2026**: removes § 41 and replaces the term “Dublin cooperation” in the heading and paragraphs of § 47 with “AMMR cooperation.” The regulation took effect on publication, while the § 47 terminology change took effect 1 July 2026. The Gazette record gives publication date 11 June 2026.
- **Regulation No. 724/2026**: changes the priority rules for processing complete residence-permit applications; adds an exception to the continuous-residence rule for certain spouses or registered partners of Icelandic citizens working abroad for Iceland or an international organization; adds § 38a on assessment of document reliability and risk; repeals § 43b; and adds a residence-permit renewal route of up to 12 months for certain doctoral graduates seeking work. The text states immediate effect and the Gazette record gives publication date 26 June 2026.
- **Regulation No. 725/2026**: replaces Chapter II, renaming and revising the short-term residence-permit rules. It describes permits of up to 180 days, eligible purposes, application evidence and refusal/revocation grounds. The regulation states immediate effect and the Gazette record gives publication date 26 June 2026.

The live base-regulation index also lists amendments Nos. 1137/2025, 649/2025, 1069/2024, 1036/2024, 746/2024, 808/2023, 629/2023, 1242/2022, 841/2022, 818/2022, 802/2022, 1048/2020, 830/2020, 769/2020, 656/2020, 460/2020, 335/2020, 305/2020, 122/2020, 638/2019, 501/2019, 276/2018 and 775/2017.
