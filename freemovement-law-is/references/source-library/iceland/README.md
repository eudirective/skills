# Iceland source pack

Iceland is an EEA EFTA State, not an EU Member State. This pack concerns EEA/EFTA free movement and third-country family members, entry and residence documents, procedures and remedies, and related public services. Icelandic EEA/EFTA, Swiss, Nordic, Icelandic-return and ordinary family-reunification routes remain distinct.

The [source list](references/source-list.md) records 36 official source references. The [source acceptance register](references/source-acceptance.json) accepts 36 external links within stated scopes; accepted local summaries: 9 (is-05, is-08 through is-11, is-13, is-14, is-16 and is-17); accepted full statutory copies: 0; accepted topics: 0; country review: incomplete. A link acceptance does not establish topic coverage or country completion.

The source set includes the current Alþingi consolidated Visa Act No. 37/2026 (in force from 12 May 2026) and the Directorate of Immigration’s current waiting-time page. The latter was last updated 7 October 2026 and reports selected-category processing status and averages among approvals so far in 2026; it is not an estimate for an individual pending case. The older EEA/EFTA family-card page reports a 2025 average and is retained only as historical guidance.

The 20 original converted review notes are preserved in the Monitor local-only research archive, not in this pack. The maintained [is-05 summary](documents/guidance/is-05.md) records the official Regulation 540/2017 amendment index and the three listed 2026 amendment texts; it does not treat the stale base text as a current consolidation. Summaries for [is-08](documents/guidance/is-08.md), [is-09](documents/guidance/is-09.md), [is-10](documents/guidance/is-10.md) and [is-11](documents/guidance/is-11.md) preserve the Directorate’s family-card eligibility, evidence, holder-rights and permanent-residence guidance. Additional records cover [is-13](documents/guidance/is-13.md) historical application guidance, [is-14](documents/guidance/is-14.md) the unresolved registration-threshold discrepancy, [is-16](documents/guidance/is-16.md) the moving hub and [is-17](documents/guidance/is-17.md) entry guidance. Each states its verification limits. Ten of 21 raw responses are indexed there; the remaining 11 are still in `/tmp/public-data/temp/iceland/raw`. Monitor’s automatic review rejected some exports, local screening found credential or page-state markers in others, and one large response exceeded the tool-output limit. All 21 raw-file hashes match the capture-results manifest. No local source copy is accepted.

Open issues include the three-month/six-month registration conflict in official guidance; court-search coverage and ongoing case-law discovery; the judicial-remedy route for immigration decisions; current mission-specific visa instructions; and machine-readable provision-level coverage and dependency mapping, plus topic-level legal analysis.
