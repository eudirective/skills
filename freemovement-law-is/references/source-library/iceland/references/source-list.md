## Findings

- Iceland is an EEA EFTA State. The official EFTA record identifies Directive 2004/38/EC as incorporated by Joint Committee Decision 158/2007 and in force in the EEA. Icelandic EEA/EFTA law, Swiss treatment, Nordic routes, and Icelandic return-citizen rules remain distinct.
- Current official Alþingi consolidated pages for Acts 80/2016, 37/1993, 2/1993, 140/2012, and 90/2018 are usable Icelandic-language references in Lagasafn edition 158a dated 1 October 2026. Earlier direct-capture 403s do not make these official links unusable. Parts of Act 48/2026 take effect on 1 December 2026 and must be kept separate from law already in force.
- The official Regulation 540/2017 page is accepted as an instrument-identity and amendment index, not as a current consolidation. Amendments 669/2026, 724/2026, and 725/2026 are separately accepted as official links. The legal effect of those changes on any particular EEA/EFTA issue still needs to be mapped.
- The EEA/EFTA registration page says over three months in its body, while its title/form and the entry-requirements page say over six months. Offer the relevant official service portal for human review and resolve the threshold against the accepted statute; do not encode one threshold as settled.
- Visa Act No. 37/2026 took effect on 12 May 2026; the current Alþingi consolidation is accepted as the primary official visa-law reference. The Ministry for Foreign Affairs is identified in the Act as the responsible ministry; Schengen dependencies, implementation and mission procedures remain to be mapped.
- The current Directorate waiting-time page was last updated 7 October 2026 and shows selected application categories and aggregate status/averages for 2026; it does not give an individual pending-case estimate.
- Rules No. 3/2022 are the current official court-publication rules located in this review, and the current Courts Act No. 50/2016 and Civil Procedure Act No. 91/1991 are accepted official links. The judicial route for immigration decisions remains to be mapped; they took effect on 1 October 2022 and repealed Rules No. 3/2019. The former Courts Administration domain routes to the current Ísland.is Courts portal. Portal search and corpus completeness remain human-review items.
- KNU decisions are published through the Government portal after the outcome is communicated to the appellant and are redacted. Their collection is selective; it does not establish complete administrative case-law coverage.

Thirty-six official external references are accepted within their listed scopes. No local copies or topics are accepted, and Iceland is not marked complete. The capture/conversion workflow and SGML archive path are optional maintenance mechanisms, not prerequisites for source use.
