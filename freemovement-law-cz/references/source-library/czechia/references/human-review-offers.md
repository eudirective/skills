## Decree 429/2010 — route applicability

- **Publisher:** e-Sbírka, the official Collection of Laws and International Treaties. The Czech Parliament’s official metadata is a corroborating index.
- **Official record:** https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2010/429
- **Retained text:** `documents/legislation/cz-decree-429-2010.md`, publisher version effective 2020-02-01, captured 2026-10-30.
- **Resolved:** Instrument identity, current publisher-marked version, effective cutoff, and full text capture. e-Sbírka labels the consolidated text informative.
- **Unresolved:** Whether the local-mission exceptions apply to EU-family short-stay visa and residence procedures.
- **Reviewer decision requested:** Determine whether the current operative provisions and their enabling rules apply to EU-family filings or whether those routes are governed by distinct provisions.
- **Effect:** Currentness is no longer an open issue. This review offer does not block retaining the dated text, and it does not establish topic coverage or a route-specific legal conclusion. No Gazette-by-Gazette reconstruction is required by default.
