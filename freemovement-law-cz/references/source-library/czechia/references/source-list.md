# Czechia — national legal sources for free movement and family members

**Inventory date:** 29 October 2026. **Status:** 67 source records and 67 scoped official external references. Forty-one dated local source copies are accepted within the register limits (17 e-Sbírka texts, 13 guidance pages, 6 blank forms with native PDFs, and 5 discovery/index snapshots); two incomplete captures have locator notes only. Decree 429/2010's current official version and cutoff are verified (effective 1 February 2020); EU-family route applicability remains pending targeted human review. The MFA local-consular page is accepted only for long-term visa and long-term/permanent residence guidance. No topic is accepted and country review remains incomplete. All 43 former candidate files have a disposition; no files remain under `candidates/`. The source acceptance register records copy hashes and artifact-specific limits. See the [source acceptance register](source-acceptance.json). **Scope:** Czech national legislation, official authorities, courts, and oversight materials relevant to EU/EEA/Swiss free movement, residence, EU-family visa processing, civil-status evidence, procedure, and remedies. EU legislation and CJEU judgments are excluded because they belong in the EU core. Primary legal texts are in Czech; official English guidance is marked separately.

Use e-Sbírka's date/version history for the temporal law applicable to a particular event. Its current-text pages label the consolidated text as informative. Keep publication, entry into force, applicability, end of validity, and operational commencement distinct. The inventory is a scoped starting list, not a claim of completeness. The legal adapter uses e-Sbírka's public page-data service to resolve each stable act record to the current effective version, capture its ordered XHTML fragments, and produce a replay-verified text copy; it does not use the registration-gated REST API. Exact version metadata and response hashes are recorded in the Monitor local research archive at `captures/czechia-candidate-promotion-2026-10-25/Czechia/czechia/e-sbirka/retrieval-manifest.json`. The 17 official e-Sbírka source references are accepted within the dates and limits recorded in `references/source-acceptance.json`; their dated local Markdown copies are accepted as working texts only within the identified version and the informative-text caveat. The 62 historical raw response files remain in the standard project temp tree and are indexed in the Monitor archive. Offline replay of the 17 e-Sbírka legal texts and 13 official HTML guidance/index pages passed on 25 October 2026. The retrieval manifests are indexed at `captures/czechia-candidate-promotion-2026-10-25/Czechia/czechia/e-sbirka/retrieval-manifest.json` and `captures/czechia-candidate-promotion-2026-10-25/Czechia/czechia/html-guidance/retrieval-manifest.json`.
