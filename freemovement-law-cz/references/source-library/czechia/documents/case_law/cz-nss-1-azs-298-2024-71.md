## Scope

Short-stay visa and refusal-review procedure for an applicant claiming dependent-parent status as a family member of a Czech citizen.

## Maintained summary

The applicant, a mother of a Czech citizen, challenged the refusal of her short-stay visa and the handling of her request to inspect the administrative file. The NSS dismissed her cassation appeal. It held that § 168 of the Foreign Nationals Residence Act excludes the relevant parts of the Administrative Procedure Code from the § 20 visa procedure and the § 180e refusal-review procedure, so the asserted § 38 file-inspection right did not apply during those proceedings. The Ministry’s failure to answer the applicant’s second request did not affect the legality of its decision in this case.
