## Scope

Short-stay visa refusal under § 20(5)(e) of the Foreign Nationals Residence Act based on alleged marriage of convenience in an EU-family route.

## Maintained summary

The NSS dismissed the applicant’s cassation appeal. Interpreting Article 35 of Directive 2004/38 and § 20(5)(e), it held that the authorities did not have to establish that both spouses participated in a sham marriage: evidence that the applicant entered the marriage to obtain free-movement or residence rights could suffice. The court reviewed the evidence in its factual context, including positive and negative indicators and the administration’s burden to establish abuse.

## Limits

This is a single fact-specific judgment, not a general finding that a marriage is abusive whenever the relationship appears unusual, nor a replacement for the full decision’s evidentiary analysis. Verify the applicable legal text and full case record before relying on it.
