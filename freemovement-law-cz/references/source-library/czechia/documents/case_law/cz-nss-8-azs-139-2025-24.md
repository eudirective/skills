## Maintained summary

The NSS quashed the regional judgment and police decision and remitted the matter. It held that in proceedings imposing an obligation to leave, police could not decline to assess whether the person had residence fiction under §87y of the Foreign Nationals Residence Act. The residence sticker was not determinative; statutory facts still had to be established.
