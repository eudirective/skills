## Applications covered

The page concerns applications for a Czech long-term visa, long-term residence permit, or permanent residence permit. It states that the place of filing follows the rule of local consular competence. It does not address short-stay Schengen visa applications.

## General rule

An applicant may file only at a Czech consular office with competence for a state meeting at least one of the page's listed connections:

- the applicant is a citizen of that state;
- the applicant has permission for long-term or permanent residence in that state and has lived there continuously for at least two years; or
- that state issued the applicant's travel document.

This summary preserves the conditions as presented by the MFA. It does not resolve how the listed alternatives interact in an individual case.

## Nationality exceptions listed by the MFA

The page says the local-consular-competence rule does not apply to nationals of these states and territories:

Albania; Andorra; Antigua and Barbuda; Argentina; Australia; Bahamas; Barbados; Bosnia and Herzegovina; Brazil; Brunei; Montenegro; Guatemala; Honduras; Hong Kong (special administrative region); Chile; Israel; Japan; South Korea; Canada; Costa Rica; Macao (special administrative region); Malaysia; Mauritius; Mexico; Monaco; Nicaragua; New Zealand; Panama; Paraguay; El Salvador; San Marino; North Macedonia; Seychelles; Singapore; United States; Serbia; Saint Kitts and Nevis; Holy See; Taiwan; Uruguay; United Kingdom; Venezuela.

According to the page, nationals in this list may apply for these long-term and permanent routes at any Czech diplomatic mission. Missions are nevertheless expected to serve primarily people within their own consular districts, and the MFA asks applicants to apply at the mission accredited for their place of residence where possible.

The page also notes that the Czech Republic has no diplomatic mission in every state and some missions are accredited to multiple states. It directs users to the Czech missions directory to identify territorial districts.

## Legal references named by the page

The MFA cites:

- Act No. 326/1999 Coll., on the Residence of Foreign Nationals in the Czech Republic, section 169g;
- Act No. 150/2017 Coll., on the Foreign Service, section 4; and
- Decree No. 429/2010 Coll., concerning exceptions to the duty to apply for a visa or residence permit at the locally competent diplomatic mission.

The decree's current consolidated wording and its applicability to EU-family routes have not been resolved in the source register. The page is administrative guidance and does not replace those legal texts.
