## Scope

Optional registration-certificate guidance for EU/EEA EFTA/Swiss citizens and the specified UK Withdrawal Agreement cohort.

## Maintained summary

The page states that the certificate is optional and not a condition of an EU citizen’s residence. It reports ten-year validity for EU citizens and five-year validity for qualifying UK citizens present by 31 December 2020; it says the certificate cannot be extended and a new application or permanent-residence route may follow expiry.

## Limits

The UK route has its own conditions. This guidance does not replace the residence rights or current statutory rules.
