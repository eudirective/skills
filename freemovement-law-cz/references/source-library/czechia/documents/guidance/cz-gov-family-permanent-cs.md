## Scope

Public-service summary of permanent residence for third-country family members of EU/Czech citizens.

## Maintained summary

The page describes the five-year continuous-residence route and a two-year route for qualifying family members who have had the required family relationship for at least one year to an EU or Czech citizen with permanent residence in Czechia.
