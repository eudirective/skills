## Scope

Public-service summary for applying for temporary residence as an EU citizen’s family member.

## Maintained summary

The service page describes a permit for stays over 90 days and outlines close and extended family-member categories, application route and supporting information. It states that specified applicants must apply within three months of entry or before expiry of an existing permission, depending on the branch.
