## Scope

Category and route index for EU citizens and family members.

## Maintained summary

The portal presents the EU-citizen route and links to registration, temporary and permanent residence procedures. Its source-list description distinguishes the treatment of EU, EEA EFTA and Swiss citizens from the UK Withdrawal Agreement route.
