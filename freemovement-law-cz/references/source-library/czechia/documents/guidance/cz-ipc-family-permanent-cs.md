## Scope

Permanent-residence guidance for third-country family members of EU/EEA EFTA/Swiss citizens, Czech citizens and qualifying UK cases.

## Maintained summary

The page describes the general route after five years of continuous residence and a qualifying two-year route for a close family member where the EU or Czech sponsor has permanent residence and the family relationship has existed for at least one year.

## Limits

Eligibility is fact-specific and subject to the current statute, including continuity rules, exceptions and evidence requirements.
