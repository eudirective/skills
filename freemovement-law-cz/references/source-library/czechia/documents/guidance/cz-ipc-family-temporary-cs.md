## Scope

MOI procedure guidance for third-country family members seeking residence over 90 days.

## Maintained summary

The page covers family members of EU, Icelandic, Liechtenstein, Norwegian, Swiss and Czech citizens and qualifying UK Withdrawal Agreement cases. It distinguishes close and extended family members and describes their relationship and dependency categories. A card may be issued for up to five years for close family members; the page also describes a three-year maximum for other cases.
