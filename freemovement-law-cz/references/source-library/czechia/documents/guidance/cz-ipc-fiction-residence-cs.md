# Residence fiction while an EU-family application is pending

- **Source ID:** `cz-ipc-fiction-residence-cs`
- **Publisher:** Ministry of the Interior of the Czech Republic, Information Portal for Foreigners
- **Language:** Czech

- **Record type:** Project-authored structured summary; not a verbatim or mechanically converted source copy.

## Scope

MOI explanation of lawful-stay fiction during certain pending temporary or permanent residence applications.

## Maintained summary

The page says close family members remain lawfully present until the decision becomes final; for extended family members it describes the period as ending on delivery of the decision. MOI evidences the stay with a long-stay visa sticker in the travel document and can issue a replacement sticker if processing continues beyond its validity.

## Limits

The page lists situations where the fiction does not arise. The statute and facts govern; an evidentiary sticker alone does not establish that statutory conditions are met.
