## Scope

Administrative guidance distinguishing voluntary end of residence from state cancellation for EU citizens and family members.

## Maintained summary

The page treats voluntary termination and state cancellation as separate events and states that residence documents must be returned. For an EU registration certificate, it describes return no later than three days before departure, including a registered-post option.

## Limits

The return instructions differ by document and procedure. Do not infer cancellation grounds, notice or remedies from this summary.
