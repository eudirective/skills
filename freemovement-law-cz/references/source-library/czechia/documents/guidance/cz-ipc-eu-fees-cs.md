## Scope

MOI administrative-fee guidance for EU citizens and family members.

## Maintained summary

The page expressly excludes consular fees. It describes payment at MOI offices by transfer or card and limits cash payment to specified copy, extract and file-inspection services. It explains that some EU-citizen changes recorded only in MOI systems are not charged as document changes.

## Limits

Amounts and fee liability can change and depend on the specific service. Confirm the current schedule; this page does not cover mission fees.
