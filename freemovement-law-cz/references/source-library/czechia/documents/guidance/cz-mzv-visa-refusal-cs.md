## Schengen visa refusal, cancellation or annulment

The MFA says that an applicant receives a standard notice form stating the reasons and available remedy when a Schengen visa is refused, cancelled, or annulled. The listed remedy is a request for reconsideration of the reasons.

The request must reach the consulate that made the decision within 15 days after the applicant receives the standard notice. It may be filed in person or by post and must be signed by the applicant or an authorized representative. The Ministry's Visa Department does not receive these requests and does not forward them to consulates. The applicant should explain why the decision is considered incorrect. The page says that material or documents not included in the original visa application are not considered in the reconsideration.

## Negative opinions after prior consultation

For a refusal based on a negative opinion in prior consultation, the standard form may identify a concern that one or more Schengen states consider the applicant a threat to public order or internal security, public health under Article 2(21) of Regulation (EU) 2016/399, or international relations.

The page says a person may seek protection in Czechia if Czechia was one of the states that entered the negative opinion. The Foreign Police Service Directorate is identified as the Czech authority responsible for prior consultation in visa issuance. If another Schengen state supplied the negative opinion, the applicant is directed to that state.

## Fee, review period and court challenge

The page lists an €80 fee for the reconsideration request, with no fee for family members of EU or Czech citizens. The consulate forwards the request to the MFA, which reviews it within 60 days; the consulate that made the original decision communicates the result.

If the MFA confirms the refusal, the applicant may bring an administrative action within 30 days after receiving the review result. The page identifies the regional court for the district where the applicant would have performed the arrival-reporting duty after entry. The expected Czech address must be clear from the visa application.

## Long-term visas and residence permits

For refusal of a long-term visa or residence permit, the page says an appeal is sent directly to the Ministry of the Interior's Department for Asylum and Migration Policy, not through the consulate. This is a separate route from the Schengen reconsideration process.

## Legal sources and limits

The page cites Visa Code Article 32(3), Act No. 326/1999 Coll. sections 169t(11)–(12), 170, 170a–170c and 172, and Act No. 500/2004 Coll. section 68(5) and Part VIII.

This is general administrative guidance. A separate MFA EU-family Schengen visa page describes a special EU-family route and a 30-day court-action period after refusal of reconsideration, but does not itself state the 15-day reconsideration filing period. Do not apply the general deadline to an EU-family case without resolving the statutory route and checking the refusal notice. The maintained Czech EU-family page is [here](cz-mzv-eu-family-visa-cs.md).
