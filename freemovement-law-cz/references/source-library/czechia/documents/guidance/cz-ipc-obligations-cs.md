## Scope

Common administrative reporting and change-notification guidance.

## Maintained summary

The page states that presence should generally be reported to the Foreign Police within 30 days of entry when planning a stay over 30 days, subject to lodging-provider reporting and an under-15 exception. It separately describes a three-working-day report for a family member when the EU citizen is not present in Czechia.

## Limits

Other reporting duties and deadlines depend on status and the change concerned. Check the current statute and the relevant authority instructions.
