## Scope

MOI filing-channel guidance for specified residence applications.

## Maintained summary

The page states that in-country applications for residence entitlements must be filed personally and lists temporary and permanent residence applications for EU-family members. It says those are generally filed at MOI asylum and migration policy offices; certain long-stay and permanent residence applications may also be filed at Czech missions.

## Limits

This does not establish local appointment availability, every exception, or the channel for every application type.
