The country pack has no candidate or capture-result files. Decree 429/2010 is accepted only as an official locator; current text/cutoff and route applicability remain pending human review. ## Scope

This pack covers Czech national legislation, official Czech guidance, forms, consular instructions, and official court/oversight discovery channels relevant to EU free movement, EU citizens and family members, entry and residence, consular process, family-status evidence, administrative procedure, access to information, data protection, and remedies. EU legislation and CJEU materials belong in the EU core. General third-country immigration, asylum, and unrelated Czech law are out of scope unless an intersection with an EU free-movement issue is recorded.

The principal legal language is Czech (`cs`). Official English administrative guidance is a separate representation and does not replace Czech legal texts. The inventory is a scoped research list, not a completeness claim.

## Retrieval rules and limits

- **Current legal text:** e-Sbírka is the official electronic Collection of Laws and International Treaties. For ordinary research, review its direct consolidated text and record the displayed version/effective date and “informative” label. The optional local-copy adapter uses its public page-data service to retrieve ordered XHTML fragments and validate identity/version metadata; the tested route required no account or key. It is separate from the documented registration-gated REST API.
- **Commands for retained legal copies:** run `python scripts/sync_esbirka_open_data.py sync` and optionally `verify` when refreshing or replaying local copies. Raw responses and temporary conversions are written under `temp/source-library/czechia/work/`; archive new or changed material through Monitor before review. The 19 October 2026 raw e-Sbírka responses remain in the standard temp tree and are indexed in Monitor's local research archive; offline replay passed for all 17 legal texts on 25 October 2026. The Node updater's former date-bound PDF route is obsolete for local statute capture.
- **Version and authority limits:** preserve e-Sbírka's informative-text caveat. Use the closest government-published edition and record its date. Check a specific promulgation only if a known discrepancy, later change, or commencement condition may materially affect the issue. A future-effective edition should be identified as such; no complete historical amendment-chain reconciliation is required for ordinary use.
- **Official guidance:** `scripts/sync-czechia.mjs --sync|--verify|--reconvert` maintains the configured official HTML guidance captures. New or changed conversions stay under the project temp tree until reviewed and archived through Monitor; accepted copies belong under `documents/`, with the source register and acceptance register updated separately. Its H1 identity, encoding, primary-region, and hash checks do not establish that parent indexes or linked attachments are complete. The Node updater still contains the legacy date-bound PDF lane; use the Python adapter for statutes.
- **Official HTML guidance and indexes:** `scripts/sync-czechia.mjs --sync|--verify|--reconvert` maintains the configured official pages. The 13 retrieved HTML responses remain in standard project temp and Monitor's local archive; offline replay passed 13/13 on 25 October 2026. Keep changed conversions in project temp until review, then update accepted files under `documents/` and both source registers. The H1 identity, encoding, primary-region, and hash checks do not establish that parent indexes or linked attachments are complete. The updater's legacy date-bound PDF lane is obsolete; use the Python adapter for statutes.
- **Forms and one-time discovery captures:** the separate 13-record Monitor batch preserves conversion history and response hashes. Six native PDF originals remain under `documents/forms/`; the seven historical HTML response bodies are not retained, so those conversions cannot be replayed from their original bytes. MOI and MFA parent indexes are rediscovery points for changing form URLs. The MFA mission directory is paginated and local practice varies. Court and oversight search pages are discovery surfaces, not individual decisions or complete feeds. Only blank forms are in scope; do not collect completed applications or personal submissions.
- **Czech-language pages and court decisions:** the register assigns each page to the official HTML lane with a publisher heading check. Four individual NSS/Regional Court decisions use `czech_official_case_document_adapter`: ordinary HTTPS through the NSS decisions host allowlist, HTML or native PDF according to the registered format, text extraction, minimum-content checks, and exact case-identifier validation. `sync-czechia.mjs --sync` stages outputs in `temp/.../work/pending/`; `--verify` and `--reconvert` replay saved response bytes. `--ack <source-id>` is available only after human review and copies the candidate into `documents/guidance/`, `documents/forms/`, or `documents/case_law/`. A decision copy remains one decision, not a complete case-law feed.
- **Decree 429/2010:** its e-Sbírka record is routed through the same public page-data/current-version adapter used for the 17 other registered texts. Run `python scripts/sync_esbirka_open_data.py sync cz-decree-429-2010`; this only creates a dated review candidate. Whole-portal review remains open for current wording, cutoff and EU-family route applicability. Do not accept it based on a successful capture or conversion.
- **Jurisdictional scope:** Czech MOI guidance describes similar procedures for Icelandic, Liechtenstein, Norwegian, and Swiss citizens. Switzerland is not an EU or EEA EFTA member. UK Withdrawal Agreement beneficiaries follow a distinct route.

## Review before acceptance

For an official reference, review the publisher, instrument identity, language, displayed date/version, relevant text, and visible caveats. For a retained local conversion, review extraction and reuse only to the extent needed for that artifact. Check enactment, commencement, or applicability records when a specific issue makes them material; do not require them as a blanket barrier to citing the government-published consolidation. Keep laws, guidance, forms, mission practice, and discovery repositories in distinct source classes.

The 17 e-Sbírka local copies are accepted as dated working texts, with the publisher’s informative-text caveat and version-effective dates retained. The 13 guidance copies, 6 form text copies with native PDFs, and 5 discovery/index snapshots are accepted only within the recorded page and date limits. Two incomplete captures are locator notes, not copied sources. Acceptance does not establish topic coverage or country completion. Decree 429/2010 is accepted only as an official locator; currentness and EU-family route applicability remain pending human review. The MFA local-consular page is limited to long-term visa and residence guidance. The country review remains incomplete.
