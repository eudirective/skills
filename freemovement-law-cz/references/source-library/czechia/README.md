The optional e-Sbírka adapter uses the public, unauthenticated page-data service. It resolves each stable act record to the publisher's current effective version, captures ordered text-fragment pages, and converts XHTML to Markdown; it does not use the separately documented registration-gated REST API. The 17 official references and dated local copies are accepted only within the version and limits recorded in the acceptance register. Recheck the stable record for later use. Preserve the publisher's “informative” label and displayed effective date; consult a particular promulgation only when a known discrepancy or commencement issue makes it material.

Both local adapters write raw responses, temporary conversions, and run state under the standard project `temp/source-library/czechia/work/` tree. The 62 raw response files from 19 October 2026 remain there and are indexed in the Monitor local research archive. Offline replay passed for all 17 e-Sbírka legal texts and all 13 official HTML pages on 25 October 2026. Their retrieval manifests are indexed in Monitor under `captures/czechia-candidate-promotion-2026-10-25/Czechia/czechia/e-sbirka/retrieval-manifest.json` and `captures/czechia-candidate-promotion-2026-10-25/Czechia/czechia/html-guidance/retrieval-manifest.json`. Changed conversions stay in project temp until reviewed and archived through Monitor; the pack has no `candidates/` directory.

The separate 13-record Monitor capture batch was dispositioned individually: six native form PDFs and five dated page captures were retained under `documents/`; two incomplete NSS and UOOU captures became official-link locator notes. The original raw HTML bodies for the seven page captures are not retained, so those historical HTML conversions cannot be replayed from their original response bytes. Four individual Czech court decisions now have maintained case summaries; they remain examples, not a repeatable court search. Other link-only references have no local copies. Coverage, dependencies, continuing discovery, and country review remain partial.

The retrieval register routes 13 identified Czech-language MOI and gov.cz guidance pages through the official HTML adapter, and four individual court decisions through a case-number-validated HTML/PDF converter. `scripts/sync-czechia.mjs --sync` stages outputs as review candidates under the project temp tree; `--verify` and `--reconvert` replay saved bytes, and `--ack <source-id>` copies a manually reviewed guidance or decision candidate under `documents/`. Decree 429/2010 remains locator-only pending confirmation of current consolidated text and applicability. It is routed through `scripts/sync_esbirka_open_data.py sync cz-decree-429-2010`; any extracted copy must retain the unresolved scope note and whole-portal human-review gate before acceptance. These routes are implemented as acquisition methods, not evidence of retrieval, acceptance, or topic coverage.

The inventory covers Czech national law and official administrative, consular, court, and oversight materials relevant to EU free movement and family members. It is not an exhaustive account of Czech law. Official Czech guidance also describes similar free-movement rights for Icelandic, Liechtenstein, Norwegian, and Swiss citizens; Switzerland is not an EU or EEA EFTA member, and UK Withdrawal Agreement cases remain separate.
