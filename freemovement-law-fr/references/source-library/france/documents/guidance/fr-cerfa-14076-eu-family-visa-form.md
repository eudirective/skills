## Purpose and family-member instructions

The form is the harmonised, free Schengen visa application form. It includes a field for the EU, EEA, or Swiss family member, and also covers UK nationals who benefit from the EU–UK Withdrawal Agreement. Those family members are instructed to leave fields 21, 22, 30, 31, and 32 blank. The names in fields 1–3 must match the travel document. Service Public lists the French embassy or consulate abroad as the recipient.

This field map records the PDF's structure. Use the official PDF and current France-Visas / consular instructions to prepare an application.

## Applicant and family details

1. Current family name.
2. Family name at birth and previous family names.
3. Given names.
4. Date of birth.
5. Place of birth.
6. Country of birth.
7. Current nationality, nationality at birth if different, and any other nationalities.
8. Sex.
9. Civil status.
10. For a minor, details and contact information for the holder of parental authority or legal guardian.
11. National identity number, where applicable.
12. Type of travel document.
13. Travel-document number.
14. Date the travel document was issued.
15. Travel-document expiry date.
16. Country that issued the travel document.
17. Identity and travel-document details for the relevant EU, EEA, Swiss, or qualifying UK family member.
18. Relationship to that family member, with categories including spouse, child, grandchild, dependent ascendant, registered partner, or another relationship.
19. Home address, email address, and telephone number.
20. Whether the applicant resides in a country other than their current nationality, including residence-permit details where applicable.

## Trip, host, and funding details

21. Current occupation (*).
22. Employer details, or educational institution details for a student (*).
23. Purpose of travel, with choices such as tourism, business, visiting family or friends, culture, sport, official visit, medical reasons, studies, airport transit, or another reason.
24. Further information about the purpose.
25. Main destination Member State and any other destination Member States.
26. Member State of first entry.
27. Number of entries requested and intended arrival/departure dates for the first trip.
28. Whether fingerprints were previously taken for a Schengen visa application, with the date and visa number if known.
29. Entry authorization for the final destination, if required.
30. Inviting person(s), hotel, or temporary accommodation (*).
31. Host company or organization, contact details, and accommodation contact information (*).
32. Who will pay travel and living costs, with the listed means of support (*).
33. Identity and contact details of a person completing the form for the applicant, if applicable.

(*) Family members covered by the form's EU/EEA/Swiss and UK Withdrawal Agreement instruction do not complete these fields.

## Administration-only area and declaration

The first page also reserves an area for the administration to record the filing date and number, filing channel, responsible officer, supporting-document checks, decision, visa category, validity dates, number of entries, and authorized stay duration.

The final pages contain the applicant's signature and date fields and a declaration. In substance, the applicant acknowledges the collection and processing of the form data, photograph, and (where applicable) fingerprints for visa examination and the Visa Information System; describes the stated access, correction, and deletion rights; confirms the information is accurate; undertakes to leave before the visa expires; and acknowledges that a visa alone does not guarantee admission. The form also states that adequate travel medical insurance is needed for multiple-entry visas and that the visa fee is not refunded after refusal.
