**Scope:** France-Visas routes for spouses, children, ascendants, and foreign parents of French minor children.

## What the page covers

The page separates four family situations and distinguishes short stays of up to 90 days from stays beyond 90 days or settlement:

- **Spouse of a French citizen:** For a short stay, the page directs the applicant to a short-stay visa and asks for proof of marriage and the spouse's French nationality. For a stay beyond 90 days or settlement, it lists proof of marriage, French nationality, shared life, and intention to continue living together in France. It describes a long-stay visa equivalent to a residence permit, with validation formalities after arrival.
- **Child of a French citizen:** The page lists foreign children under 21, dependent children older than 21 in specified circumstances, children whose parentage with a French citizen is legally established, and children adopted by a French citizen. For a long stay or settlement, it lists evidence of parentage, both parents' nationalities, age, and the French parent's residence or intended residence in France. It also identifies consent from the other holder of parental authority for a minor where parental authority is joint. The page describes a three-month long-stay visa followed by an application for a residence permit at the prefecture.
- **Ascendant of a French citizen or the French citizen's foreign spouse:** For a short stay, it lists proof of family relationship and nationality, sufficient personal resources, accommodation, and medical insurance. For settlement, it lists the family relationship and nationality, dependency on the descendant or spouse in the country of residence, accommodation in France, and medical insurance for the first three months. The page describes a three-month long-stay visa followed by an application for an “ascendant à charge” residence permit. For a stay beyond 90 days without settlement where the person's own resources suffice, it points to the tourist/private-stay route.
- **Foreign parent of a French minor:** The page concerns a parent who contributes to the child's maintenance and education under Civil Code Article 371-2, from birth or for at least two years. For settlement it lists evidence of parentage, the child's French nationality, age and residence in France, effective contribution to maintenance and education, and that the parent is not living in a state of polygamy. It describes a three-month long-stay visa followed by an application for a residence permit at the prefecture.

For supporting-document details, the page sends applicants to the France-Visas online visa assistant. It also links to the online visa-application service.

## Route distinction

This is the family-of-French-citizen route. It must not be merged with the France-Visas route for family members of EU, EEA, or Swiss citizens moving under a free-movement route.
