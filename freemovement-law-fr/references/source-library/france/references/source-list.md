# France source list (reviewed 2026-10-29)

**Scope:** bounded discovery of official French sources related to EU/EEA/Swiss free movement, immigration, family and civil status, visa/residence practice, administrative procedure and remedies, courts, access to information, privacy, oversight, and legal aid. France is an EU Member State; EU, EEA EFTA, and Swiss legal routes remain distinct.

**Inventory:** 57 source records; 21 accepted local documents (8 Service Public/DILA, 4 DILA LEGI statutory texts, 1 CNIL, 1 Conseil d’État, and 7 project-authored maintained summaries); 36 usable official references by link; the 2018 DTD's applicability to current LEGI data remains open for human review; 1 N-Lex item is discovery-only/already represented. All 18 candidate captures remain in the Monitor's local-only archive; 21 were promoted into documents/ (14 retained source texts and 7 clearly labeled maintained summaries), 4 remain link-only as search/catalogue interfaces, and 1 remains discovery-only. These counts distinguish local copies, external references, currentness and topic coverage.
