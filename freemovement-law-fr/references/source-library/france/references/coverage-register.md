## Counting and source rules

This register maps the six approved core pathways. `P01`–`P06` topic and layer states in [coverage-model.json](coverage-model.json) are authoritative for machine-readable counts. The local paths below identify actual retained text or clearly labeled project summaries. Candidate IDs and official-reference records identify discovery/provenance records; a URL or locator is not source text. Files proposed in draft PR #168 are marked and do not exist on `main` yet.

A layer is `partial` when some relevant material is recorded but the route-level scope or evidence remains incomplete; it is `unresolved` when no adequate layer evidence has been established. `adequately_covered_topic_count` remains zero.

## Completion gate

No pathway becomes adequate until its national and shared-source provisions, procedure, authority, and remedy are checked for the relevant route and stage. A local text copy, official page, source-list row, or this register alone does not close coverage. Keep EU, EEA EFTA, Swiss, EU-family, ordinary Schengen, and other national routes distinct. Recheck dynamic service pages before date-sensitive use.
