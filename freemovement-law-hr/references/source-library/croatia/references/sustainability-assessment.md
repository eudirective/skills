# Croatia source sustainability review

**As of 25 October 2026**

## Result

Croatia has 82 source records, 43 accepted local documents and 41 official references usable by link within their recorded scopes. The pack remains incomplete: all 14 topic gates are open, none of the 40 planned court searches is recorded as run, and mission/local-authority practice remains unreviewed.

The Monitor local-only archive now holds Croatia's candidate/discovery notes, raw responses, selector-bound legal-text fragments and acquisition manifests. Their hashes were reconciled with the working files before pack copies were removed. The accepted documents remain under `documents/`; the pack has no candidate/capture directories or parallel acquisition manifests. Four current MUP forms were visually reviewed page by page and retained unchanged with a narrow Article 18(3) basis. A missing revision date remains a limit.

## Sustainable elements

- **Official source references:** Twenty-nine link reviews record publisher, identity, date/cutoff, relevant content and limitations. The source-review process does not require copying a page to use a human-reviewed official link.
- **Local documents:** Twenty-five legal-text representations, eight MUP/MVEP PDFs and ten guidance article texts have separate acceptance records, including nine AZOP, MVEP, Ombudsman and Commissioner article bodies or scoped excerpts. Seven are retained for their reviewed source scopes; the legacy MVEP FAQ and 2019 Commissioner excerpt are historical-only and excluded from current use. The mixed AZOP SIS/VIS page is retained only for its SIS rights subsection; its other sections remain excluded. Legal-text replay evidence, source fragments and raw responses live in the Monitor archive; the accepted documents live in the pack. The four EEA form PDFs have empty text layers and were visually reviewed. The four SIS/VIS PDFs have searchable text and have text/page-count/property checks; their page-image layout has not been independently inspected.
- **Version review:** The current MUP Propisi index supports the listed EEA and third-country amendment chains. It is not treated as a consolidated text, and the form index's omission of NN 155/22 is recorded separately.
- **Registers:** Source acceptance, topic coverage, legal dependencies, source rules, attachment review, currentness, case-law discovery and operational discovery remain separate records. Structural validation does not promote a source into topic coverage or country completion.

## Sustainability blockers

1. **No Croatia pack action:** `list_source_packs` does not register Croatia. Archive migration and the accepted form promotion used the Monitor's generic local archive tools and a manual Croatia workflow. No automated end-to-end Croatia action ran.
2. **Archive replay:** The Monitor archive is the canonical store for captures, but the repository's Python utilities cannot read that archive. The old replay, capture-manifest and candidate-file utilities have been retired with the country-pack copies. The acceptance register retains earlier ordered-visible-text evidence for 25 legal texts, but a fresh pack-script replay is unsupported.
3. **Source discovery:** Existing retrieval rules describe sources but do not implement a repeatable monitor workflow for all 82 source IDs. Twenty-six records are located but uncaptured; web guidance and mission/local practice need scoped maintenance.
4. **Attachments and post-transition formats:** Six official legal texts contain 160 inventoried images. The relevant specimens and image groups remain visually/currentness-open, including the Form 4b/11b residence-card transition question after 3 August 2026.
5. **Topic and discovery gates:** The 42-row Directive map remains preliminary, all 14 topics remain incomplete, none of 40 court searches is recorded, and the operational evidence plan lacks mission/local-authority results.
6. **Reuse boundary:** Article 18(3) is applied only to individually identified published official legal texts and the unchanged MVEP VIS administrative form. MUP Terms of Use support attributed retention of seven identified MUP forms and the body of hr-mup-eea-guidance; the exact page and scope are recorded in source acceptance. No MUP page shell, third-party material, other unreviewed page content or database-wide extraction is retained.

## Maintenance rule

For ordinary research, use a human-reviewed official link within its recorded scope and follow the closest government-published text with its date/cutoff. For retained copies, keep the exact source identity, version evidence, format-appropriate content and attachment checks, hash and reuse basis. Archive candidate, discovery and capture material only in the Monitor's indexed local-only store. Do not mark a topic or the country complete until its evidence-backed layers, dependencies, continuing discovery and operational evidence are resolved.
