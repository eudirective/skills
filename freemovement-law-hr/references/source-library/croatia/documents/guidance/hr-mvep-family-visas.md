## Source text (English)

FAMILY MEMBERS OF EU/EEA CITIZENS

Family members of citizens of the European Union or of the European Economic Area who are third-country nationals shall be entitled to free movement in accordance with Directive 2004/38 / EC of 29 April 2004.

A family member of an EU/EEA Member State may travel to Croatia without a visa if:
- has a valid passport and a valid identity card entitled “Residence card for a family member of a Union citizen”, and
- accompanies or joins a family member who is a citizen of an EU/EEA Member State.

The text of Directive 2004/38 / EC can be viewed here: *(obsolete source hyperlink omitted)*

Family members of EU/EEA Member States are given priority to apply for a visa directly at the Embassy. Their applications are processed expeditiously and without a visa fee, with the presentation of minimum documentation: visa application form, valid passport, photograph and proof of family relationship with the EU/EEA citizen.
