## Source identity and scope

The live English page is titled “Registration of temporary residence for EEA nationals.” It is served on the Government of Croatia's gov.hr portal and covers EEA/Swiss nationals, family members, temporary and permanent residence, application evidence, and links to Ministry of the Interior (MUP) forms. The portal footer identifies the Government of the Republic of Croatia. The page was available and readable during this review.

This file is an independently written source locator and factual summary. It does not reproduce the page's prose. gov.hr's Terms and Conditions page was checked; it did not provide an express content-reuse licence. Page wording and the wider website shell are therefore not copied here.

## What the page covers

As presented on the page checked on 29 October 2026, it:

- distinguishes stays up to three months from residence registration for longer stays;
- describes registration of temporary residence for employment, self-employment, study/training, other purposes, family reunification, and life partnership;
- lists police administrations/stations as the competent filing points and gives an eight-day period after the initial three-month stay for the described registration or residence-card application;
- outlines identity, relationship, resources, insurance, and other supporting evidence for different routes;
- describes permanent residence and points to MUP application forms; and
- separately discusses family members who are EEA nationals and those who are not.

These are descriptions of the page's contents, not independent legal conclusions or advice. The English page contains awkward and inconsistent wording in places, and it does not display a revision date.
