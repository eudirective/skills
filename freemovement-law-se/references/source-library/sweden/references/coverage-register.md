# Sweden issue-led coverage register

**Reviewed:** 23 October 2026
**State:** Seventeen official external references accepted within dated scopes; no local copies, topics, or country review accepted. Four Swedish marriage/adoption statutes have narrow external-reference decisions; the three regular year:number SFS items are in the updater manifest. Lag 1904:26 s.1 remains external-only because the updater’s current SFS parser expects a simple numeric year:number identifier. This is a pack assessment, not a legal-sufficiency certification.

Select the version by the marriage/proceeding date and cross-check relevant EU, Nordic, and treaty rules. The links do not decide an individual recognition question.
