## Confirmed scoped statements

Family members of qualifying EU/EEA citizens are exempt from the visa fee; a separately published VFS service fee of EUR 14 applies to the VFS channel. A service fee cannot automatically be classified as an unlawful visa fee. Publication verified 2026-10-10; currentness must be rechecked.
