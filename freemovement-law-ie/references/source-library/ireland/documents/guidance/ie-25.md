# Ireland visa and non-visa required nationalities

## Scope

This ISD page explains the main basis for deciding whether a person needs an Irish visa and lists specified exemptions. It is a mutable service page, not a substitute for the current official visa-required nationality schedule, legislation, or an individual route assessment.

## Summary

The page says an Irish visa is generally required according to the traveller's nationality, or, for a refugee, the issuing country of the travel document, regardless of residence or the nationality of a family member. It lists exemptions for holders of a valid Irish Residence Permit, an Irish-issued travel document, and an Article 10 residence card issued under Directive 2004/38/EC. It also mentions a limited temporary suspension for certain minors usually resident in Ireland.

The page states that Ireland temporarily suspended operation of the European Agreement on the Abolition of Visas for Refugees for 12 months from 19 July, so holders of 1951 Convention travel documents generally require an Irish entry visa, subject to listed exemptions. It separately addresses 1954 Convention travel documents.

## Currency and use limits

The page says it was last updated on 14 March 2025, despite containing a temporary 12-month notice measured from 19 July. Its notice and linked nationality information must therefore be checked for current status before use.
