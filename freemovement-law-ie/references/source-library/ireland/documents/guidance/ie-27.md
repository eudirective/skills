# Ireland: 2026 removal of appeals for certain short-stay visa refusals

- **Source ID:** ie-27
- **Official publisher:** Department of Justice, Home Affairs and Migration (gov.ie); operational guidance by Immigration Service Delivery (ISD)
- **Official announcement:** https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-justice-home-affairs-and-migration/news/removal-of-appeals-for-certain-short-stay-visa-refusals/
- **Current ISD instructions:** https://www.irishimmigration.ie/appeal-a-negative-decision/
- **Language:** English
- **Checked:** 29 October 2026
- **Record type:** Maintained project-authored summary; announcement access limitation recorded

## Scope and confirmation

The Department's announcement URL was identified as the official source, but direct retrieval returned HTTP 403 during this review. The operational rule was independently confirmed on the live ISD appeal page: from 1 June 2026, some short-stay refusal appeals are removed, while specified short-stay applications under Directive 2004/38/EC remain appealable. ISD's page lists spouse, de facto partner, and other family-member categories for an EU/EEA/Swiss citizen.

## Summary

The 2026 change concerns appeal availability after refusal of certain short-stay visa applications. It is not a blanket removal of every short-stay appeal: ISD expressly preserves the listed EU Free Movement Directive categories. The applicant's refusal letter states whether an appeal is available. Where ISD permits an appeal, its current instructions set a two-month deadline, no fee, and postal submission requirements.
