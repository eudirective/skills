# Ireland High Court Practice Direction HC140: Asylum, Immigration and Citizenship List

**Source ID:** ie-48  
**Publisher:** Courts Service / High Court  
**Official direction:** <https://www.courts.ie/practice-directions/full-practice-direction?url=asylum--immigration-and-citizenship-list-2026>  
**Checked:** 29 October 2026  
**Language:** English  
**Effective:** 3 June 2026

## Scope and status

HC140 applies to specified High Court proceedings concerning asylum, subsidiary protection, immigration, freedom of movement, naturalisation, citizenship and related EU-law matters, including proceedings under section 5 of the Illegal Immigrants (Trafficking) Act 2000. It applies to proceedings started before or after its commencement and revoked Practice Direction HC81.

The direction sets specialized list sittings and requirements for parties, pleadings, affidavits, translations, exhibits, leave applications, service, electronic communication, case progression, written submissions, hearings, appeals and non-compliance. It emphasizes continuing duties of candour, enquiry, disclosure and diligence. It requires precise grounds and supporting facts, an affidavit setting out material facts, appropriate translations, and the relevant decision and record. Leave-stage written submissions are generally due seven days before the hearing; where leave is granted, the direction states standard service steps and a seven-day period, subject to court orders.

## Maintenance and limits

The live Courts Service page was opened on 29 October 2026 and confirms HC140 and its 3 June 2026 effective date. This is a procedural direction for the named High Court list, not the substantive basis for an immigration decision or an exhaustive remedies map. Read the full current direction, applicable Superior Court Rules, legislation and any case-specific court order before filing. This summary omits many detailed requirements and exceptions.

Recheck the official direction page and commencement/revocation information at each review.
