## Scope

The page explains how applicants enter information in AVATS for an Irish visa or preclearance application and generate the application form. It is procedural guidance; visa requirements and supporting documents depend on the chosen route and relevant visa office.

## Summary

ISD advises applicants to read the route-specific requirements and prepare supporting documents before entering details online. The system is in English and asks applicants to select the passport nationality (or “Stateless” for a 1951 Convention travel document); that answer affects the available application categories. The applicant then selects the travel reason and, where applicable, short-stay C or long-stay D. ISD defines C as a proposed stay of 90 days or less and D as more than 90 days, but states that Free Movement Directive visa applications use C even where the intended stay exceeds 90 days. All preclearance applications are D.

The guide describes the information gathered across the form, review and declaration steps, and producing and submitting the application form with supporting documents. For applicants under 18, a parent or legal guardian completes the details and signs the generated form. ISD warns that false, fraudulent or misleading information or documents may result in refusal, loss of an appeal, and a bar of up to five years on another application.

## Use limits

AVATS screens and ISD instructions may change. Mission-specific document requirements can apply. The visa-required nationality schedule, route-specific policy and the applicant's circumstances determine which category and supporting evidence are appropriate.
