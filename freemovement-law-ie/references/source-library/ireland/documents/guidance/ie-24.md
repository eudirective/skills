# Ireland long-stay join-family visa for an Irish-national sponsor

**Source ID:** ie-24  
**Publisher:** Immigration Service Delivery (ISD), Department of Justice, Home Affairs and Migration  
**Official page:** <https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-join-family-in-ireland/joining-an-irish-national/join-family-visa/>  
**Checked:** 29 October 2026  
**Language:** English

## Route summary

This page concerns a person seeking to reside in Ireland for more than three months with an Irish-national family member. Visa-required nationals need a long-stay join-family visa before travelling. The page says a non-visa-required national does not need a visa or preclearance to travel with an Irish spouse, but must present the purpose of travel to an immigration officer at entry.

Applications are submitted online through AVATS, followed by the signed and dated application form and supporting documents at the location specified in the generated form. The page links to a separate Non-EEA Family Reunification Policy, which governs sponsor eligibility, eligible family members, any waiting period, financial thresholds, permitted activity and dependency. It expressly excludes Directive 2004/38 cases and several other distinct schemes from that policy.

The live page describes a temporary processing-model transition. As of this review, it says a specialist Join Family Case Processing Function is being established, with a limited dedicated stream for some Critical Skills Employment Permit-linked cases while other cases continue in the ordinary chronological stream. It cautions that decisions may not issue in a single overall date order.

## Limits and maintenance

The live ISD page was opened on 29 October 2026. The linked policy, financial thresholds, document list, fees, visa decisions and local submission instructions may change separately. This summary is route-specific guidance, not a determination of sponsor eligibility or approval. It must not be applied to EU free-movement, UK, refugee/protection, or other family-reunification routes.

Recheck the page’s policy, processing notice, application form, fees and supporting-document links at each review.
