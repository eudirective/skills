# Ireland family-reunification route selector

**Checked:** 29 October 2026  
**Language:** English

## Purpose and route boundaries

The page is an official route-selection hub for non-EU/EEA and non-Swiss nationals seeking to join family in Ireland. It separates routes according to the sponsor or applicant category, including Irish citizens, UK nationals, EEA/Swiss nationals, other non-EEA/Swiss residents, international-protection beneficiaries and the Afghan Admission Programme.

The hub directs people joining an Irish citizen for more than three months to the long-stay join-family visa route. It directs family members of an EU citizen exercising free-movement rights to the separate residence-card / EU Treaty Rights route. UK nationals, ordinary non-EEA sponsors, and international-protection beneficiaries have their own distinct procedures.

A prominent update states that, from 12 June 2026, certain refugees and beneficiaries of subsidiary protection are no longer eligible under the former Non-EEA Family Reunification Policy and must use the procedure under section 205 of the International Protection Act 2026, subject to the page’s stated exception and waiting period for relationships formed after entry.

## Limits and maintenance

The live page was opened on 29 October 2026; it states that it was last updated 12 June 2026. It is a navigation hub, not the substantive eligibility policy or complete procedure. Follow the selected route’s current rules and forms, especially the new international-protection procedure and any transition instructions.

Recheck the hub’s update date and each linked route at every review. Do not use this page to infer that an unlisted family relationship qualifies.
