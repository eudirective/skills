# Ireland: appeal a negative visa decision

- **Source ID:** ie-26
- **Official publisher:** Immigration Service Delivery (ISD), Department of Justice, Home Affairs and Migration
- **Official source:** https://www.irishimmigration.ie/appeal-a-negative-decision/
- **Language:** English
- **Checked:** 29 October 2026
- **Record type:** Maintained project-authored summary of live official guidance

## Scope

This page describes ISD's administrative process for appealing visa refusals. The refusal letter controls whether an appeal is available in the individual case. This summary is not a statement of judicial-review rules or every remedy in law.

## Summary

ISD says that, from 1 June 2026, appeals are no longer permitted for some short-stay visa refusals. It expressly retains appeals for short-stay applications under Directive 2004/38/EC, including the listed de facto partner, other family member, and spouse categories for an EU/EEA/Swiss citizen. Applicants should use the refusal letter to confirm appeal eligibility.

For appealable refusals, ISD states there is no fee and the appeal must arrive within two months of the refusal letter date. The applicant must explain why the decision should change and address the refusal reasons. ISD specifies original-document rules, a postal submission to the address in the refusal letter, and no fax or email submission. The page sets out additional requirements where an authorised person appeals for another applicant.

ISD warns that false or misleading information may remove appeal rights and lead to a bar of up to five years on further applications.

## Use limits

Confirm the current page and refusal letter before acting. Do not infer an appeal right from this summary; category definitions and exceptions must be checked against the live ISD instructions and applicable law.
