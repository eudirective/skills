## Scope described by the landing page

Migri says citizens of EU Member States, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland do not need a Finnish residence permit. It says a person intending to stay in Finland for longer than three months must register the right of residence.

The page routes users to three application-finder steps:

- “I am an EU citizen”
- “I am a family member of an EU citizen”
- “I want a permanent right of residence”

The application finder directs users to a page with the requirements and supporting-document list for the selected application. It then links to Enter Finland for completing the application. Migri says answers in the finder are not recorded or saved.
