- Main official guidance route: https://dvv.fi/en/foreigner-registration
- Related official route: https://dvv.fi/en/international-moving
- Publisher: Digital and Population Data Services Agency (DVV), Finland
- Language: English (the guidance route is also part of DVV's multilingual service)
- Checked: 2026-10-29

## What this record supports

DVV's foreigner-registration guidance is the official route for registering a foreigner's personal data in Finland's Population Information System, including obtaining a Finnish personal identity code when one has not already been issued. This is a population-register service. It is distinct from the Finnish Immigration Service's decision on a residence permit or registration of an EU citizen's right of residence.

DVV's linked English identity-code and personal-data form is for foreign citizens who do not yet have a Finnish personal identity code. The current form requests identity and travel-document details, intended duration of stay, and Finnish address information. It distinguishes a short stay, a stay of at least a year with or without a municipality of residence, and a request for an identity code without intending to live in Finland.

The linked general municipality-of-residence form is for applicants who already have a Finnish personal identity code and intend to stay for more than a year. It includes an “EU registration” basis for a registered EU citizen or a family member with a residence card. The form says that after online submission most applicants must attend a DVV service location within one month; it states an exception for those whose municipality was granted on the basis of EU-right registration or a family-member residence card when that decision was received less than 12 months earlier. If the applicant lacks a Finnish personal identity code, the form says it cannot be used and directs them to a DVV service location.

## Source-access and scope limits

The English DVV guidance pages returned HTTP 403 in the research viewer. A browser retrieval attempt also could not be completed because the Playwright CLI package could not be fetched in this environment. The linked official DVV forms were available and reviewed, so this note records the operational steps and distinctions shown by those forms; it does not summarize unseen page content.

The forms are interactive services. This note covers the rendered instructions and field purposes that were exposed, not client-side behavior, eligibility adjudication or every supporting-document rule. The general municipality form refers users to the international-moving page for temporary-address instructions, but those instructions were not independently reviewed. Check DVV's current guidance and appointment route before acting.
