# DVV online form: application for a municipality of residence in Finland

- Source ID: `fi-dvv-municipality-form`
- Publisher: Digital and Population Data Services Agency (DVV)
- Form title: Application for a municipality of residence in Finland
- Language reviewed: English rendition; the form also announces Finnish and Swedish versions
- Official form: https://lomakkeet.dvv.fi/lomakkeet/6889/lomake.html?rinnakkaislomake=en
- Checked: 29 October 2026
- Form technology: Eduix E-Lomake dynamic web form
- Record type: Maintained transcription of the complete rendered form's instructions and field schema. No application was completed or submitted and no personal data was collected.
- Scope note: This is an address/municipality registration form, not a residence-permit application.

## Eligibility and who may complete the form

The DVV says to use this form only when the applicant has a Finnish personal identity code and intends to stay in Finland for more than one year. A person who does not yet have a Finnish identity code is directed to the DVV foreigner-registration instructions and an in-person service location.

The form may cover the applicant and family members living at the same address when municipality registration is also requested for them. It may also be completed for another person using a power of attorney. The page says no power of attorney is required when applying for one's child under 18 or spouse, or when a district court has appointed the representative for an underage child.

## Follow-up visit

After electronic submission, the applicant must personally visit a DVV service location within one month. The page states that this visit is not required where the applicant has been granted a municipality of residence on the basis of EU-citizen registration or an EU-family-member residence card, and received that decision less than 12 months earlier.

Otherwise, the applicant must book an appointment. For students, a personal appointment is needed only if the educational institution does not arrange a joint visit; the form directs students to check with their institution or tutor.

## Rendered form field schema

### Background information

The applicant selects whether the request is:

- for the applicant alone;
- for the applicant and family members at the same address; or
- made on behalf of another person, where the person completing it is not registering their own address.

### Person completing the form

If completing the form for someone else, enter the completing person's name and relationship to the applicant. The form gives guardians and court-appointed representatives of minors as examples. Leave these fields blank when applying for oneself.

### Main applicant

The form requests:

- all given names as shown in the passport;
- surname;
- Finnish personal identity code (11 characters);
- Finnish Immigration Service customer number (seven digits, found on a residence-permit decision or in Enter Finland);
- confirmation of intent to stay for more than a year and request for a permanent address and municipality;
- basis for the municipality request;
- country of residence before arrival in Finland; and
- where the applicant moved from Estonia or another Nordic country, the identity code in that country.

The form's listed bases include residence permit type A, P or B; EU registration (registration of an EU citizen's right of residence or an EU family member's residence card); Nordic citizenship; and family relationship to a person who already has a Finnish municipality of residence when the applicant has neither a residence permit nor EU registration.

### Family members at the same address

For each family member added, the form asks whether they intend to stay more than a year and seek a permanent address and municipality, their basis for the request, their relationship to the first person on the form, all given names, surname, Finnish personal identity code, Finnish Immigration Service customer number, prior country of residence, and (for moves from Estonia or another Nordic country) the identity code in that country.

The reviewed page rendered this family-member block repeatedly and displayed an “add” control. The repeated blocks describe the same field schema; the page does not establish a legal maximum number of family members. The form can be used for family members only when they live at the same address as the main applicant.

### Ineligibility notice

The form warns that it cannot be used where the person does not meet the conditions for a municipality of residence. It says a person who lacks a residence permit based on temporary protection, or who has not yet lived in Finland for at least a year, cannot use the form. For a temporary address, it directs the user to DVV's international-moving page instead.

The form includes an acknowledgement control for a person who understands that they cannot apply using this form but still wants to continue filling it in.

### Finnish address and effective date

The form asks for postal code, city, and full street address, including doorway letter and apartment number.

An optional date field allows a request to register the permanent address retroactively. If no date is entered, the submission date is used. A requested date cannot precede the date the eligibility conditions were met and cannot be more than 30 days before submission. If an earlier date is supplied, the submission date is used instead.

### Contact and submission

The applicant provides an email address and selects whether a possible decision may be sent by email. A phone number is requested for contact during processing; the form states that the phone number is not saved in the Finnish Population Information System.

After submission, the application is queued for processing and DVV will contact the applicant if more information is needed. The page links to the DVV privacy and accessibility statements.
