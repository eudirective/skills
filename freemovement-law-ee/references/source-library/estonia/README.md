## Accepted legal texts

Thirty-three saved legal-text acquisitions produced 32 distinct accepted documents under [`documents/legislation/`](documents/legislation/) after one duplicate Aliens Act acquisition was consolidated. Fourteen are Estonian editions within publisher validity on 25 October 2026 (13 current Act consolidations and the EU-citizen residence-procedure regulation); one English Administrative Procedure Act edition is within its publisher validity through 31 December 2026. The remaining 17 are version-specific historical, future-effective, or expired editions. English texts are supporting translations; consult the Estonian text for controlling wording.

Current Estonian editions were selected from the official date-filtered search for 19 October 2026 and checked against each publisher record's title, ID, text type, publication status, and validity metadata. Use each only within its recorded scope and check Riigi Teataja again for later dates. The Citizen Act edition `103062026006` does not take effect until 31 October 2029. The residence-data forms regulation edition `108012019012` is historical. The EU-citizen residence-procedure regulation `125042025013` is published and has a validity period beginning 1 January 2026.

The local texts replay from the exact saved publisher responses. Acceptance of a text does not establish topic coverage, legal effect after its recorded period, or country completion.

## External references and discovery routes

Eighteen official operational or court pages are accepted as external-only references. Together with three statutory link decisions that overlap accepted local texts, the source acceptance register records 21 external-reference decisions. Their scope, access limits, and freshness notes are recorded there. They are not accepted local legal texts; check the live publisher before relying on procedures, fees, forms, mission details, or court-search results. Human review remains open before operative use of two references: the Ministry page has a terminology conflict with the statute, and the Tallinn service page returned HTTP 403 in the research viewer, leaving its content and parent index unverified. Both remain link-only. The MFA application portal is a route reference only; do not automate submissions or collect applicant data.

Three routes remain discovery-only: the Riigi Teataja portal, its FAQ, and N-Lex. They are not counted as accepted source references or topic coverage. The official Riigi Teataja [FAQ](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/kkk) documents `?leiaKehtiv` for a link that resolves to the version in force when opened.
