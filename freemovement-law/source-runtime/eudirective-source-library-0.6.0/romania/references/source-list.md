# Romania — national legal sources for free movement, visas and administrative remedies

**Research date:** 10 October 2026; case-law follow-up added 11 October 2026. **Scope:** Romanian legislation, judgments of Romanian courts, Romanian authorities' official guidance, and explicitly labeled secondary case-law reproductions/digests from the follow-up. This is a **list of sources**, not a pack of law texts. No EU legislation, CJEU judgments or other supranational source documents are included. Official Romanian legal texts are generally in Romanian.

**Version note:** Official source links are preferred. The 11 October case-law follow-up also includes three clearly marked secondary items where the official full judgment was not retrieved: two third-party reproductions and one court-authored digest. These are not represented as official texts. The *Portal Legislativ* may provide a consolidated form and historical versions; the currency of each individual consolidation as at 10 October 2026 has **not** been independently verified. Judicial entries distinguish decisions resolving an issue from inadmissible referrals. Lower-court case-law coverage is not exhaustive.

## 1. Constitutional rights, immigration, EU-family entry and borders

- **Constituția României (2003 republication)** — constitutional guarantees of equality, petitions, information, family life and access to justice. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/47355).
- **OUG nr. 102/2005** — entry, residence and permanent residence of EU/EEA/Swiss citizens and their family members; family visas and refusal safeguards. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/208825).
- **HG nr. 1.864/2006 (republicată)** — implementing measure for OUG 102/2005 and residence-document forms. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/136986).
- **Normele metodologice de aplicare a OUG nr. 102/2005 (anexă la HG 1.864/2006, republicate)** — particularly Article 2 on family-member visa documents and communication of refusal/review rights. [Republished rules](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/136987). **Use the republished rules, not the superseded 2006 wording.**
- **OUG nr. 194/2002 privind regimul străinilor în România** — general entry, visa, residence, removal and remedies regime; distinguish its scope from the special EU-family legislation. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/50236).
- **Legea nr. 248/2005** — Romanian citizens' travel abroad, travel documents, exit and return rights. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/228250).
- **OUG nr. 105/2001 privind frontiera de stat a României** — border-crossing and frontier rules. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/292545).
- **OUG nr. 104/2001 privind organizarea și funcționarea Poliției de Frontieră Române** — Border Police organisation and authority. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/303040).
- **Legea nr. 287/2009 privind Codul civil (republicată)** — marriage, civil status and the domestic family-law provisions relevant to residence documentation, including Article 277. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/130142).

## 3. Administrative law, petitions, litigation and judicial remedies

- **Legea nr. 554/2004 a contenciosului administrativ** — review of administrative acts, unlawful refusals and failure to respond, preliminary complaints, remedies and enforcement. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/298762).
- **OG nr. 27/2002 privind reglementarea activității de soluționare a petițiilor** — registration, handling and response to petitions. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/33817).
- **OUG nr. 57/2019 privind Codul administrativ** — public-administration organisation, officials, duties and administrative acts; **not** a universal procedural code equivalent to all administrative-procedure statutes elsewhere. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/305877).
- **OG nr. 2/2001 privind regimul juridic al contravențiilor** — administrative contraventions and challenges to sanctions. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/307794).
- **Legea nr. 134/2010 privind Codul de procedură civilă** — civil procedure and rules relevant to administrative judicial proceedings. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/120644).
- **OUG nr. 80/2013 privind taxele judiciare de timbru** — litigation filing fees and exemptions, subject to subsequent amendments. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/149314).
- **Legea nr. 304/2022 privind organizarea judiciară** — domestic court organisation and jurisdictional framework. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/288614).
- **Legea nr. 47/1992 privind organizarea și funcționarea Curții Constituționale** — constitutional review procedure. [Republication](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/53577).
- **Legea nr. 24/2000 privind normele de tehnică legislativă pentru elaborarea actelor normative** — legality and drafting requirements for regulations and other normative acts. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/106782).

- **Legea nr. 52/2003 privind transparența decizională în administrația publică** — publication of draft normative acts and participation in administrative rulemaking; distinct from FOI requests. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/41571).
- **HG nr. 831/2022** — methodological rules for Law 52/2003 on decision-making transparency. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/256871).
- **Legea nr. 182/2002 privind protecția informațiilor clasificate** — framework for classified records and claimed disclosure exemptions. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/35209).
- **HG nr. 585/2002** — national standards for classified-information protection. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/37319).
- **Legea Arhivelor Naționale nr. 16/1996** — institutional recordkeeping, archive preservation and access. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/7937).

## 5. Data protection, equality, complaints and independent oversight

- **Legea nr. 190/2018** — Romanian supplementary provisions for personal-data protection; access, processing and national derogations. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/203151).
- **Legea nr. 363/2018** — data processing by competent authorities for prevention, investigation and prosecution of offences and execution of penalties; distinct from the general data-protection regime. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/209627).
- **Legea nr. 102/2005** — establishment, organisation and functions of the Romanian Data Protection Authority (ANSPDCP). **Distinct from OUG nr. 102/2005 on free movement.** [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/201568).
- **Legea nr. 35/1997 privind organizarea și funcționarea instituției Avocatul Poporului** — Ombudsman investigations, petitions, information demands and recommendations. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/198173).
- **OG nr. 137/2000 privind prevenirea și sancționarea tuturor formelor de discriminare** — anti-discrimination protections and CNCD procedures. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/79377).
- **Legea nr. 361/2022 privind protecția avertizorilor în interes public** — whistleblower reporting channels and protection; relevant to reports made by officials, **not** a general substitute for applicant complaints. [Official text](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/297292).

## 7. Romanian case law — Curtea Constituțională (CCR)

- **CCR Decizia nr. 534 din 18 iulie 2018** — Civil Code Article 277 and residence rights deriving from legally concluded same-sex marriages; **substantive conditional constitutionality ruling**. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/205439).
- **CCR Decizia nr. 12 din 14 ianuarie 2020** — administrative-litigation time limits under Law 554/2004; addresses **former** Article 11 wording. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/223771).
- **CCR Decizia nr. 738 din 8 octombrie 2020** — FOI exceptions and personal information under Law 544/2001, Articles 12 and 14. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/238318).
- **CCR Decizia nr. 391 din 4 iulie 2023** — FOI and personal-information exceptions, Articles 12(1)(d) and 14(1) of Law 544/2001; constitutionality challenge rejected as unfounded. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/281920).
- **CCR Decizia nr. 279 din 28 mai 2024** — FOI response rules and remedies under Law 544/2001; challenge to Article 7 rejected, other issues inadmissible. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/288849).
- **CCR Decizia nr. 92 din 27 februarie 2025** — FOI Law 544/2001 and petition-handling OG 27/2002; Article 7 challenge rejected, remaining challenges inadmissible. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/301121).
- **CCR Decizia nr. 708 din 9 decembrie 2025** — judicial consideration of FOI Law 544/2001. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/309623).
- **CCR Decizia nr. 768 din 11 decembrie 2025** — FOI access exceptions and judicial remedies under Law 544/2001; published in 2026. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/311166).

## 8. Romanian case law — Înalta Curte de Casație și Justiție (ÎCCJ)

- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 37 din 7 decembrie 2015 (hotărâre prealabilă)** — access to mixed FOI/personal-data documents: identifying details require anonymisation, but anonymised public information should be disclosed; **admitted and binding**, in the legal framework examined at that time. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/175268).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 28 din 24 aprilie 2017 (hotărâre prealabilă)** — interpretation of “autoritate publică” in administrative litigation; **partly admitted and binding**. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/189428).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 9 din 7 iunie 2021 (recurs în interesul legii)** — exception of illegality in relation to certain older individual administrative acts under Law 554/2004. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/247374).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 2 din 16 ianuarie 2023 (hotărâre prealabilă)** — enforcement of judgments ordering public authorities to issue an individual administrative act; application of Article 24 and delay penalties under Law 554/2004; **admitted and binding**. [Judgment](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/FormaPrintabila/00000G1FAZJKTJYXD7R2P99RIVAO9381).

### Additional Romanian referrals — inadmissible (no substantive precedent on referred question)

- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 19 din 14 septembrie 2020 (recurs în interesul legii)** — FOI access/cost issues; **referral declared inadmissible**, not an authoritative resolution of the cost question. [Decision](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/234297).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 50 din 14 iunie 2021 (hotărâre prealabilă)** — eligibility to request public information under Article 6 of Law 544/2001; **referral declared inadmissible**. [Decision](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/246015).

## 10. Romanian case-law follow-up — 11 October 2026

These follow-up records are categorized by topic and source quality. Official ÎCCJ/CCR pages are preferred where found. Reproductions and the court-authored digest remain explicitly secondary and must not be represented as official original texts. Outcomes are case-specific; the summaries below do not create general rules.

### Free movement and border remedies

- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 2100/2021 (31 March 2021)** — the Court annulled a restriction decision for failing to state the actual challenge deadline; the separate appeal against the border-refusal measure was inadmissible. The decision did not grant a substantive right of entry. **Quality: official ÎCCJ text.** [Official decision](https://www.scj.ro/1093/Detalii-jurisprudenta?customQuery%5B0%5D.Key=id&customQuery%5B0%5D.Value=183362).

### Visa refusals, evidence and reasons

- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 4163/2025 (24 September 2025)** — upheld issuance of short-stay visas to minor children of Nepalese parents legally resident in Romania; not an EU-family case. The follow-up identifies it as previously found, but it was absent from the active source manifest. **Quality: official ÎCCJ text.** [Official decision](https://www.scj.ro/1093/Detalii-jurisprudenta?customQuery%5B0%5D.Key=id&customQuery%5B0%5D.Value=237168).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 4135/2024 (26 September 2024)** — rejected post-hoc reliance on an undisclosed interview to justify a national work-visa refusal; not an EU-family case. **Quality: official ÎCCJ text.** [Official decision](https://www.scj.ro/1093/Detalii-jurisprudenta?customQuery%5B0%5D.Key=id&customQuery%5B0%5D.Value=224616).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 1089/2022 (24 February 2022)** — upheld a result requiring issuance in an ordinary family-reunification case because the Ministry's appeal grounds were procedurally insufficient; the High Court did not decide every substantive allegation. **Quality: official ÎCCJ text.** [Official decision](https://www.scj.ro/1093/Detalii-jurisprudenta?customQuery%5B0%5D.Key=id&customQuery%5B0%5D.Value=195590).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 2601/2018 (15 June 2018)** — permitted examination of an underlying IGI opinion during review of the final MAE decision, but upheld the refusal on the evidence; authority-favourable and not an EU-family case. **Quality: official ÎCCJ text.** [Official decision](https://www.scj.ro/1093/Detalii-jurisprudenta?customQuery%5B0%5D.Key=id&customQuery%5B0%5D.Value=156624).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 589/2020 (5 February 2020)** — upheld a long-stay visa refusal on the facts and record in that case; ordinary national route, with chronology inconsistencies noted in the reproduced record. **Quality: official ÎCCJ text.** [Official decision](https://www.scj.ro/1093/Detalii-jurisprudenta?customQuery%5B0%5D.Key=id&customQuery%5B0%5D.Value=187525).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 299/2025 (23 January 2025)** — dismissed a challenge to a national employment-visa refusal and distinguished an earlier work-permit approval from the later visa decision. **Quality: official ÎCCJ text.** [Official decision](https://www.scj.ro/1093/Detalii-jurisprudenta?customQuery%5B0%5D.Key=id&customQuery%5B0%5D.Value=231772).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 2236/2012 (8 May 2012)** — held MAE was the proper defendant for its own visa-revocation act and upheld the linked return decision and revocation; historical law. **Quality: reproduced judgment; official original not located in this follow-up.** [Reproduction](https://legeaz.net/spete-contencios-inalta-curte-iccj-2012/decizia-2236-2012).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 3799/2010 (23 September 2010)** — upheld an order requiring MAE/consular personnel to receive a citizenship application but removed moral damages; an analogy about receiving filings, not a visa-lodgement holding. **Quality: official ÎCCJ text.** [Official decision](https://www.scj.ro/1093/Detalii-jurisprudenta?customQuery%5B0%5D.Key=id&customQuery%5B0%5D.Value=60414).

### Court access, fees, digital filing and public information

- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 3838/2024 (13 September 2024)** — the appeal was not heard on its merits after an unpaid court fee and failure to use available fee-review/legal-aid procedures; no merits holding on the underlying visa refusal. **Quality: official ÎCCJ text.** [Official decision](https://www.scj.ro/1093/Detalii-jurisprudenta?customQuery%5B0%5D.Key=id&customQuery%5B0%5D.Value=227409).
- **ÎCCJ Decizia nr. 3405/2024 (19 June 2024)** — rejected an immigration work-authorisation appeal after residence status had lapsed; it did not establish a general equivalence between online and physical filing. **Quality: official ÎCCJ text.** [Official decision](https://www.scj.ro/1093/Detalii-jurisprudenta?customQuery%5B0%5D.Key=id&customQuery%5B0%5D.Value=228545).
- **Curtea de Apel Oradea Decizia nr. 566 (6 November 2024)** — judge-attributed digest concerning access to information already held, limits on requiring new statistical analysis, and repeated FOI requests. It is not the complete official judgment. **Quality: court-authored digest hosted by a professional association.** [Digest](https://apjgalati.ro/jurisprudenta/comunicare-informatii-de-interes-public-legea-nr-544-2001/).
- **Curtea de Apel Suceava Decizia nr. 21 (12 January 2021)** — reports that Law 544/2001 did not require an authority to process supplied raw information into a new requested breakdown. **Quality: reproduced judgment; official original not located in this follow-up.** [Reproduction](https://www.jurisprudenta.com/jurisprudenta/speta-17vkqkup/).

### Petitions and preliminary complaints

- **CCR Decizia nr. 1.355 (11 December 2008)** — rejected constitutional challenges concerning repetitive petitions under OG 27/2002 and preliminary complaints under Law 554/2004. Its statutory context is historical; check later amendments. **Quality: official Portal Legislativ text.** [Official decision](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/101319).
