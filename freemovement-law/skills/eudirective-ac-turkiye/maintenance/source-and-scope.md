# C9 source note module (limited scope)

The package contains official-source **identity notes**, not the full official acts/pages.

Only use for reusable candidate legal analysis and local procedural orientation. Do not infer established violations, guaranteed currentness, authenticated reviewer identity, or permission to process private applicant data.

C3 contract conformance does not prove legal correctness or source-text authenticity. A `verified_source_note` means the official URL/identity and scoped proposition were checked, not that the notes reproduce exact statutes. The SHA-256 binds the **local note** bytes and does not authenticate the remote webpage.

This module does not replace the separate Sweden legal-pack maintainer or an independently curated complete national source pack.
