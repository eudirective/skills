# C25 - Grievance completeness, authority position and applicant strategy

**Normal-use, read-only guidance.** Use with C23/C24 whenever a real or fictional applicant describes an EU free-movement or family-visa dispute. Preserve their first-hand account as working facts for advice; distinguish that from what a court or authority will accept as proved. Keep all analysis and drafts in the conversation unless the user explicitly authorizes another action. No C6/C7/C17/C21 private-data intake, tracking or sending is enabled.

## 1. First reconstruct the authority's acts, not a theoretical immigration-law exam

Before deciding which provision matters most, create a concise **internal event-to-grievance register** from every distinct reported authority action, statement, demand, refusal, procedural omission, treatment issue and threatened deadline. Identify the actor and date only as far as needed. Record what the applicant actually seeks: admission of a visa application, written reasons, an apology, a conduct investigation, preservation/disclosure of evidence, disciplinary review, or another remedy. In the *user-visible* response, summarize only material findings; do not expose a mechanical ledger unless useful.

For EACH material reported event, determine whether it supports: (i) a prima facie grievance, (ii) an unresolved issue needing one decisive fact, (iii) an evidence/preservation action despite uncertain legal merits, or (iv) an explained reason why no actionable issue is established. **No event may silently disappear.** Before grouping grievances under one claim, preserve separate event-level dispositions: a shouted threat and physical removal are two acts even where both fall under staff treatment. For each, record its independent legal question, evidentiary need, remedy/preservation consequence and any explained exclusion. Do not let a completed category label conceal an unassessed event. A legal flaw in one track cannot automatically defeat independent tracks (staff conduct, lodging procedure, document demands, evidence access, delay, formal review). Staff mistreatment and disproportionate physical handling trigger a **separate conduct track** whether or not the visa could lawfully be accepted at that post. Before giving national remedies, verify authority, procedure, powers and limits; the Visa Code Articles 38(5) and 39 are relevant EU-level starting points for applicable consular conduct, not proof of misconduct.

### C27 pre-advice disclosure and timing review

Before recommending any applicant communication, consider what was **drafted but never sent**, sent to a named recipient, demonstrably delivered, merely acknowledged as received, expressly acknowledged in content, or actually relied on. Check that same document used in a different proceeding was separately provided there. Identify information already furnished before the authority's next action while distinguishing event occurrence from later discovery. Relevance of mandatory disclosure and choice of procedural strategy are distinct inquiries. Cross-reference `references/state-engine/c27-case-state.md` and explain material unfinished checks in each response.

## 2. Maintain a distinct defense register and identify the authority's actual position

For each potentially adverse rule or defense, classify separately:

- **Authority position:** `expressly_invoked`, `indicated_unconfirmed`, `not_invoked`, or `unknown`. Never recast a possible defense as the authority's actual ground without case evidence.
- **Authority knowledge:** `acknowledged`, `disclosed_unacknowledged`, `not_shown`, or `unknown`. A fact buried in an email is neither proof of reading nor waiver; where appropriate it shows the information was supplied before later acts.
- **Source of adverse proposition:** binding statute/decision, national procedure, official explanation, applicant report, model inference. Show the gap where one exists. **For an expressly invoked ground, cite or identify the actual authority communication or case-file passage and its date/sequence, distinguishing exact quoted language from a paraphrase of the applicant's account.** If no inspectable authority statement exists, do not silently classify the ground as expressly invoked. If the authority relied on a ground only later, preserve both the original and later positions instead of rewriting history. An applicant's own early disclosure is not an authority statement.
- **Legal role:** an actual threshold to the *relief requested*, a potential defense, an alternative administrative explanation, or peripheral background.
- **Procedural impact:** recorded grounds for the decision, question affecting available relief, or a future contingency only.

**Strategic proportionality:** An uninvoked potential defense goes in the *internal risk/contingency analysis*, not automatically into the first complaint or its headline. Consider raising it only if it is necessary for a requested remedy, an answer to an actual objection, or avoidance of a foreseeable procedural error. Do not suggest misrepresentation, false answers or withholding legally required information. An authority's silence does not waive mandatory statutory competence or bind the authority to accept an otherwise inadmissible case. If such competence conditions are prerequisites to the applicant's specific demand for relief, analyse them candidly without falsely attributing them to the authority.

Keep **legal importance**, **evidential support**, **authority reliance**, **relief relevance** and **strategic priority** separate. No single automated scalar score substitutes for judgment.

## 3. Choose remedy actions issue by issue, not a generic escalation list

For each viable grievance, connect: applicant fact -> precise applicable provision/scope -> the conduct complained of -> desired practical outcome -> actual competent route and its powers -> documentary requirements -> deadline or trigger if verified -> foreseeable downside. Distinguish:

- **Visa entitlement, lodging and written grounds:** request acceptance, a written account of status, or reasons according to the applicable rules; formal visa appeals may depend on an actual refusal and service.
- **Process access:** mission/central supervisory complaint, Visa Code Article 38(5) complaint procedure where applicable; seek records and a proportionate corrective action.
- **Staff treatment/physical removal:** separate conduct/supervisory complaint and incident review; do not make it depend on visa success. Check attribution where contractors, diplomatic staff or external security are involved; do not automatically treat all staff as consular employees subject to identical duties.
- **Records and evidence:** Article 15 subject access where personal data are held, administrative file access and preservation; see `references/c25-evidence-acquisition.md`.
- **EU-level assistance:** SOLVIT may address the underlying cross-border EU-rights administrative problem, but generally cannot impose staff discipline, order damages, replace national appeals, or suspend deadlines.

An outcome may be legally arguable yet practically difficult. Distinguish access to a complaint process, investigation, acknowledgment, corrective measure, disciplinary sanction, court order and compensation. Do not imply all remedies have equivalent powers.

## 4. Evidence standards and questioning discipline

Use *reported fact* as the working premise for advice, *document already seen* to check competing accounts, and *proof before a decision-maker* as a distinct later evidentiary question. Do not write generic 'could not be independently verified' disclaimers; explain when corroboration materially changes the chosen action or likely outcome. Where statements are disputed, suggest honest first-person complaint wording and ways to obtain corroboration. Do not instruct applicants to invent or embellish facts.

Ask at most three **outcome-changing** questions. For each, state what changes: applicable legal regime, distinct remedy, deadline, competent body, actual defense invoked, or preservation need. Already answered questions are not asked again. Do not force the applicant to establish lawful status merely to discuss an independent staff-conduct grievance or EU-family checklist-scope issue. Treat any country-specific lawful-presence requirement as relevant to the precise consular relief sought, not to every grievance.

## 5. Mandatory pre-answer completeness and strategic-safety review

Before delivering substantive advice, check:

1. Did every material adverse act/omission receive its own grievance disposition or justified exclusion?
2. Did independent conduct/access/records complaints survive even if territorial competence or visa entitlement is disputed?
3. Did I identify the **actual** authority position rather than invent defenses? If uninvoked, is any proposed proactive disclosure truly necessary?
4. Am I making the difference between an internal candid risk note and information in an external complaint clear, without suggesting evasion of mandatory disclosure?
5. Did I consider targeted access/preservation of authority-controlled evidence, including potential GDPR Article 15 rights, and address time-sensitive footage or records promptly? If considered, did I actually give a route-specific next action or explain why that route is inappropriate, rather than merely mention GDPR?
6. Have I checked authority powers, deadline triggers, source currency and the consequence of each question, instead of supplying a generic escalation list?
7. If the user supplies new facts, which issue tracks, actual-position tags, risk weights and actions change—and which independent claims stay intact?

### Action-order guidance (categorical, not a numerical merits score)

Lead with **time-sensitive records preservation** and **verified formal deadlines**, then the strongest actionable conduct/process/entitlement issues aligned to what the applicant wants, then non-urgent supervisory and information requests. Note possible mandatory threshold obstacles to the *precise relief requested* in a short contingency section, not as an invented consular ground. These priority categories reflect urgency and practical leverage, not a guarantee of winning. Do not hide a serious threshold obstacle simply because it makes the complaint harder.

**Answer pattern:** lead with the applicant's strongest presently actionable grievance(s), provide immediate low-regret steps, give a short **contingent risks** note for uninvoked legal obstacles that matter to requested relief, then ask at most three decisive questions. For a simple question, be concise; do not force public tables.

## 6. Runtime and evaluation boundary

If available, `scripts/c25_strategy_gate.py` is a **synthetic categorical review aid**, not an extractor of narratives, independent law validator, legal strategist or privacy system. Its output flags incomplete modeled grievance/evidence coverage; the reviewer is responsible for mapping real events and interpreting law. Do **not** feed real narratives, names, concrete dates, case IDs or identifying combinations into its fixtures. Never claim this gate ran when it did not. Real-case legal guidance is available conversationally even without Python. Perform fresh, independently graded ChatGPT first-response and multi-turn tests before asserting user-facing reliability.
