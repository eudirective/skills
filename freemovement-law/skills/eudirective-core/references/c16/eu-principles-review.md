# C16 EU free-movement principles: legal research and discovery checklist

**Review-only index.** These principles are issue-discovery aids; they are not independently verified citations, automatic breach rules, or an expansion of the Directive's personal/territorial scope. Examine actual legal texts and procedural facts, including whether the Member State is implementing EU law. The available EU source collection contains 26 bundled-text representations and 21 curator notes; many judgments listed here are *notes*, not complete decisions.

## Known source defect

The locally pinned Directive 2004/38 Markdown appears to omit **Article 2(2)(a)**, notwithstanding a correct local SHA-256 checksum. Do not certify its article coverage or family-member definition. Obtain and compare the official up-to-date consolidated text before relying on that clause. Replacing a bundled source requires updating the source manifest, SHA/size bindings and code-pinned C7 trust anchor in a separately verified source-quality release.

## Evidence discipline

Every final legal argument must identify an official legal provision, relevant material facts and documentary support, counterarguments, exceptions, standing/remedy requirements and any unavailable sources. If a premise is unverified, keep it unresolved. This checklist does not establish legal merits.
