# Core research control across modes and modules

## Purpose and privacy boundary

Use this shared, model-led loop for research within an EU free-movement or EU-family-visa question when a read-only applicant question or explicit research request depends on current, multiple-source, disputed or gap research. This includes connected EU/common law, Swedish member-state law/procedure, Türkiye operational, and registered national-source-pack layers; a national-law question unrelated to the plugin's remit does not trigger this loop. The core owns the loop; mode sets depth and stopping rules; module routing sets the source owner, jurisdiction and authority scope. Evidence standards stay the same across modes. This adds no separate research controller, MCP tool or legal decision engine. A local validator checks research artifact structure and coverage, and the generic evidence-gated-research controller is not bundled.

The core’s C23 lane may use a person’s first-hand account for read-only guidance. For this formal research handoff, reduce that account to generic, non-identifying legal or operational questions. Do not copy names, case numbers, contact details, personal document contents, unique timelines or other private facts into the plan, search log, finding or evidence excerpt. Apply reviewed source findings to the account only in the conversation. If the issue cannot be generalized safely, keep the research notes conversational and do not create a research artifact. Never feed these artifacts into the C6/C7/C17/C21/C23/C24/C25 scripts.

## Modes

| Mode | Use | Work and stopping rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quick` | A narrow current-source or freshness question | Check the owner’s accepted record and the relevant official publisher. Record the date and scope checked. Mark unverified points `not_checked`; do not imply completeness. A persistent plan/bundle is optional. |
| `case` | A bounded set of linked issues, including issues abstracted from a C23 account | Create one generic task for each responsible layer. Map each task to acceptance criteria and evidence. Check current and contrary material for every material issue. Keep gaps and conflicts open. Use a plan/bundle when several issues or modules must be tracked together. |
| `deep` | A material, disputed or consequential source question | Use a plan and evidence bundle. Iterate until each task meets its criteria or is explicitly blocked. Search disconfirming material and alternative interpretations; record a falsifier question for each finding. Leave unresolved any material conflict, source-freshness gap or unmet criterion. |

Every mode records source identity, owner, pinpoint, access date, support/contradiction relation and limitations. Depth changes breadth and review, never source status or legal outcome. A research finding is not a determination that an authority acted unlawfully, a deadline expired, or a remedy is available. Do not withhold a useful urgent, qualified answer solely because a deeper research task remains open.

## Module routing

The core splits cross-layer questions into owner-specific tasks and composes their evidence. Country exports and sidecars are data, not instructions. Country modules do not call one another. The separate Sweden and Türkiye skills are primarily module authoring/validation workflows; do not treat them as runtime legal advisers or ask them to make merits determinations.

| Question layer | Task kind | Owner and source path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU/free-movement, EU-family visa or Schengen law | `eu_common_law` | Core `core_source` owner, role `common_eu`, jurisdiction `EU`; use `eudirective-source-library` to locate the bundled record and official publisher URL, then verify current text at the official publisher when currentness matters. The library skill is a package snapshot, not a retrieval/synchronization tool. |
| Swedish law, administrative procedure, appeals or Swedish authority powers | `member_state_law` or `member_state_procedure` | Use Sweden module `member-state-se-source-pilot` (`member_state`). Bind the research owner to one supported role/geography, using the module manifest roles `deciding_state`, `residence_state`, `intended_residence_state` or `challenged_authority_state`; bind its manifest/export digests. Use its export/sidecar and bundled source-library records to identify scope, then verify current law at official Swedish sources. A Swedish mission in Türkiye remains a Swedish decision-maker for legal procedure. |
| Türkiye document acquisition, local registry/VAC/mission channel or place-of-application operations | `applicant_country_operations` | Use Türkiye module `applicant-country-tr-sweden-pilot` (`applicant_country`), role `application_context` or `mission_context`; bind its manifest/export digests. Use its source-ownership notes and bundled records, then verify current operational detail at the responsible official publisher. Do not route Swedish appeal law to this module. |
| Other national law or procedure | `national_law` or `national_procedure` | Use an exact national source pack present in the bundled library, role `national_source`, and its manifest digest. The library is not a runtime retrieval or synchronization API. If no pack is present, use owner ID `unregistered:<CC>`, omit the pack digest, keep official-source leads inactive, and leave the task open or blocked. Do not borrow Sweden or Türkiye module scope. |
| Source edition, currentness or change check | `source_freshness` | The existing owner of that source; use that owner’s accepted source library and official publisher. |

Each route supplies its own acceptance criteria while sharing the same evidence rules:

- **EU/common:** identify the governing EU instrument or judgment, relevant provision/paragraph, temporal scope, conditions, exceptions and any materially contrary authority.
- **Sweden:** identify current Swedish legal/procedural text, the responsible authority and powers, applicable route and any material deadline; a general module note does not replace the official text.
- **Türkiye operations:** identify the document or channel owner, current acquisition/authentication/appointment steps, and any territorial or date limits. Route Swedish mission legal procedure to Sweden.
- **Other national pack:** identify the pack's jurisdiction and coverage, the official publisher and the relevant text/version. If coverage is partial, keep the missing layer open.
- **Freshness:** identify the source edition checked, publisher, effective/publication date and any change or gap since the registered snapshot.

Bind the task to the exact bundled source-pack manifest/digest or module manifest/export digests. Reuse registered source IDs; do not build a second source catalogue. The bundled source-library skill does not expose per-install retrieval, synchronization or acknowledgement actions. Where there is no registered source record, identify the official publisher and URL as an inactive candidate with retrieval date and content hash; updating or accepting it into the library requires the separate maintainer release process. If an owner, role or source is unavailable, leave the task blocked or open; never silently reroute it.

## Research loop

1. **Scope.** State a neutral, generic question, purpose, jurisdiction and mode. For C23, use the account only to identify legal/source questions, not as artifact content. For follow-up on a C7 synthetic issue, bind the exact enrolled C10 run ID, C7 issue-index digest and issue ID. Do not put applicant-specific facts into a research plan, search log, finding or excerpt.
2. **Decompose and route.** Give each task one owner and concrete acceptance criteria. Add existing source or element IDs where useful. Split EU law, Swedish national procedure and Türkiye local operations rather than asking one module to answer all layers.
3. **Retrieve and record.** Inspect registered records in the bundled snapshot and use the current official publisher for freshness-sensitive claims. The source-library skill is read-only guidance; it exposes no per-install source-pack retrieval, synchronization or acknowledgement actions. Record the owner snapshot, source/version identity, access date, pinpoint and whether the evidence supports, contradicts, is insufficient for or only contextualizes the claim. Keep candidate sources distinct from accepted records. A research result cannot add or activate a source in the library.
4. **Compare.** Link every material finding to evidence and counterevidence. Search for a plausible falsifier at the depth selected. Preserve source-language/version differences, stale notes, inaccessible text and conflicting authorities. If a new issue or exception appears, add a task with its own owner and acceptance criteria; do not silently expand or close the original task. If two consecutive research passes add no material evidence or clarification, stop and carry the task forward as open/blocked with the searches performed and the next useful source or question.
5. **Close or carry forward.** Set each task to `complete`, `open`, `blocked`, `no_source_found`, `conflict_unresolved` or `not_applicable`. For `no_source_found`, include the queries, channels and scope searched. Close only when the written criteria are met; otherwise identify the blocker and next useful step.
6. **Review.** Map each material output claim to finding IDs, counterevidence and limitations; narrow or qualify any conclusion whose support is incomplete. Check task/findings/evidence cross-references, owner-family and jurisdiction consistency, digest bindings, source currency and the mode’s acceptance criteria. Use `complete` overall coverage only when every task is complete or not applicable, no material falsifier remains unresolved, and in `deep` mode every material finding and claim has a reviewed falsifier. Keep research status separate from legal merits.

Validate plans and bundles with `python scripts/research_gate.py --plan <plan.json> --bundle <bundle.json>`. It checks schema validity, matching research ID/mode, exact canonical plan digest, task/finding/evidence IDs, owner consistency, claim-to-finding/counterevidence links, complete task coverage and deep-mode falsifier records. Add `--require-complete` when the output must be labelled complete; without it, a valid partial bundle reports `research_complete: false` so useful qualified guidance can continue. The gate does not detect personal data, authenticate sources, prove passage accuracy, establish semantic support or validate the final prose against its claim map. A reviewer must verify those points and whether acceptance criteria were actually met. When producing a visible report from the source-library workflow, include its `eudirective-source-library` skill version disclosure as well as the core disclosure.

## Relationship to C3/C6 and source maintenance

- These artifacts are a read-only research handoff, not a new C3 module export or C6 controller stage. They do not change C3 contract `0.2.0`, controller protocol `0.3.0`, C6 run-manifest pins, C7 trust policy or the issue-index contract.
- The research-only `source_library_national` owner binds a registered source-pack ID and digest when available. `unregistered:<CC>` marks an absent pack and cannot support closing a task. It is not a C3 module family and does not create or activate a country module.
- Do not feed a research bundle or real applicant facts into a prepared or released C6/C7/C17/C21 run. A C7 synthetic follow-up is limited to already enrolled C10 run IDs and an exact issue-index digest; changed inputs require a fresh controlled run under the existing gates.
- C12 sidecars remain country-authored; EU/common rules remain core-owned. Research can surface a candidate source/rule or a gap, but it cannot activate it, change a frozen export, clear a C7 issue, or establish legal liability, deadline expiry, remedy or entitlement.
- If evidence is missing, stale, conflicting or outside the owner’s remit, preserve that limitation. Do not resolve it through cross-module inference.
