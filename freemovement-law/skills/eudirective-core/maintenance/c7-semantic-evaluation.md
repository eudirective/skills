For each task, replay the exact bundled synthetic prompt and preserve its input/output digests. Obtain the official full text and applicable version for every source ID, then ask a legally competent reviewer to examine the actual claim against the text, pinpoint passages, adverse or limiting authorities, and uncertainty. Record identity, date, review scope, source links and conclusions in a separate immutable result with a digest. Prompt text or source inventory alone cannot establish a legal conclusion. Keep the synthetic/controller mechanical results separate from legal correctness. Never convert a mock evaluation, fixture hash, stage replay or this checklist into an independent legal-merits approval.
