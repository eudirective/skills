# C23 applicant assistance - modifier-only boundaries

C23 introduces an explicit **model-led, read-only real-user guidance lane**. This is a routing and answer-quality change, not an amendment to C6/C7/C17/C21 Python intake guarantees.

- Preserve the C6/C7 controller's synthetic-enrolled input checks; no new raw private document acceptance, no telemetry and no automatic consular communications.
- Preserve the C21 pinned fictional fixture and non-send contracts; the new advisory lane must never claim that users' real applicant data has passed local pre-model anonymisation.
- Keep national rules in their country modules. The Romanian scenario in `references/c23-applicant-case-guidance.md` and evaluation fixtures is an illustrative external-source check, **not** a new Romanian law catalogue in core.
- Separate the legal scope of document requirements from the fact of what an officer did. Make source-based yes/no/conditional answers rather than using an evidence disclaimer to evade a normative conclusion.
- Test missing-data triage, non-journalistic style, competence separation, meaningful remedial paths and conflicting underlying facts. Test cases with a user statement but no recorded interview; corroboration is not a precondition to advisory analysis.
- When the next plugin adapter is developed, do not broaden its `c22_portable_api.py` input schema to accept raw applicant data merely because the skill now answers real cases in conversation. Host data isolation remains not verified.
- The C23 scripted checker, if used, validates an *abstract, non-identifying analytical disposition* only; it cannot validate whether a user's story or legal merits are true.
- C15f and country-law completeness remain open. Do not claim production legal validation, privileged confidentiality, host deletion, or safe external publication.
