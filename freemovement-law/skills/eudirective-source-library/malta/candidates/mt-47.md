Freedom of Information Application

Home | File an FOI Application

In terms of article 23(1) of the Freedom of Information Act (Cap. 496 of the Laws of Malta), you may apply to the Commissioner for a decision notice whether, in any specified respect:

- a request for information made to a public authority has been dealt with in accordance with the requirements of the Act; or

- a public authority is in compliance with the requirements set out in Part III of the Act.

Before applying for a decision notice, you must exhaust the internal complaints procedure which is provided by the public authority.

Your complaint shall be lodged with our Office within sixty (60) days from the receipt of the public authority’s final decision.

The applicant and the public authority may appeal against the decision notice of the Commissioner before the Information and Data Protection Appeals Tribunal within twenty (20) working days from the date of service.

The decision notices will be published on our website and may be accessed through this link.

To submit your complaint, you are being guided to complete dedicated online form.

If you close the form before pressing ‘Submit Application’ button, no information will be retained, and your complaint will not be recorded in our internal system.

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are mandatory.

Our Data Protection Notice can be viewed by accessing this link.

Last updated, 4th May 2023
