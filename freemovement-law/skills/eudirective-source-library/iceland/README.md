# Iceland source pack

Iceland is an EEA EFTA State, not an EU Member State. This pack concerns EEA/EFTA free movement and third-country family members, entry and residence documents, procedures and remedies, and related public services. Icelandic EEA/EFTA, Swiss, Nordic, Icelandic-return and ordinary family-reunification routes must remain distinct.

The [candidate manifest](references/candidate-manifest.json) lists 34 source records. The [source acceptance register](references/source-acceptance.json) accepts fourteen official external references within stated limits: seven Alþingi consolidated Acts in edition 158a (1 October 2026), the EFTA EEA-Lex record, Rules No. 3/2022 on court-decision publication, the Regulation 540/2017 identity/amendment page, three 2026 amendment texts, and the Ministry of Justice immigration directory. No local copy or topic is accepted, and the country review remains incomplete.

The earlier capture pass saved 21 responses and converted 20 pages for review. Those local copies remain unaccepted. The six Alþingi capture errors do not prevent use of the current official law-page links. Local capture, conversion, and an Iceland-specific updater are optional and are not prerequisites for using the accepted references.

The main open source issues are the three-month/six-month registration conflict in public guidance; the practical completeness of court searches; current mission-specific visa instructions; and mapping the accepted laws, Courts Act and civil-procedure rules to concrete judicial remedies. The linked base text of Regulation 540/2017 is not a current consolidation after amendments 669/2026, 724/2026 and 725/2026; the register and amendment links preserve the relevant official material without reconstructing the full Gazette chain.

See the [source list](references/source-list.md), [acceptance register](references/source-acceptance.json), and [sustainability assessment](references/sustainability-assessment.md). The repository-wide [source selection policy](../references/legal-text-edition-policy.md) governs government consolidations, external links, and optional local copies.
