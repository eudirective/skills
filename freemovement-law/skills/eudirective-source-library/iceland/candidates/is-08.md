Residence card for family member of an EEA/EFTA citizen

Immediate family members of EEA/EFTA citizens, who are themselves non-EEA/EFTA citizens, may reside in Iceland if their residence is based on the rights of an EEA/EFTA citizen residing in Iceland.

If immediate family members of EEA/EFTA citizens wish to reside in Iceland for longer than three months, they must apply for a residence card to the Directorate of Immigration.

Who is the application for?

Immediate family members of EEA/EFTA citizens

Spouse (marital or cohabiting spouse).

Descendants, i.e. children or grandchildren of the EEA/EFTA citizen or his/her spouse, who are under the age of 21 or are their dependants.

Dependent direct relatives in the ascending line and those of the spouse (parents/grandparents).

Custodial parent living with their child who is an EEA/EFTA citizen and under 18 years of age.

Family members of students
Only spouses and decendants under the age of 21 have the right to reside in Iceland as family members of EEA/EFTA citizens who reside in Iceland as students.

EEA/EFTA citizens

EEA/EFTA citizens are citizens of Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden and Switzerland.

Icelandic citizens are not considered EEA/EFTA citizens in this context, except in cases where they have resided in another EEA/EFTA country and have recently moved back to Iceland.

The close relative of an Icelandic citizen can therefore only apply for a residence card as a relative of an EEA/EFTA citizen in Iceland if:

the Icelandic citizen has lived in another EEA/EFTA country with a right of residence for at least three months. The person in question has had a right of residence if they have, for example, worked, looked for work, studied or been self-sufficient during their stay in the other EEA/EFTA country.

the Icelandic citizen and their close relative lived together in the other EEA/EFTA country immediately before moving to Iceland together.

If you are a citizen of a country outside the EEA/EFTA and want to move to Iceland to live with an Icelandic citizen who is not considered an EEA/EFTA citizen according to the above requirements, you must instead apply for a residence permit.

How to apply

Applications must be submitted in their original form on paper together with the necessary supporting documents.

They can either be sent by regular mail to the Directorate of Immigration or delivered to the drop box in the Directorate’s reception.

Directorate of Immigration (see map)
Dalvegur 18

Iceland

Applications can also be handed in and paid for in the Directorate’s reception or at the offices of District Commissioners outside the capital area.

Processing fee

The fee for the issuance of a residence card for an EEA/EFTA family member is 8.000 ISK.

Application confirmation

An applicant for a residence card as a family member of an EEA/EFTA citizen can ask for a confirmation of the application by contacting the Directorate of Immigration.
