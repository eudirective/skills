Submit a tip to the Data Protection Authority

You can submit a tip to the Data Protection Authority on alleged violations of data protection laws, irrespective of whether they concern you or others. The Data Protection Authority will assess whether a tip gives rise to a reaction, such as guidance or supervisory operations. The Data Protection Authority does not, in its own initiative, inform you on the progress or the conclusion of the case.

Fields marked with an asterisk are required.

You are welcome to contact the Data Protection Authority for telephone consultation on Thursdays from 9 AM to 12 PM (tel. +354 510 9600).
