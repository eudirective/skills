## Findings

- Iceland is an EEA EFTA State. EFTA's official record identifies Directive 2004/38/EC as incorporated by JCD 158/2007 and in force in the EEA. The Icelandic Foreign Nationals Act separately defines EEA and EFTA citizens and contains Chapter XI special rules. This supports a partial functional comparison with Denmark's EU-residence source; it does not establish legal equivalence.
- The strongest national-law acquisition lead is the Althingi Lagasafn SGML ZIP. The official archive lists dated editions and says they can be unpacked and imported into other systems. It also states that later-passed laws are not included in an older edition. A sustainable monitor should track new editions and supplement the bundle with authentic later A-deild publications.
- Iceland's EEA/EFTA family guidance distinguishes third-country family members, EEA/EFTA citizens, Icelandic citizens returning after exercising EEA rights, and ordinary Icelandic family residence. It groups Switzerland within its public-facing EEA/EFTA label, so the source record must preserve the Icelandic statutory categories and treaty basis separately.
- The EEA/EFTA registration page says over three months in its body, while its form heading and the entry-requirements page say over six months. This must be confirmed with Registers Iceland and reconciled to the Act before operational use.
- Regulation 540/2017 has an unresolved freshness problem: the regulation collection lists several 2026 amendments, but the linked “current” PDF identifies a text dated before those amendments. Keep the PDF as the closest government-published text with its stated cutoff; check only the listed 2026 changes that affect provisions in scope. A full Gazette-chain reconciliation is not required.
- KNU decisions are published through the Government portal after the final result is communicated to the appellant and are redacted. Court publication rules also require anonymisation; neither collection was shown to be exhaustive or available through a stable feed.

See the [sustainability assessment](sustainability-assessment.md) for proposed-route evidence and open work. No source is accepted, and no retrieval/conversion rule is implemented.
