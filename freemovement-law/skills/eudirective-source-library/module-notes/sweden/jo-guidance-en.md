## Confirmed scoped statements

JO does not change or annul visa decisions or award damages. A complaint generally becomes a public record on receipt, subject to statutory confidentiality. Normally no inquiry for matters older than two years; not all complaints investigated. Ordinary appeal deadlines continue separately. Verified 2026-10-10.
