- Capture rules use ordinary allowlisted HTTPS, fixed response and redirect limits, exact-byte hashes, recorded provenance and separate external-reference/local-copy dispositions. The workflow does not bypass access challenges.
- Captured files are stored under `captures/raw/`. The tracked responses are replayable without ignored `temp/` copies. A changed hash must return to review.
- The source-specific converter has deterministic replay for Greek and English Protocol No. 10, Greek and English 2015 Green Line snapshots, the Greek Cap.105 amendment enactment, and the EDD 215/19 decision. It verifies captured-source hashes, source identity markers, page runs and output hashes.

The 56-page replay matches the stored candidate after 29 correction rules (32 replacements) covering 26 logged discrepancy entries. Use this OCR text only if a specific passage is needed and verify that passage against the scanned page; full-document OCR acceptance is not required where a government-published consolidation or closer government text can be used.

## Remaining sustainability risks

- The closest identified government-published texts for Law 7(I)/2007 and Cap.105 have not been directly reviewed: the OLC English Law 7(I)/2007 PDF route and Greek Cap.105 PDF route returned errors. Their index records remain useful official leads, but metadata alone does not supply the wording. Local copies are optional. Do not reconstruct every Gazette amendment; review a usable consolidated text and check only a material post-cutoff change or commencement condition.
- Article 146, administrative-procedure and court-law texts, procedural rules, legal aid, and Law 99(I)/2014 need authoritative current Greek verification.
- There is one captured official humanitarian-return appeal and two Greek-language EU-family judgment leads on a nonofficial mirror. The mirror decisions are not officially corroborated or locally captured, and reproducible discovery across competent Cypriot courts remains open.
- Current official forms, checklists, consular instructions, appointment routes and providers need direct, date-bound review where they affect the work. Local capture and refresh rules are optional unless the library will retain and monitor copies. Existing evidence still leaves the Greek MEU2 three-month/four-month discrepancy unresolved, a broad Washington mission claim to reconcile, and no identified Greek counterpart for MFA visa-exemption guidance.
- The Green Line Regulation is held only as a 2015 consolidated snapshot; later EU changes and Cyprus operating rules are unresolved.
- The historic Gazette OCR has page-level visual review and deterministic correction, not exhaustive word-level comparison. That review is needed only if relying on the full OCR transcription; it is not a gate where a usable government consolidation can be used instead. Re-running OCR still depends on a separate OCR tool.
- Publisher reuse terms are not assessed for redistribution.
