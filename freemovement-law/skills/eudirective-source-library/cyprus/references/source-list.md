# Cyprus source discovery register

**Skill:** author-source-pack-rules v1.0.1  
**Research date:** 18 October 2026  
**Target:** Cyprus, EU Member State. Comparison leads include Denmark, Sweden, Czechia, Hungary, Romania, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, and Croatia; these do not establish equivalent laws or complete coverage.  
**Languages:** Greek and English official materials; Greek is the operative Gazette language.  
**Scope:** legislation and guidance for EU/EEA/Swiss movement and third-country family members, visas, forms, administrative procedure and remedies, oversight, access to information, privacy, legal aid, and courts.

Candidate relationship, verification status, and retrieval status are separate. “Official search result only” means the official result was seen but the document/page was not directly inspected. A bounded search outcome is not proof that no source exists.

## Capture outcome (2026-10-19)

The shared bounded HTTPS rule attempted all 44 candidates. Five source texts converted and replayed offline: the N-Lex page was staged as a changed candidate, three CyLaw legal-text pages were decoded from their declared Windows-1253 encoding, and the original Gazette PDF for Law 7(I)/2007 was saved. Six responses were interactive/search shells; 31 requests returned HTTP 403; two official Law Commissioner files returned HTTP 404. No source is accepted.

Official search results identify current Gov.cy pages for residence cards and MEU forms and a Law Commissioner English consolidation of the free-movement law through 2023 ([residence cards](https://www.gov.cy/moi/en/residence-cards/), [MEU family-member forms](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/en/documents/e-u-e-e-a-citizens-family-member/), [consolidated legislation index](https://www.gov.cy/officelawcommissioner/documents/armodiotites-epitropoy/translations-consolidations/)). Direct requests to those pages returned 403 in this capture environment, so search indexing is discovery evidence only, not a stored full-text capture. The direct original 2007 Gazette PDF route succeeded, but that is not a current consolidation.

## Search coverage and limitations

Official channels reviewed on 18 October 2026 included N-Lex Cyprus; the Government Printing Office Gazette and archive; gov.cy and the Migration Department; the Law Commissioner; the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and mission pages; the Law Office of the Republic; the Ombudsman; the Information Commissioner; the Data Protection Commissioner; and Cyprus court sites. The review also used EURES Cyprus as an official guidance cross-check and CyLaw only as a secondary amendment-chain lead.

Search themes covered the Government Gazette and legislative publication, EU/EEA residence certificates and family-member routes (MEU1/2/3 and MVIS8), visas and Schengen status, mission-specific instructions, Article 146 administrative recourse, Ombudsman complaints, information access, personal-data complaints, legal aid, and court decisions.

The exact query strings from the wider source sweep were not retained in the research notes. A supplemental web search on 18 October 2026 used “Cyprus Law 158(I)/1999 99(I)/2014 28(I)/2020 official Administrative Law CyLaw”, “Cyprus Administrative Court Law 131(I)/2015 CyLaw official Gazette”, and `site:gov.cy "Law 158(I)/1999" Cyprus administrative law`. It found CyLaw amendment-history leads and the official Law Office explanation of Article 146 jurisdiction. Before implementing any retrieval rule, rerun and record exact Greek and English queries, date, publisher, page reached, and result for each requested source class. Search outcome is therefore not marked as exhaustive.

Direct opens of many gov.cy pages returned 403 in the research client. Official search results and snippets were inspected where available; affected records remain search-result-only until independently opened. The Government Printing Office page did open and states that its material is available in Greek only. No documented public API or registration route was found in the bounded search; absence of a located API is not proof that none exists.

The visa hub and the January 2026 Schengen status PDF contain an unresolved inconsistency about travel in the Schengen area using Cyprus residence documents. The status of each card type and relevant sponsor relationship must be resolved against current EU primary law and current Cyprus official material.

No applicant forms, completed complaints, case records, or personal submissions were collected. No candidate is accepted.
