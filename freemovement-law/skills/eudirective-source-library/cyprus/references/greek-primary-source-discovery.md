# Cyprus Greek primary-source discovery

**Checked:** 19 October 2026  
**Scope:** Greek Gazette texts for Law 7(I)/2007 and its identified amendments.

Prefer a complete government-published consolidation. If a current Greek consolidation is not usable, the closest government-published text may be used within its stated scope and cutoff; record its publisher label, language, date and uncertainty. The OLC English consolidation is listed through Laws 2007–2023, so it can support directly reviewed passages within that cutoff but does not establish current Greek wording in 2026. Do not reconstruct the law from every Gazette fragment by default. Check a particular Gazette act only when it may resolve a material post-cutoff change, commencement issue, correction or repeal. Browser-indexed PDF text is discovery evidence until the relevant text itself is reviewed; storing raw bytes is not required for ordinary reference use.

## Greek consolidation and currentness

- The [OLC English translations and consolidations index](https://www.gov.cy/officelawcommissioner/en/documents/competences-of-the-law-commissioner/translations-consolidations/) lists the English consolidation of Law 7(I)/2007 through 2023. An [official GPO notice in issue 5503, 10 July 2024](https://www.mof.gov.cy/mof/gpo/gazette.nsf/521965EB7068C7C4C2258B56001D1F86/%24file/5503%2010%207%202024%20KYRIO%20MEROS%20TMIMA%20B.pdf) identifies the new OLC publication as GEN(A)-L.185. Search indexing exposes text from the linked PDF, but direct PDF retrieval returned HTTP 500 in this review, so no raw PDF bytes or hash were recorded. The OLC consolidation is a government-published English working text and may be used within its stated scope and cutoff; it does not establish Greek wording or changes after that cutoff.
- The [Migration Department legislation index](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/documents/nomothesia/) says its linked consolidations are unofficial and lists Law 7(I)/2007. Its indexed page is not retrievable from this research environment.
- No usable current Greek consolidation of Law 7(I)/2007 has been recorded. For ordinary research, use the closest government-published version available, including the OLC English consolidation through 2023, and state its date/cutoff and limits. Treat Greek-current-wording as a specific open issue only when the question depends on the Greek wording or a known post-cutoff change. Review an individual Gazette text only to resolve that material issue; do not require capture or reconciliation of the full amendment chain.
- The direct official 193(I)/2020 PDF was readable through browser extraction, while local TLS retrieval failed because the host could not be resolved in this environment. The 2013 January–June volume route for 8(I)/2013 was discovered through the official archive index, but browser open returned a cache miss and local retrieval remains unavailable. No publisher restriction was bypassed and no response bytes were saved.

## Acceptance consequence

Official routes and issue metadata are located for all seven amendments, through either act-specific PDFs or official half-year Gazette volumes. Those routes are supplemental leads, not a requirement to build a full chain. A human-reviewed official reference can support the content and version actually checked, with its cutoff and limits. Local capture, hashes, replay and passage-by-passage acceptance apply only when retaining a local artifact. The 2007 facsimile remains a historical promulgation; uncertainty about Greek wording beyond the government-published cutoff is a gap only when it affects the research question.
