# Cyprus source captures

Exact official response bytes are stored under `raw/` and referenced by repository-relative paths. Derived, source-specific transformation output is under `derived/`; it is not a publisher response. For Law 7(I)/2007, the original scanned Gazette PDF remains in `raw/`, the Adobe OCR PDF is saved in `derived/`, and the unaccepted text candidate is replayed with the pinned converter. Historical N-Lex and Supreme Court index responses are listed in `references/cyprus-capture-results.json` as `historical_captures`; they are retained for comparison but are not the current manifest version. The prior committed N-Lex candidate and pending conversions were reconciled to the saved responses: captured-source text is identical, and the current candidate matches the latest pending conversion byte-for-byte. The ignored `temp/cyprus/` copies were verified duplicates and removed. For dependency setup and exact replay commands, see the [Cyprus README](../README.md). Do not regenerate a prior response from a changed live page.

Most files are research review captures. The original Greek Gazette PDF for the 2007 promulgation of Law 7(I)/2007 is accepted only as a historical facsimile; its OCR transcription remains unaccepted. Greek Gazette text is authentic; English OLC files are translations/consolidations with cut-off dates; CyLaw is secondary. Reuse and redistribution terms have not been established, so do not treat repository presence as permission to republish. No completed application or personal submission is stored.
