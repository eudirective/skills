## Accepted scope

Official explanatory page for the Data Protection Office complaint route; not legislation or a complete remedy analysis.

## Review note

The page describes complaints under GDPR Article 77 and the Liechtenstein Data Protection Act, identifies the Datenschutzstelle as the national authority, recommends written submission and links an electronic form and secure document channel. It says the authority updates complainants on status and outcome; the page also explains that abusive complaints may lead to a fee or refusal to act.
