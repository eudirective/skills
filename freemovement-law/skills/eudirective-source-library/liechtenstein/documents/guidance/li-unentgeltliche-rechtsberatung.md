## Accepted scope

Official court-service reference on limited free oral legal advice and filing assistance.

## Review note

The court says legal advice is offered orally in German in simple cases, with advance telephone registration, and only in matters within court jurisdiction. It expressly excludes administrative-law advice, written opinions, email advice, and representation; procedural assistance is a separate legal route.
