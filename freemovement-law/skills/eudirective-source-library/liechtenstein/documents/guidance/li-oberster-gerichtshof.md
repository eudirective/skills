## Accepted scope

Official institutional reference for the OGH role and the linked decision-publication routes.

## Review note

The court states that the OGH exercises ordinary jurisdiction at third instance and also functions as Verwaltungsgerichtshof. It links publication to gerichtsentscheidungen.li and separately links OGH decisions published on ogh.li for 2020–2025.
