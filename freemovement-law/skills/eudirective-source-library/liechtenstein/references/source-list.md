# Liechtenstein official-source discovery register

**Research date:** 19 October 2026. **Jurisdiction:** Liechtenstein, an EEA EFTA State. **Status:** 40 candidates, 0 accepted; a direct retrieval and conversion rule now saves accessible HTML as review candidates and preserves official PDFs without extraction. The comparison seed is the Danish free-movement residence source set. Prior country findings were carried forward as discovery and adapter-design leads, not as evidence of Liechtenstein law.

Searches used official Liechtenstein, EFTA, court, and Data Protection Office channels, primarily in German, with selected official English pages inspected. The search covers national law and EEA incorporation; residence/family routes; visa and consular procedure; administrative procedure and remedies; forms; court and oversight sources; privacy/public access; and family-status evidence. Each row separates functional relationship, verification, and retrieval status. `Candidate found` means a first-party lead was identified; it does not mean the full source was retrieved or accepted.

## Jurisdiction and route distinctions

Liechtenstein's EEA participation does not make its residence regime interchangeable with Denmark's or a standard EU/EEA residence system. The official EFTA Joint Committee Decision 191/1999 recognizes a Liechtenstein-specific solution for free movement and residence. The PFZG is the national source for EEA and Swiss nationals and their specified family categories, while the AuG/ZAV govern distinct third-country routes. Switzerland-related rules must be traced to their own treaty and bilateral cooperation basis. Family of a Liechtenstein national, EEA/Swiss sponsors, third-country residents, discretionary partner permits, and asylum cases should not be silently combined.

The official `Gesetze.li` consolidated collection is useful for discovery and version history, but its own notice says consolidated texts are informational and have no legal effect. Liechtenstein says the signed electronic State Law Gazette PDF/A has been the authentic publication since 1 January 2013. A future rule should therefore use the consolidated database to locate and compare text, then verify identity, dates, and amendments against the signed Gazette. It must also inspect current reuse conditions and not infer that an Open Data law, API reference, or free download authorizes every automated use.

## Search evidence and limits

Official German-language queries included `Personenfreizügigkeitsgesetz PFZG`, `Personenfreizügigkeitsverordnung PFZV`, `Ausländergesetz AuG`, `Zulassung Aufenthalt Ausländer ZAV`, `Landesverwaltungspflegegesetz LVG Beschwerde`, `EWR Register`, `Familiennachzug EWR`, `Visum Schweizer Vertretung`, `Datenschutzstelle Beschwerderecht`, `Informationsgesetz`, and `Anerkennung Zivilstandsereignisse`. Selected English official pages were checked for EEA/Swiss family guidance, third-country family guidance, consular route, and foreign civil-status recognition. Official EFTA material was used to verify the EEA and Swiss treaty distinctions.

The search is bounded rather than exhaustive: no complete case-law corpus, every EEA incorporation, every consular post, every e-form, or every amendment was reviewed. The direct capture used ordinary HTTPS GET and did not require registration for the accessible pages. It does not establish API availability, bulk-reuse rights, or permission for recurring automated retention. Search coverage does not establish that unlisted sources do not exist.

## Capture results

The refresh captured and converted 16 HTML pages and two PDFs to Markdown; the original PDFs remain preserved byte-for-byte. Sixteen candidate routes returned HTTP 403. Four staging-only routes were not fetched. One form was captured only at its initial public step, and the decisions portal returned only a JavaScript application shell. No individual judgment was retrieved. All 40 records remain unaccepted.
