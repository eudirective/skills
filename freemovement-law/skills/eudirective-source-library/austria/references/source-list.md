## Seed and comparison profile

The seed is Denmark's *EU-opholdsbekendtgørelsen* (BEK nr. 1457 af 06/10/2020), an official regulation specifically about residence in Denmark for people covered by EU free-movement rules. The existing Danish pack links the official Retsinformation record and PDF. Sweden's *Utlänningslag* and *Utlänningsförordning* were prior partial matches, with agency guidance as a separate source class. The previous Finland findings were provisional because direct inspection of Finlex and Migri was blocked.

Austria's selected NAG §§ 51–57 provisions are a partial functional match: the official RIS records contain an EEA/free-movement cluster within a wider settlement and residence statute, including registration and permanent-residence certificates and a Swiss extension. They are not a one-to-one counterpart to Denmark's dedicated regulation. The official Austrian portal separately explains third-country family members' residence-card route and explicitly includes Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, and Switzerland in its scope.

## Official case-law discovery routes

The sample decisions are leads, not a complete case-law inventory. RIS exposes separate first-party search applications for the Landesverwaltungsgerichte (LVwG), Bundesverwaltungsgericht (BVwG), and Verwaltungsgerichtshof (VwGH). The official [RIS information page](https://ris.bka.gv.at/UI/Info.aspx) describes their different coverage: LVwG contains selected decisions from 2014, BVwG decisions from 2014, and nearly all VwGH decisions from 1990. The [LVwG search form](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Lvwg) and [official handbook](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/RisInfo/HandbuchLvwg.pdf) document search by text, date, recent RIS additions/changes, state, decision type, case number, and norm; an unselected state searches across all nine LVwGs. The [BVwG search form](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Bvwg) and [application information](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/UI/Judikatur/Bvwg/Kontakt.aspx) expose its RS/TE records and current search surface. The [VwGH search form](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Vwgh) and [search help](https://ris.bka.gv.at/UI/Help.aspx?Abfrage=Vwgh&Field=SuchworteField) expose decision text, date, new-in-RIS, case number, and norm fields. These are official discovery channels; the LVwG collection is selective, and none of these pages establishes complete coverage of unpublished decisions.

For a repeatable review, keep LVwG, BVwG, and VwGH searches separate; search both Rechtssätze (RS) and Entscheidungstexte (TE) where available; record German search terms, norms, dates, filters, result-page boundaries, and observed document identifiers. A no-result query is only a bounded search outcome. No Austria-specific recurring query or result-change monitor has yet been implemented.

## Source-rule sustainability evidence

The current [RIS Open Government Data FAQ](https://ris.bka.gv.at/RisInfo/OGD-FAQ.pdf), created 7 September 2026, says no special registration is required, data use carries CC BY 4.0 attribution, and the service makes no guarantee of accuracy, currency, completeness, or continuous availability. RIS offers no direct ZIP, snapshot, archive, or dump download; larger initial data retrieval is directed to the OGD interfaces. For OGD use beyond a single or small amount comparable to manual website use, the FAQ requires advance notice to the RIS service address with the application purpose, User-Agent, source IP, planned timing, and other relevant technical details. Requests must not exceed 0.5 per second, must not run in parallel, and must be made only 20:00–05:00 Austria time or on weekends/Austrian public holidays. The FAQ warns that noncompliance can result in IP blocking. For a small selection of laws where daily currentness is unnecessary, it recommends manual download through the website. Its history query is intended to detect additions, changes, or deletions without repeatedly downloading a full collection.

For case law, RIS provides distinct official [LVwG](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Lvwg), [BVwG](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Bvwg), and [VwGH](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Vwgh) search applications. The [LVwG handbook](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/RisInfo/HandbuchLvwg.pdf) documents text, norm, date, new/changed, state, decision type, and case-number filters; leaving the state empty searches all nine courts. RIS states that this collection includes only selected decisions from 2014, while BVwG decisions are available from 2014 and nearly all VwGH decisions from 1990 ([RIS application coverage](https://ris.bka.gv.at/UI/Info.aspx)). Separate queries across the official systems can therefore support bounded discovery, but search results cannot establish completeness, and no Austria-specific repeating query or result-state comparison has been implemented.

The preferred retrieval candidate is a small, review-only check of the public RIS current-section pages using stable locators (`Gesetzesnummer` + paragraph). The official RIS handbook documents that route, and the current § 53 page demonstrates version/date fields, but the exact raw HTML and recurring automated access behavior have not been captured. Do not pin a version-specific ELI as the only locator. A changed current section should create a review candidate, while the last accepted text remains intact. If OGD APIs are used for history/discovery, their no-registration, attribution, rate, timing, and no-bulk requirements must be enforced; they are not needed as a credentialed API. Conversion remains blocked on raw-response inspection because the research reader exposed parallel standard/expanded paragraph text.

For public guidance, pin the authority page identifier and exact heading, capture the visible page-update date and linked forms, and maintain an official topic-index/search fallback if the page moves. Hash-only alerting is not sufficient for DSB or Ombudsman HTML because navigation, forms, and page chrome can change without an operative guidance change.
