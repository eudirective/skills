# Austria coverage register

Checked: 19 October 2026. This register distinguishes captured text from unfinished source discovery and analysis. It does not certify legal completeness.

## Why the listed gaps remained

The Austria pack was built as a bounded, one-time discovery and capture exercise. It was not built as an exhaustive authority-by-authority inventory or as a scheduled monitoring service. The initial pack was then described as “complete” when only its declared 37-record inventory had been captured. That wording was too broad: the capture set was complete; Austria coverage and ongoing maintenance were not.

Some work was also deferred because it is location-specific or open-ended. Mission instructions vary by the applicant's place of residence and consular district. Local residence procedures are distributed among federal, state, district and city authorities. Austrian case-law searches return a changing set of records across several courts, so five selected cases cannot establish broad coverage. These are reasons to define a repeatable discovery scope, not reasons to omit the gaps from the completion report.

## NAG §§ 54–55: what is captured and what is not

The current candidate texts for [NAG § 54](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20004242&Paragraf=54) and [NAG § 55](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20004242&Paragraf=55) include all paragraphs in those sections. § 54 includes the exceptional refusal rule in subsection 7; § 55 includes the notice/BFA referral, outcome, title, and discontinuance rules in subsections 3–6. The gap is therefore not missing subsection text. It is a reviewed procedural map that connects these rules to the relevant application, authority, remedy, deadline, and case-law routes.

The next procedure review must cross-check, at minimum, the already captured NAG §§ 51–55, 57; AVG §§ 17 and 73; VwGVG §§ 7–8; and the official current [BFA-VG § 9](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20007944&Paragraf=9) and [BFA-VG § 10](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20007944&Paragraf=10). The procedural map must distinguish a § 54(7) refusal from the § 55(3) route and must not imply that every residence-card dispute follows the same remedy path.

## Scheduled updater

The offline `scripts/convert_ris_capture.py` alone did not retrieve or monitor anything; manual cadence text had been mistaken for a schedule. A fixed-manifest network monitor and monthly GitHub Actions workflow have now been added in `sync_austria_sources.py` and `.github/workflows/austria-source-monitor.yml`. They use ordinary serial HTTPS, allowlisted official hosts, identity checks, and stage changed bytes/text for PR review. They do not call the RIS OGD/API, accept a source, or replace current candidate text automatically. The workflow is not active until it is merged to the repository default branch, and the repository must permit `GITHUB_TOKEN` to create pull requests. It checks only the fixed manifest; discovering new mission pages, broader cases, and Land practice is still not automated.
