IMPORTANT
You will find important information (e.g. list of documents to be submitted) in the information sheet. Therefore, please read it! It is available at https://www.bmi.gv.at/312_EN/60a/start.aspx. Please note the official language of Austria is German. The authority will do its best to communicate in English but for legally binding declarations you will have to use the German language.
C. Closing statement
1. I have entered all the information correctly. I have not intentionally misrepresented anything. I am aware that all relevant documents must be presented in person at the time of application. I have provided all the relevant documents I have. If the authority requests translations, I have to provide them in German. If a document is not in German or English, it is recommended to have my documents translated before applying. Then the authority can work faster.
2. I have to provide my address. The authority sends all letters to this address. If this address changes, I have to report this to the authority immediately. For example when I move. If the authority is repeatedly unable to deliver a letter to me, it will discontinue my application. Then I will not get a residence permit! I can also specify an authorized recipient. The authority then sends my letters to this person. What an authorized recipient is can be found on page 2 under authorized representative. If any of the information on this form changes, I must report this to the authority immediately! I have to do this in writing. I also have to send all relevant documents.
3. If I provide wrong information or if I don‘t provide all relevant information, it‘s possible that I will not get a residence permit! It is also possible that the authority will not extend my residence permit. This can also happen if I do not follow points 1 to 5 of this closing statement. status: 08/2026

4. I am not allowed to marry anyone just to get a residence permit! I am not allowed to enter into a registered partnership with anyone just to get a residence permit! I am not allowed to arrange a marriage or registered partnership for anyone just so that they can get a residence permit.I am not allowed to adopt anyone just to get a residence permit. No one can adopt me just to get a residence permit. I am not allowed to arrange an adoption just so that someone gets a residence permit.These marriages, registered partnerships and adoptions are punishable by law! That means I will be charged. I may have to pay a fine or go to jail. It is also punishable if I give false information in order to get a residence permit!
5. If I commit an administrative offense, I have to pay a fine of at least 50 Euros and up to 250 Euros.
– I have to immediately inform the authority if the purpose of my stay changes. I am only allowed to do what is related to the purpose of my stay.
– I have to immediately report to the authority if someone has stolen my residence permit, if I have lost it or if the residence permit has become damaged. I also have to report immediately if my data changes. For example, if I get married and have a different name.
– If my family circumstances change, I have to report this to the authority after one month at the latest. For example, if I get married or get divorced. page 3 of 6
I am committing an administrative offense if I do not comply with the statements in point 5.
6. Possible refund of the fee:
If you have applied for the issuance of a registration certificate, a certificate of permanent residence, a residence card or a permanent residence card, the authority must collect a fee when you submit the application.
If the requested document is not issued, you can request a refund. To do this, you must send a refund request by post to the Finanzamt Österreich – Dienststelle Sonderzuständigkeiten, Postfach 222, 1000 Wien.
The application must include the following: documents showing that you paid the fee, the reference number and the date of your original application, the authority that collected the money, your bank details, your name, your address, and your signature.
ATTENTION: If you applied for a photo identity card for EEA nationals and it is not issued, a refund of the fee is not possible. status: 08/2026 documentations

In accordance with Article 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we would like to inform you that the personal data you have provided will be processed electronically. You cannot object to this processing. Nor can you request a restriction. This is stated in section 34 Settlement and Residence Act (NAG).
