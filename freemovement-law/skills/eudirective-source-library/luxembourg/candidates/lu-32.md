In this section you may find all english forms, set at your disposal by the national commission.

At the moment, the English version is limited.

Please refer to the French or German version.

COMPLAINT FORM

Link to complaint form

The aim of this online complaints form is to facilitate both the submission of a complaint by a data subject as well as the handling thereof by the CNPD.

The use of this online form allows the CNPD to process your complaint more swiftly, as an electronic copy of the completed form is automatically sent to the CNPD.

DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION FORM

Data Breach Notification Form (English version)

Data controllers must notify the CNPD of any personal data breaches within 72 hours of becoming aware of them, if the breach is likely to pose a risk to the rights and freedoms of the individuals concerned (See the section on data breaches for more information).

The breach notification can be sent to the email address databreach@cnpd.lu. You may use the clé publique gpg to secure the transmission of information by encrypting it.

DATA PROTECTION OFFICER (DPO)

Form: Declaration of the DPO

Data controllers and processors must provide the CNPD with the contact details of the Data Protection Officer (DPO) they have designated, where applicable (see the DPO section for more information).

The duly completed and signed form must be sent to declarationDPO@cnpd.lu.
