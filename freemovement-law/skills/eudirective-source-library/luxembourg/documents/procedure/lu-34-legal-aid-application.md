## What this source supports

Guichet states that natural persons may request full or partial legal-fee assistance subject to conditions, and links application forms hosted by the Bar. It describes Bar-association involvement and the 2023 legal-aid law.

## Scope and limits

General legal-aid route and official forms locator only. Eligibility, scope, means assessment and coverage depend on the current law and proceeding.

The guidance and linked forms have separate dates. Verify the form and legal-aid decision-maker with the relevant Bar before use.
