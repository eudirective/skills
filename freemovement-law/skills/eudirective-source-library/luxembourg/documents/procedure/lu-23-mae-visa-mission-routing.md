## What this source supports

The Ministry says applications go to the mission competent for the applicant’s residence. Where Luxembourg is not represented, another Schengen state may represent it for short stays; long-stay Visa D applications are handled by Luxembourg or Belgian posts. External providers and mission-specific instructions vary. The official mission directory is regularly updated.

## Scope and limits

Use these pages to identify the competent mission and then follow its current instructions. The directory establishes contact and accreditation routes, not an appointment, local checklist, service-provider role or complete mission practice.
