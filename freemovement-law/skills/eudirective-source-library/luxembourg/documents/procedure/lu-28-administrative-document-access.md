## What this source supports

Guichet outlines access requests via MyGuichet or a written request, identifies categories of public bodies and exceptions, and describes ordinary response periods. This is an access-to-documents route.

## Scope and limits

Access to administrative documents under the relevant access rules. Access rights and permission to re-use or republish a document are separate questions.

The page is dated 2021. Do not rely on it as a statement of re-use licensing under the separate 2021 open-data law. For specific requests, check the 2018 access law and current procedure.
