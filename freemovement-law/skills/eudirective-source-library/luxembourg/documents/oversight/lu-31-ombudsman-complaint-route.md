## What this source supports

The published complaint instructions direct a person first to approach the administration for an explanation, clarification or challenge. The page states that a complaint may follow if there is no answer within one month or the answer is unsatisfactory, and warns that Ombudsman complaints do not suspend administrative or judicial appeal deadlines.
