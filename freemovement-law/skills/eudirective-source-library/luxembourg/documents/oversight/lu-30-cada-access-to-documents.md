## What this source supports

CADA is the independent consultative commission created under the 2018 open-administration law. Its published role includes monitoring access rules, advising covered bodies and issuing opinions; its opinions are not binding.

## Scope and limits

Oversight of access to administrative documents only, within the statutory remit.

An advisory opinion is not a court judgment or a binding decision. Do not conflate CADA with immigration merits review or CNPD data-rights complaints.
