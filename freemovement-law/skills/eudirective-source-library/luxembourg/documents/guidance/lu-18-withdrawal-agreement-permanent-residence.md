## What this source supports

The official page describes a permanent-residence document route after five years of legal, uninterrupted Luxembourg residence for UK nationals and family members who are Withdrawal Agreement beneficiaries.

## Scope and limits

Withdrawal Agreement permanent residence only; retain the distinct legal basis and beneficiary eligibility limits.

Verify individual beneficiary status and current forms with the competent authority. This is not a current EU-family route.
