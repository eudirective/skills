## What this source supports

The official overview covers third-country family members of EU, EEA, Swiss and Luxembourg citizens, including spouses, registered partners, certain descendants and dependent ascendants, other dependent/household members, and durable partners. It links residence procedures, forms and municipal contacts. It also includes a changing commune directory.

## Scope and limits

Use as an official route and overview for the published family categories and listed procedures. Treat every linked checklist, form, commune contact and submission route as a separate, date-sensitive record; this note does not establish substantive legal eligibility.

Candidate IDs LU-12 and LU-41 resolve to the same current Guichet family page and are represented by one document to avoid duplicate source records. No static copy of the municipal directory or application forms is retained.
