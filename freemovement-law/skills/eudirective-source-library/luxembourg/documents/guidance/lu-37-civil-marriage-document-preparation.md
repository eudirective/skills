## What this source supports

Guichet describes preparation of a Luxembourg civil-marriage file and states that required steps/documents can vary with nationality and local commune. It notes a residence connection for a couple marrying in Luxembourg.

## Scope and limits

Civil-status document-acquisition context only. It does not determine residence rights or recognition of a marriage for immigration purposes.

Municipal practices and document lists can vary. Verify directly with the competent commune and check translation/authentication requirements separately.
