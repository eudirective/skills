## What this source supports

The Ministry publishes the nationality-based list of third countries whose citizens are exempt from the short-stay visa requirement. Consult the current live attachment rather than treating the archived candidate PDF as current.

## Scope and limits

Short-stay nationality visa-exemption navigation only. Visa exemption is not a residence permit and does not displace EU-family rights.

The PDF is mutable and undated. Recheck it at the point of use and assess the applicant’s passport, nationality, travel purpose and any family-member status separately.
