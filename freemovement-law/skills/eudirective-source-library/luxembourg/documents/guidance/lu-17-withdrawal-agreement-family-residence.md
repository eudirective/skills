## What this source supports

The Guichet page distinguishes UK nationals and family members who qualify as Withdrawal Agreement beneficiaries from current free-movement applicants and describes the residence-document application route.

## Scope and limits

Withdrawal Agreement beneficiaries only. Eligibility depends on the Agreement’s personal and temporal scope; this record does not determine an individual’s status.

The linked form has its own date and must be checked separately. Do not apply this route to all UK nationals or their family members.
