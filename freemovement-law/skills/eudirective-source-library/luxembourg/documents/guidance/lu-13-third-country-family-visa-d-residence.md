## What this source supports

For a third-country national joining a third-country sponsor for a stay over 90 days, current Guichet guidance describes a two-step route: apply for Visa D before entry through the competent diplomatic or consular channel, then after arrival make a commune arrival declaration, complete a medical check and apply for the family-member residence permit before the visa expires. This is separate from EU-family residence.
