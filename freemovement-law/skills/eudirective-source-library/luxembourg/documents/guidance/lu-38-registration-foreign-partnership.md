## What this source supports

Guichet describes applying to enter a partnership formed abroad into Luxembourg’s civil register and explains that registration is not mandatory. It provides an administrative evidence route, not an automatic immigration classification.

## Scope and limits

Civil-register procedure for an existing foreign partnership only.

Do not infer that registration alone establishes eligibility as a family member under free-movement law or national immigration rules. Check current evidence and legal categories separately.
