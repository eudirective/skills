## What this source supports

The Guichet page describes an application for permanent residence documentation after five years of lawful, uninterrupted residence for EU/assimilated citizens and family members who are themselves EU/assimilated nationals. A separate page governs third-country family members.

## Scope and limits

Official administrative guidance about the listed permanent-residence certificate route; not a substitute for Directive 2004/38 or national legislation.

Use the separate LU-16 record for third-country family members. Forms and municipal steps may change independently.
