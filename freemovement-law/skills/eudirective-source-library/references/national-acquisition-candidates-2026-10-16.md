# National acquisition candidates: Portugal, Spain, France, Netherlands, Belgium and Italy (2026-10-16)

**Status:** discovery register only. These are candidate official sources for acquisition and review; none is represented here as downloaded, converted, trust-promoted, or legally accepted in the public library.

**Scope:** applies the existing 2026-10-15 acquisition taxonomy to six additional EU Member States. The candidates prioritize EU-citizen family entry and residence, civil-status and foreign-document registration/recognition, international adoption and parent-child status, and official operational channels. Shared EU instruments stay in the EU core and are not repeated here.

**Inventory comparison:** the public source-library top-level inventory has no Portugal, Spain, France, Netherlands, Belgium, or Italy national pack. Candidate list is new relative to the 2026-10-15 register. V means the official publisher and source identity were identified online; it does not prove that bytes have been retrieved or that a consolidated text is current. I means the precise official version or its access path still needs verification.

**Completeness review (2026-10-16):** the first pass lacked several operational filing routes and review procedures. The additions below cover those gaps where official sources were identified. This remains a scoped acquisition queue, not complete coverage of every taxonomy category or every national, regional, and local procedure. Belgium’s English Immigration Office overview itself labels the detailed family guidance as “work in progress”; French- or Dutch-language legislation and service pages need review.

## Acquisition handling

- Retrieve legal texts from the official national publisher, preserving the promulgated text and any separate official consolidated view; record the effective-date/version used.
- Treat official administrative pages as operational evidence, not substitutes for legislation or appeal rules.
- Resolve every I item’s exact publisher record, currentness and scope before retrieval; do not let one candidate stand in for an entire taxonomy category.
- Add accepted country material only in its country pack with source identity, retrieval metadata, conversion review and explicit acceptance status.
- Shared EU materials remain in the EU core; check for duplicate source identities before adding language or national representations.
