## Search coverage and limits

Searches initially dated 18 October 2026 and retrieval checks dated 19 October 2026 used official channels including Lovdata and data.norge.no; EFTA and EEA legal-text pages; UDI and UDI Regelverk; Police; UNE; Sivilombudet; Statsforvalteren; regjeringen.no and Stortinget; Norway.no; the Courts of Norway; and the national Data Protection Authority. Norwegian queries included `utlendingsloven`, `utlendingsforskriften`, `EØS-borger`, `EØS-regelverket`, `oppholdskort`, `familieinnvandring`, `varig oppholdsrett`, `klage på vedtak`, `fri rettshjelp`, `postjournal`, `personvern`, and `apostille`, alongside English queries for UDI/UNE pages.

- **Legislation and publisher/reuse:** Lovdata’s live API list and OpenAPI specification were fetched without credentials. Current laws, central regulations, and current-year Avdeling I archives were retrieved through the documented publicData routes; systematic Lovdata website extraction was avoided. The API list supplies package sizes and modification times. Selected XHTML records preserve publisher identifiers, effective dates, amendments and document structure.
- **EEA and EFTA legal route:** EFTA's official pages identify the EEA EFTA States and updated EEA legal texts, and separately describe the revised EFTA Convention's movement-of-persons rules between Switzerland and the EEA EFTA States. The actual national implementation, current annex/adaptation, and Swiss family details remain record-specific checks.
- **Migration authority and forms:** UDI publishes a dynamic EEA hub, FAQs, family card routes, checklists, EEA Rulebook documents and ordinary family routes. Country selectors, parent-child links, personalized checklists and mission pages vary; a bounded search did not enumerate every nationality/family category or overseas post.
- **Appeals and courts:** UNE publishes EEA case explanations and a representative selected practice base; Sivilombudet has an immigration-statement archive; the Supreme Court has a public ruling index. UNE selection and case-law publication do not provide a complete lower-court or administrative-decision corpus.
- **Access, privacy, legal aid, civil status:** central guidance and oversight routes were identified. Individual file/data request systems and legal-aid eligibility are personal or procedural services and are not acquisition targets. County-level apostille instructions and consular document practice require location-specific discovery.

A failed direct request is not proof that the publisher is unavailable. Twenty-three candidates remain unconverted after HTTP 403/404 or network failure, one Avdeling II route has no package in the documented public API list, and eInnsyn returned a shell without extractable body text. No Norway source is accepted, and no individual, family or immigration-case data were collected.
