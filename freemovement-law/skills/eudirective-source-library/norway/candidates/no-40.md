Grand Board decisions

In Grand Board hearings, three board chairs and four board members decide the case. The decisions create a precedent for the UDI and UNE in subsequent cases. The precedent of the decisions applies until there is a change through subsequent legislation, legal or administrative practice.

The types of cases that can be decided by a Grand Board are regulated by the Immigration Regulations (external link). They include:

Cases of fundamental significance

Cases with substantial social or economic consequences

Cases in areas where practice tends to vary

Cases where the board chair considers a board decision to be unlawful in the appellant’s disfavour
