## Accepted scope

## Review evidence

- Language: en
- Version or displayed date: Live official page; no revision date exposed.
- Inspected: The FAC says substantive judgments are published, procedural decisions are published only if of general public interest, reference judgments do not cover every field, and the page links the FAC judgments database.
- Currentness and scope limit: The Court does not guarantee completeness and accuracy of internet information. The database contains all published decisions, which is not the same as all decisions made.
