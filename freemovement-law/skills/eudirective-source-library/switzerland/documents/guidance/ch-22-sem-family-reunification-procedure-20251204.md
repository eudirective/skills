## Accepted scope

Official SEM procedural directive for specified third-country family-reunification routes under the AIG and Asylum Act. It identifies the handoff between Swiss representations, cantonal migration authorities, and SEM.

## Review evidence

- Language: de; official French parallel version also available
- Version or displayed date: SEM Directive No. 322.3-12 dated 2025-12-04; its official parent index was last modified 2025-12-12 and currently links the directive and a separate country list.
- Inspected: The 18-page directive is addressed to Swiss foreign representations and cantonal migration authorities. For family reunification under AIG Articles 42 ff., the representation conducts preliminary review and forwards the application; the canton examines and decides, with SEM approval reserved in some cases. It separately describes routes for temporarily admitted persons and refugees and states that AFMP family reunification is outside its scope.
- Currentness and scope limit: Use the parent index to resolve the current PDF and its separately linked country list. This is official operational guidance, not the statutory basis. Do not apply it to AFMP/EU-EFTA family routes or infer all 26 cantonal requirements from it.
