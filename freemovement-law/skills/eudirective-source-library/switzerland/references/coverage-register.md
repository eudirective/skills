| Issue area | What is established | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| Route and jurisdiction boundary | Switzerland is an EFTA state but not an EEA contracting party. Official sources identify the AFMP route and AIG Article 2's treaty relationship. | Review the AFMP treaty text, annexes and protocols; the EFTA Convention; family of Swiss nationals under AIG; ordinary third-country, UK acquired-rights and other distinct routes. Do not infer one route from the label “family reunification.” |
| Core federal legislation | Dated official references are available for AIG (German, 15 October 2023), VEV (German, displayed 12 June 2026 and marked in force), OLCP/VFP (French, 1 January 2026), and VwVG (German, 1 July 2022). | Check the responsible Fedlex portal for the current relevant version and scope of AIG, OASA/VZAE, VEV, VFP, AFMP, EFTA Convention, court statutes, access and data-protection law, and civil-status rules. The dated references do not establish the whole relevant statutory framework. |
| EU/EFTA residence administration | SEM says cantons issue permits and points applicants to the competent cantonal authority. SEM maintains an authority directory. | Map the relevant procedures, competent office, forms, evidence, appointments, fees, filing channels and remedies across all 26 cantons. The directory is a discovery route, not the completed process map. |
| Swiss-national and ordinary third-country family routes | The AIG is an identified primary framework, and SEM maintains family and residence guidance candidates. | Map statutory eligibility and procedure by sponsor/status category, including Swiss citizens, EU/EFTA sponsors, third-country residents, refugees/provisionally admitted persons, and UK acquired-rights cases. Review current AIG/OASA provisions and relevant SEM directives. |
| Entry, visas and refusal remedies | VEV has an official dated reference. SEM describes a distinct 30-day, written and reasoned objection process for visa refusals. | Separate visa-free entry, Schengen short-stay, national visa, residence authorisation and residence-card routes. Verify the current legal basis, fees, refusal notice instructions, and mission-specific appointments/checklists. Do not apply the visa objection to cantonal residence decisions. |
| Consular operations | FDFA provides a Swiss representation directory; Istanbul is one candidate example. | Review relevant posts for local competence, appointments, providers, fees, evidence checklists, submission channels and language versions. One mission is not a national procedure. |
| Federal and cantonal procedure and remedies | VwVG is an official dated reference for federal administrative authorities, including refusal/delay and appeal provisions within its scope. SEM routes EU/EFTA applicants to cantonal authorities. | Identify current remedies for each decision type and authority, including cantonal administrative procedure, appeal periods, court competence, interim relief and onward review. The 2022 federal statute does not establish the 26 cantonal procedures. |
| Case-law discovery | FAC provides an official searchable published-decisions route and explains the limits of its publication corpus. | Search the FAC, Federal Supreme Court and relevant cantonal courts by issue and language. Record holdings, outcomes, procedural posture and applicability. A search of published FAC decisions cannot establish exhaustive case-law coverage. |
| Bilateral III dependencies | The Swiss Parliament docket 26.023 was updated on 19 October 2026 and reported several drafts under deliberation. It is a repeatable status-monitoring route. | Recheck the official treaty and legislation records for parliamentary decisions, any referendum, implementing provisions and commencement. The docket is not itself operative law. |
| Family status, access, privacy and oversight | Candidate records identify FOJ civil-status material, federal access/privacy sources, FDPIC guidance and ombudsman information. | Verify current statutory requirements, authority mandates, complaint and access routes, and canton-level regulators/ombuds offices. Historical or federal-only information must not be generalized to cantonal bodies. |
| Source maintenance | Direct official links can be accepted within verified identity, language, date/version, legal status and topic scope. | If recurring monitoring is actually needed, define a simple route per source group and check version/date changes. API registration, full amendment-chain reconstruction, local capture and conversion are not prerequisites for human-reviewed link use. |

## Human review offers

Offer review of the whole official Fedlex portal for current consolidated texts and version interpretation; the complete SEM guidance/directives site for procedures and forms; the FDFA representations directory and relevant post sites for mission practice; the 26 cantonal authority sites linked from SEM; the FAC, Federal Supreme Court and cantonal court portals for decisions; the parliamentary docket and linked legislative stages for Bilateral III; and FOJ/FDPIC sites for family status and oversight. These offers identify places to resolve open questions; they do not imply that the sites are unreliable.
