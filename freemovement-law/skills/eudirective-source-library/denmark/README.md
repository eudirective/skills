## Immediate legal-source status

The official Retsinformation PDF printed 19 October 2026 marks BEK nr 1457/2020, the EU-Residence Order, as current and lists BEK nr 965/2025 as a later amendment. The amendment's official text states that it took effect on 1 July 2025. Read the base order and amendment together where the changed provisions matter.

The retained local XML conversion reports an end date of 2 July 2026, which conflicts with the official PDF status. That conflict remains open; the local conversion is not accepted as an integrated current text. The 2025 Aliens Act consolidation also has known later 2026 amendments and is not a current consolidated text.

The official July 2026 marriage and adoption consolidations were checked as PDFs after their ELI HTML pages returned JavaScript shells. The adoption consolidation states that § 30(3) is scheduled for repeal on 1 January 2027; preserve that future effective date when checking transition cases. These external links do not add local copies or close topic coverage.

The source inventory does not establish complete Danish coverage. Court and appeal discovery, mission practice, and review of the remaining legal and guidance references are outstanding.
