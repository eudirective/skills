# Denmark source-rule sustainability assessment

Skill: assess-source-rule-sustainability v0.2.0

Assessment date: 2026-10-18  
Inspected source: local source-runtime/eudirective-source-library-0.6.0/denmark/  
Rule versions after repairs: national ELI rule 0.2.3; shared guidance adapter 2.1; shared EU core retriever 1.0.1.

## Scope and evidence

The active configuration contains 16 Danish national legal records in pack-manifest.json, 14 Danish authority guidance records in guidance-manifest.json, and 23 selected EUR-Lex/CJEU IDs in core-source-ids.json. The separate discovery catalog now contains 41 records, including legal amendments, consular and administrative guidance, appeal decisions, and EU-law references. Catalog candidates are not active records and receive no sustainability verdict unless an implemented rule covers them.

Inspected the three manifests, Denmark's SKILL.md and pipeline contract, the national ELI updater, the shared guidance adapter, the selected-ID wrapper, and the shared EU core updater. The current workspace has no saved Denmark national/guidance documents or retrieval state, so offline replay over the existing collection could not be run. A previous live --check attempt failed before retrieval with workspace DNS EAI_AGAIN; this is an access limitation, not evidence of a Retsinformation outage.

Current official publisher evidence was checked through Retsinformation's official indexed records and official document text. Its own ELI documentation says that the Atom feed retains at least 60 days and the sitemap is refreshed at least monthly ([ELI channels](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/technicaldocumentation/eli-channels)); its ELI URI documentation describes stable document identifiers and XML/PDF/HTML formats ([ELI URI and format specification](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/technicaldocumentation)). Direct browser opens of some live ELI pages returned only the JavaScript application shell, so the following is official search-result or document evidence, not a complete live retrieval audit.

Retsinformation's official results show that LBK nr 1183/2025 has later amendments, including LOV nr 263/2026, 726/2026, 765/2026, and 766/2026. LOV nr 726/2026 itself identifies law 263/2026 among prior amendments; its title concerns implementation of parts of the EU migration pact ([LOV 726/2026](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/accn/A20260072630)). LOV nr 766/2026 amends the Aliens Act after law 726 ([LOV 766/2026](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/766)). LOV nr 765/2026 creates a new collective-agreement-based pay scheme ([LOV 765/2026](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/765)). BEK nr 798/2026 sets 28 September 2026 as the commencement date for § 1, no. 1 of law 1699/2024 ([BEK 798/2026](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/798)). A later student-residence regulation, BEK nr 829/2026, also appears in official results ([BEK 829/2026](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/829)).

The active 2020 EU-residence regulation was amended by BEK nr 965/2025, effective 1 July 2025 ([BEK 965/2025](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/965)). The catalog records the amendment separately; the fixed 2020 text must not be presented as the integrated current regulation. The bounded search did not identify a later consolidated Aliens Act, but that search result is not proof that no such consolidation exists.

## Summary

The Danish collection has distinct national, guidance, and EU/CJEU lanes, with stable ELI identifiers for national legislation and an exact selected-ID filter for the shared EU lane. The national and guidance rules are now better bounded: response-size limits, stricter text decoding, offline conversion replay, and candidate-promotion checks were added. The principal remaining weakness is discovery of amendments that do not repeat a tracked source's title. The catalog captured law 263/2026 through a targeted search; the present title matcher can miss cross-sector amendment acts like this. The national lane therefore remains fragile as a currentness-discovery rule even though retrieval of a registered ELI is strongly identified.

No single verdict is assigned to the pack as an average. The national rule is fragile; the guidance rule is fragile; the selected EU/CJEU lane is sustainable with limits. The missing local raw state prevents an end-to-end replay result for this assessment.

## Repairs made in this review

- National ELI rule version 0.2.3 now enforces 45-second timeouts and a 30 MB response cap for XML and legacy rendered HTML, rejects non-UTF-8 XML and malformed UTF-8 JSON, adds offline --verify, and requires a current complete successful sync report plus matching saved source/candidate hashes before acceptance. --ack-all now accepts only actual review candidates, not unchanged observations. Legacy conversion records the shared core converter version.
- The shared guidance adapter now caps all response bodies at 30 MB, keeps a 45-second timeout active through download, decodes declared text encodings strictly, rejects unsupported response types, and leaves new or changed sources pending until review. This changes shared code used by several packs and needs cross-pack validation before release.
- Shared EU core retrieval version 1.0.1 now keeps the request timeout active through response streaming and rejects response bodies over 50 MB. The converter version is exported so the Danish legacy-HTML conversion can include it in provenance.
- The Danish source catalog was refreshed to 41 records with relation leads for the 2026 acts and commencement order, the 2025 EU-residence amendment, and the student-residence regulation. These remain catalog candidates and have not been added to the active national manifest or accepted as current text.

## Repair priorities and remaining limits

1. Extend national discovery beyond title similarity. Parse and use official amendment relationships/identifiers from Retsinformation records, then reconcile the linked amendment chain for tracked laws. Include cross-sector acts such as LOV nr 263/2026; validate relation semantics against official XML or publisher documentation before relying on them.
2. Close long feed gaps with a documented historical reconciliation route. The official ELI feed retains at least 60 days, while the implemented harvest API replay is absent. Keep the existing gap flag and do not claim complete change coverage until missed days are reconciled.
3. Give every Danish HTML guidance record a tested expected heading or source-specific body selector, and fail closed rather than convert the whole page when the configured main content container disappears.
4. Run national, guidance, and selected core live checks from a runtime with official-site access, then run each offline verifier against saved responses. This workspace did not contain Danish raw snapshots or state, and its prior national live request failed at DNS resolution.
5. Review all catalog-only candidates—especially the 2026 amendments, commencement records, and EU-residence amendment—against the live official record and current integrated text before promoting any into active manifests or accepted source documents.
