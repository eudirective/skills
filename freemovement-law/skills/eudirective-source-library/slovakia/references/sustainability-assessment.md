# Slovakia source-rule sustainability assessment

**Reviewed:** 21 October 2026. The pack has 40 candidate records, 31 converted and replay-verified local candidate texts, 15 reviewed Slov-Lex legal references, four reviewed Ministry of Foreign Affairs links, and one dated Ministry of Interior FAQ link. Twenty external official references are usable within the scope and limits in [source-acceptance.json](source-acceptance.json). No local copy, converted text, topic, or country review is accepted.

## Official consolidated legal sources

Slov-Lex is the Slovak government's electronic Collection of Laws. Its per-act history pages expose effective-date versions and link to binding PDFs. The static history and binding PDFs were independently opened and checked for the 15 listed statutes. They provide an adequate government-published legal reference. Use the direct versioned record and binding PDF in the acceptance register; the history page helps identify a later version.

The active statutory references include Act 404/2011 and amendment 128/2026 (effective 1 October 2026); Acts 71/1967, 162/2015, 211/2000, 9/2010, 85/1990, 18/2018, 564/2001, 327/2005, 365/2004, 154/1994, 36/2005, 145/1995, and 69/2026. Act 404/2011 is only a partial functional match for Directive 2004/38. Its broad member-state definition generally includes EU, EEA, and Swiss states, while §§30 and 51–57 narrow it to EU Member States. Act 327/2005's statutory legal-aid coverage is specific and does not establish aid for every residence or visa dispute.

The dated references for Act 36/2005 and Act 145/1995 have known end dates: the next versions begin on 1 January 2027 and 1 December 2026 respectively. Reopen the official history and use the effective text for any later event. Other version-history records were open-ended when checked on 21 October 2026; check a later amendment when it could materially affect the issue.

The Ministry of Justice's reuse page discusses access to individual Gazette issue files in `.vp` format. Those conditions concern copies and do not prevent ordinary linking to the current government-published Slov-Lex text. No API, Gazette-fragment chain, written reuse clearance, download, hash, conversion, replay, or local archive is needed to use the official legal references. If a future task wants to retain or redistribute copies, review the terms for that specific action.

The existing Slov-Lex downloader and replay workflow is optional maintenance for retained local copies. It is not a prerequisite for legal-source use and should not be run just to validate an official link. The archive's monthly bulk packages are also optional discovery/reconciliation tools; their size does not block use of per-act current versions.

## Government guidance and mission practice

The MZV central supporting-documents page (updated 17 August 2026), stay-guidance page (updated 16 August 2026), official Schengen form, and mission directory are accepted as official references with narrow scopes. They do not establish every mission's procedure. The directory lists 82 diplomatic representations, and the Ministry says local document requirements vary. Offer the official MZV mission network for human review when current mission instructions, appointments, outsourcing, or checklists are needed.

The MZV English EU-family page is dated 2022 and contains an Act 404/2012 error. The Hanoi page is dated 2022 and cites repealed Regulation 562/2006. Neither is accepted as current operative guidance. A Minsk page was found but the exact family-member passage and location applicability were not verified, so it remains a candidate. Eight local MFA requests had returned HTTP 403; browser access to selected current MZV pages succeeded. These failures are recorded as route-specific, not evidence that the official site is unavailable.

The Interior Ministry's March 2025 EU-citizen FAQ is accepted only as a dated operational reference; it is not a current checklist. Compare its registration and evidence information with Act 404/2011 effective from 1 October 2026 before present-day use. The July 2024 FAQ remains a superseded/older lead. Office addresses, booking categories, mission representation, appointments, and local forms can change. Follow current parent pages and relevant locations when needed rather than relying on snapshots as permanent rules.

## Coverage and semantic review

The 31 local text conversions passing hash/replay checks show only that the captured conversions can be reproduced from their saved inputs. They do not independently prove that the text is legally complete or correctly classified. No local conversion is accepted here. For ordinary research, read the official source directly and verify the passages that matter. If local copies are retained later, review them against the official PDF at the level needed for the intended use, including tables, footnotes, page breaks, and annexes.

The [coverage register](coverage-register.md) maps 11 issue areas and marks all as open or partial. It distinguishes finding a law, accepting an official link, covering an issue, and completing a country review. In particular, the Act 404 scope boundaries, EU-law concordance, decision-specific remedies, consular practice, regional procedures, legal aid, and systematic case-law discovery remain unresolved.

## Disposition

Use the 20 accepted official links within their dated scope and limitations. Keep all local artifacts as unaccepted review material and all 11 baseline topics open. The next useful work is issue-led legal mapping and relevant mission/court discovery, not archive capture, reuse-permission research, or a mandatory conversion pipeline.
