# Slovakia source discovery register

Research date: 2026-10-18; retrieval update: 2026-10-19. Scope: bounded official-source discovery for EU/EEA/Swiss free movement and third-country family members; entry, residence, visa and mission practice; administrative procedure, access and remedies; family-status evidence; privacy, oversight, legal aid and courts. Sources were checked in Slovak and English where available. This is not a legal-equivalence opinion or a complete national inventory.

Comparison seed: Denmark's EU free-movement residence regulation and guidance. Prior Sweden, Finland, Czechia, Hungary and Romania findings informed source classes and publisher-risk checks; those findings were treated as leads, not proof of Slovak coverage. EEA EFTA, Swiss, EU, returning-Slovak-national and UK Withdrawal Agreement routes remain distinct unless the cited Slovak source expressly groups them.

Status: 40 candidates; 31 local candidate texts converted and replay-verified; 15 Slov-Lex laws and four narrowly scoped MZV references accepted as external official links; 0 local artifacts and 0 topics accepted. Eight MFA retrievals returned HTTP 403 in the local capture workflow. Selected current pages were accessible in browser research. See [source acceptance](source-acceptance.json), [coverage register](coverage-register.md), and [sustainability assessment](sustainability-assessment.md).

## Search interpretation

The current Slov-Lex PDF route is a promising stable date-bound candidate but is not yet an automated rule: one verified Act 404 route does not prove the URL template works for other laws, and the Justice Ministry's precise reuse policy for consolidated portal content remains unresolved. The HTML page's generated PDF link failed because of an extra `/static` segment, while the corrected official direct PDF URL opened. Candidate links remain leads until an exact rule can verify publisher, act number, language, date/version, binding status, media type and conversion.

The legal scope is not uniform across all source classes: Act 404/2011 itself draws a distinction between EU/EEA/Swiss inclusion generally and EU-only wording in §§30 and 51–57. Older MFA instructions and changing foreign-police/mission details show that a current law source cannot keep forms, appointments or local evidence lists current by itself.
