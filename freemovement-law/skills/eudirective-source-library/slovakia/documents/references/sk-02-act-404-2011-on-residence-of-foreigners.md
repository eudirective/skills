## Scope and evidence

**Scope:** Act 404/2011 on Residence of Foreigners, including entry, residence, residence documents, removal, and EU-family provisions.

**Version or date evidence:** Version effective from 1 October 2026; Slov-Lex history page showed it as the current open-ended version. The official act history and binding PDF identify the act, version, and effective date. The act is a broad immigration statute and only a partial functional match for Directive 2004/38. Its general member-state definition includes EU, EEA, and Swiss states, while §§30 and 51–57 narrow the term to EU Member States.
