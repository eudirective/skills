## Reviewed content

The portal identifies CADA as an independent administrative authority concerned with access to administrative documents and public archives, as well as reuse of public information. It describes the Commission’s role in advising on whether documents may be communicated and in issuing opinions after an administration refuses access. The page also describes advice to administrations about disclosure and reuse conditions, and links to the public opinions and advice database, activity reporting, and an online help tool.

The homepage states broadly that CADA referral is required before court proceedings. Treat that as a portal summary; check the CRPA and the procedure at issue for the precise scope and any exceptions. The live homepage checked on 2026-10-24 featured examples about copying medical records and access to a public employee’s email. Those examples are not universal rules.
