## Reviewed content

The page covers a family member whose sponsor is a citizen of an EU country other than France, an EEA country, or Switzerland. It lists spouses; direct descendants under 21 or dependent; dependent direct ascendants; and dependent direct ascendants or descendants of the spouse. For a student sponsor, it limits the listed family categories to the spouse and direct descendants.

For short-stay travel, the page directs applicants to the France-Visas visa assistant. It describes one route for people who need a short-stay visa and another for people exempt from that visa requirement; in the latter route, it says the person may enter and stay for three months and apply to the prefecture during that period. For a stay beyond three months or a move to an overseas territory, the page directs readers to the relevant embassy or consulate.

This summary records what the page says. It does not establish that a particular applicant qualifies for a visa or residence document.
