## Reviewed content

The homepage provides an online filing route through Télérecours and links to Sagace and Ariane Web. It also provides hearing-role information, a directory for finding administrative courts by department, and a changing feed of court news and decisions. These are operational entry points; their availability and details should be checked on the live site.

The legal notice says that only texts resulting from the Tribunal’s deliberations engage it and that the Journal officiel version controls for legal texts reproduced on the site. It describes other documents as informational. This summary does not reproduce or interpret any individual decision.
