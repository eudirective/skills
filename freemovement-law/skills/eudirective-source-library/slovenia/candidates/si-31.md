INFORMATION FOR SCHENGEN VISA  APPLICANTS  
ON THE PROCESSING OF PERSONAL DATA  
PURSUANT TO ARTICLE 13 OF THE GENERAL DATA PROTECTION REGULATION 
1. Data Control ler 
The controller of the personal data processed in visa application procedures is the Ministry of 
Foreign and European Affairs of the Republic of Slovenia, address: Prešernova  25, 1000 
Ljubljana, Slovenia, T: +386 1 478 2000, E: gp.mzz@gov.si. 
2. Contact details of the Data Protection Officer 
Data Protection Officer at the Ministry of Foreign and European Affairs of the Republic of 
Slovenia may be contacted at E: dpo.mzz@gov.si. 
3. Purposes and the legal basis for data processing 
Your personal data is processed exclusively for the purpose of examining the visa application 
in an administrative procedure conducted by the Ministry of Foreign and European Affairs of 
the Republic of Slovenia, represented by the designated Diplomatic Mission or Consular Post.  
Processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the exercise of official 
authority vested in the controller which is a lawful basis for the processing of personal data 
pursuant to Article 6/1/e of the General Data Protection Regulation. Pursuant Article 6/2 of the 
Data Protection Act of the Republic of Slovenia this official authority is vested in the Ministry 
of Foreign and European Affairs by law, namely  the Regulation (EC) No 810/2009 of the 
European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 establishing a Community Code on 
Visas (Visa Code) and Regulation (EC) No 767/2008 of the European Parliament and of the 
Council of 9 July 2008 concerning the Visa Information System (VIS) and the exchange of data 
between Member States on short-stay visas (VIS Regulation).

7. Rights of the data subject 
D
ata subjects have the right to request from the Ministry of Foreign and European Affairs of 
the Republic of Slovenia access to and rectification or erasure of personal data or restriction 
on processing concerning the data subject or to object to processing as well as the right to 
data portability, pursuant to Articles 15 through 20 of the General Data Protection Regulation. 
Your data is processed partly in hard copy at the designated Diplomatic Mission or Consular 
Post of the Republic of Slovenia and partly in the Visa Information System (VIS) accessible to 
all Schengen Member States. 
Request for information, correction or deletion of data in the Visa Information System (VIS) in 
Slovenia may be filled on a form available here. 
8. The right to lodge a complaint regarding the processing 
Data subjects have the right to lodge a complaint concerning the processing of personal data 
with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia, address: Dunajska cesta 22, 
1000 Ljubljana, Slovenia, E: gp.ip@ip-rs.si. 
9. Is provision of personal data obligatory? 
The provision of your personal data is necessary if you wish to apply for a Schengen visa. 
10. Automated decision-m aking, including profiling 
There is no automated decision- making, including profiling, in the processing of your data in 
the visa procedure.
