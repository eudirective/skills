## Accepted scope

Official overview of the Advocate’s advice, complaint investigation and equality-protection functions under the Protection against Discrimination Act.

## Review note

The official English page describes complaints based on protected personal grounds, investigation procedures, and possible support in related administrative or judicial proceedings. A current official page was opened directly on 25 October 2026.

## Limits

- The Advocate handles discrimination matters within the statutory protected-ground and subject-matter scope; this is not a general immigration appeal channel.
- The page does not establish a decision-specific filing deadline or result.
- Consult the Act and current complaint instructions for a particular matter.
