## Accepted scope

Official general guidance on when and how to submit an initiative to the Ombudsman.

## Review note

The page advises first contacting the responsible body, its supervisory body and using other available remedies. It describes the Ombudsman process as free and informal.

## Limits

- This page does not identify or extend statutory appeal or court deadlines.
- The Ombudsman’s role is not a substitute for a decision-specific appeal or court action.
- This link-only guidance does not establish the full Ombudsman mandate or topic coverage.
