## Accepted scope

Official service guidance for non-EU family members applying for permanent residence on an EU-family route.

## Review note

The page was directly accessible and checked on 25 October 2026. It describes a general five-year lawful continuous-residence period and a two-year route for specified family members of an EU citizen with permanent residence or a Slovenian citizen.
