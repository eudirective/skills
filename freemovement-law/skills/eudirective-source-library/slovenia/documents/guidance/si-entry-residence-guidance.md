## Accepted scope

Central government guidance and discovery page for entry, residence, EU/EEA routes, Swiss nationals, visas, linked services, forms and mission information.

## Review note

The page states that provisions of the Foreigners Act applied to EU citizens also apply to EEA citizens and their family members. It presents Swiss nationals separately and links the service, visa and supporting-document material. Candidate records `si_29` and `si_30` captured this same parent page, so they are represented here rather than as duplicate documents.

## Limits

- Keep EU/EEA routes distinct from the separate EU–Swiss Agreement route and from Slovenian-national sponsor cases.
