## Accepted scope

Official directory of Slovenian diplomatic representations and consulates abroad.

## Review note

The page distinguishes embassies and permanent representations from the different consular offices and links onward to individual posts.

## Limits

- The directory alone does not determine visa competence, representation arrangements, appointment access, current checklist, fees or local filing procedure.
- Resolve the relevant post from the applicant’s location and review that post’s live instructions.
- This directory does not establish a complete mission-operations record.
