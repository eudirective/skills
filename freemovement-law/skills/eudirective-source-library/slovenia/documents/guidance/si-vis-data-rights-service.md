# VIS access, correction and deletion request service

- **Jurisdiction:** Slovenia
- **Source ID:** `si_32`
- **Publisher:** Government of the Republic of Slovenia
- **Official source:** [Request access to, correction of, or deletion of VIS data](https://www.gov.si/zbirke/storitve/zahteva-za-seznanitev-s-podatki-v-vizumskem-informacijskem-sistemu-vis-v-sloveniji/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-25
- **Source-copy status:** link-only; the service page and form are not retained.

## Accepted scope

Official service route and linked form for an individual seeking access to, correction of or deletion of their data in the Visa Information System in Slovenia.

## Review note

The page identifies the application form and refers to the Visa Code and VIS Regulation. It links to the Information Commissioner for oversight information.

## Limits

- This records a public request route, not an individual’s data or a completed request.
- Use the current form linked from the service page. Do not collect personal submissions in the source library.
- The page does not replace the applicable EU and Slovenian data-protection rules or establish complete privacy-rights coverage.
