## Accepted scope

Official service guidance for third-country family members of an EU citizen or Slovenian citizen, including the application route and linked forms.

## Review note

The page was reopened on 25 October 2026. It says the family member may remain without a residence permit for 90 days and must apply before that period ends to stay longer.

## Limits

- The page groups EU-citizen and Slovenian-citizen sponsor routes. Keep their legal bases and any return routes distinct.
- This is government service guidance, not a complete legal definition of family membership or entitlement.
- Attachments are mutable; follow the current links on the service page.
