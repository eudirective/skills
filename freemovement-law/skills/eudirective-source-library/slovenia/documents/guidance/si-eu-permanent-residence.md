## Accepted scope

Official service guidance for an EU citizen applying for a permanent-residence registration certificate.

## Review note

The page describes a general five-year lawful continuous-residence route, examples of absences that do not interrupt continuity and listed routes for obtaining the certificate earlier.

## Limits

- Confirm the applicable route in the current PISRS text and distinguish the EU citizen’s certificate from a family member’s permit.
- The central page states the relevant Foreigners Act provisions extend to EEA nationals and their family members; check the route for the specific applicant.
