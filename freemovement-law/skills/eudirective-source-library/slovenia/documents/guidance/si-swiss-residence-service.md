## Accepted scope

Official service guidance and linked application forms for Swiss citizens and their family members, including temporary and permanent residence.

## Review note

The page expressly grounds the route in the Agreement on Free Movement of Persons between the EU and Switzerland and provides distinct forms for some applicant categories.

## Limits

- Keep this agreement-based route distinct from EU and EEA free-movement routes.
- Service guidance does not establish the complete rights of each family category.
- The linked forms are mutable. Rediscover them from the current service page; this record does not accept a local copy or complete Swiss-route coverage.
