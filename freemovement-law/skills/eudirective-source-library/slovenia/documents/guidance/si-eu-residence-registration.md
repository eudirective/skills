- **Jurisdiction:** Slovenia
- **Source ID:** `si_23`
- **Publisher:** Government of the Republic of Slovenia; Ministry of the Interior and Public Administration

## Accepted scope

Official service guidance for an EU citizen who intends to reside in Slovenia longer than three months, including filing with an administrative unit, listed residence purposes and linked application forms.

## Review note

The page was reopened on 25 October 2026 and its title, procedure scope and form links were confirmed.

## Limits

- The service page is specifically for EU citizens. The parent entry-and-residence page states the Foreigners Act extension to EEA citizens and family members; verify that route for the particular applicant.
- Forms may change. Use the current attachments linked from the service page.
- This guidance does not establish complete statutory coverage or accept a local copy.
