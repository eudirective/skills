## Accepted scope

Official privacy notice for personal data processed in Slovenian Schengen visa applications.

## Review note

The notice identifies the Ministry as controller and lists its data-protection contact. It describes processing for the visa procedure, VIS-related processing and the retention period stated in the notice.
