## PISRS and official legal publication

PISRS is the official Slovenian legal-information portal. Its government-published consolidated text is an acceptable working reference when the act, displayed edition/date, and informational label are clear. Use the consolidated text directly; there is no need to reconstruct the Gazette history or acquire every amendment.

The PISRS API instructions require authorization for API use. That restriction applies to an automated API client; it does not prevent manual browsing or citing a public PISRS record. This review client receives a JavaScript shell for the PISRS records, so the listed legal pages are offered for human review through the whole official PISRS site. The human reviewer can use the official record’s displayed consolidated version and note its edition. Check a particular Gazette item only when a material amendment, commencement date, or specific discrepancy matters. Do not build an API or Gazette adapter just to make an official law link usable.

OPSI’s CC BY 4.0 dataset covers basic regulation metadata, not full legal texts. Neither this dataset nor PISRS API documentation is needed for ordinary link use. Copying or redistribution can be checked separately if a local artifact is later required.

## GOV.SI guidance, forms, privacy and missions

The central Entry and Residence page is a strong discovery parent: it links the EU, EEA and Swiss sections, service pages, visa process, forms, privacy notices, VIS/SIS rights, and mission directory. It is a dynamic HTML page with page-level edit metadata and mutable attachments. When a current form or procedure is needed, follow the parent page and use its current attachment. There is no need to validate every attachment or automate a change detector before ordinary link use.

Mission instructions, application channels, appointment access, fees, checklists and representation arrangements vary by location. The Copenhagen Embassy page is one dated sample; it cannot represent the national mission network. For a particular case, start from the current directory and review the relevant mission page. A full all-post inventory is unnecessary unless the project specifically needs network-wide comparison. The present discovery does not attempt a complete mission search.

The English Schengen privacy notice was available as an official PDF and identifies MZEZ, VIS and external service provider processing. It is an opaque attachment, so the parent page must remain the rediscovery route. Public procedural guidance can be monitored; individual visa files, privacy requests and submitted personal data must not be collected.

## Legal and geographic scope

GOV.SI expressly says that Foreigners Act provisions used for EU citizens also apply to EEA member-state citizens and their family members. The same central page and service inventory present Swiss nationals separately, and the Swiss service explicitly cites the EU–Swiss Agreement on Free Movement of Persons. Keep those as distinct legal routes. A shared GOV.SI page or form directory is not evidence that all detailed rules, family definitions, or remedies are the same.

The EU-family service for non-EU relatives groups EU citizens and Slovenian citizens on one page. Its two legal bases may differ, so the source record must retain that distinction. The directly linked permanent-family service is now accessible and accepted as a dated official link; its legal conditions and form links still require a provision-level crosswalk. Family-status evidence, recognition, translation and authentication were only partially searched.

## Procedure, remedies, privacy, legal aid and courts

ZUP-I has a direct official Gazette issue PDF and PISRS reports that the consolidated ZUP text begins use on 7 February 2026. Keep the amendment, publication, application date and consolidated record separate. GOV.SI procedure guidance is explanatory, and special immigration provisions may govern particular steps. Ombudsman, Information Commissioner, equality-body, administrative appeal and judicial review channels have different mandates; none should be treated as a substitute for a decision-specific remedy.

The PISRS advanced search offers selected Supreme Court, higher-court, labour/social-court and Administrative Court collections. The official court portal lists a case-law RSS feed, but the target endpoint, retention, inclusion criteria and coverage were not established. Do not claim complete national case-law coverage from either surface. eUprava says foreign nationals may qualify for legal aid under statutory conditions; its legal-aid pages were last changed in August 2025 and warn that they have not yet been renewed and will redirect to the old eUprava site. The FAQ describes eligibility by residence/status and reciprocity or treaty conditions. Reconcile that guidance with current ZBPP and the live service; the page migration is a concrete change-detection risk.
