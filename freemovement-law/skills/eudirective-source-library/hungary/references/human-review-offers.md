## AJBH complaint, equality and protected-reporting routes

- **Publisher:** Office of the Commissioner for Fundamental Rights (Alapvető Jogok Biztosának Hivatala, AJBH).
- **Website:** [AJBH](https://www.ajbh.hu/).
- **Relevant routes:** [Contact the Commissioner](https://www.ajbh.hu/forduljon-a-biztoshoz), [equal-treatment application](https://www.ajbh.hu/ebff-kerelem-benyujtasa), and [equal-treatment procedure](https://www.ajbh.hu/ebff-hogyan-zajlik-az-eljaras).
- **Known status:** These pages and the official PDFs are on the AJBH domain. Direct page retrieval returned HTTP 403 in this review client. Search results identify the 2025 PDF as guidance on public-interest and abuse reports through the protected electronic system; it is not a general individual complaint guide.
- **Reviewer decision:** Inspect the full official site and identify the current route, forms, deadlines, and competent AJBH unit for individual Ombudsman complaints, equal-treatment authority cases, and public-interest/abuse reports. Record each route's scope and displayed date.
- **Effect:** The three identified AJBH publications remain usable as official links within their document titles and dates. No local copy or remedies topic is accepted. This review does not block the other Hungary source work.
