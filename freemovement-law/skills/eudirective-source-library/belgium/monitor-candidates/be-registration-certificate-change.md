Registration certificate to change as of 28 september 2026

17/08/2026

The Immigration Office is modernising the Registration Certificate (RC), also known as the “orange card.”

From the end of September 2026, a new model will be gradually introduced.

This change aims to make the document more modern, more secure, and better protected against forgery.

What will change in practice?

Until now, the Registration Certificate has existed in the form of:

an orange card; or

an A4-format document (“temporary/provisional model”).

The new model will now take the form of a secure passport-type booklet.

This new format will provide better protection for the document while also facilitating its administrative management.

Do I need to replace my current document?

No.

If you already hold a valid Registration Certificate (RC), you do not need to take any action.

The current models (the orange card or the A4 format) will remain valid until their expiration date.

There is therefore no need to apply for a new document immediately.

Will my rights change?

No.

This modernization concerns only the document format.

It does not change:

the conditions for issuing the Registration Certificate;

the right of residence evidenced by the certificate;

any rights associated with it.

In other words: the document is changing, not your residence status.

Key points to remember

Registration certificate to change as of 28 september 2026

A new model will be introduced from 28 September 2026

The document will become a secure passport-type booklet

Existing models will remain valid for a transitional period

If you already have a valid RC, you do not need to do anything.
