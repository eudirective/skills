The complaints process

The complaints process

Body

We handle your complaint with care, independently and in an unbiased manner. As we are bound by professional secrecy, your complaint will be treated confidentially.

File number

When you lodge a complaint, we will acknowledge receipt in writing within 5 working days and give you a file number (for instance “IDO/2022/00001”). Quote that number any time you contact us so that we are able to help you faster.

Checks

Before we investigate your complaint, we will first make sure that it involves a federal public serviceand that we have the authority to intervene on your behalf. If we cannot, we will send you the details of the service that may be able to sort out your problem without undue delay. If that happens to be another ombudsman, we will pass on your complaint to the ombudsman in question directly.

Investigating your complaint

Our expert will gather all the relevant information and consider all points of view. If necessary, he will ask you for further details or additional documents. He will also make enquiries with the federal public service concerned. Our expert will regularly update you on how matters are progressing. We have investigative powers and can ask the public services to give us access to any documents and to reply to our questions.

In 2 complaints out of 3, our investigation will take less than 3 months.

Solution

Many of our solutions are arrived at through mediation and dialogue. If our expert deems that the federal public service made a mistake or did not handle you file correctly, he will ask them to rectify the situation. In the opposite case, if he deems that the public service acted correctly or if he is unable to help you, he will explain to you why. Our expert always informs you of his exact conclusions in plain language.

In more than 8 founded complaints out of 10, we obtain a positive solution.

Sometimes your problem may call for a more comprehensive solution. When we feel that an administrative procedure, a regulation or a law needs to be amended, we make a recommendation to the administration or to the Federal Parliament. In that way, your complaint can lead to a solution that will benefit others who find themselves in the same situation.

Closing of the complaint

Our expert concludes his investigation and notifies you to that effect. He will also inform the federal public service concerned of his findings.
