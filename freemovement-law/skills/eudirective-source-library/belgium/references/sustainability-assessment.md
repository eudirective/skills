# Belgium source review and sustainability assessment

Review date: 24 October 2026

## Decision

The register contains 48 candidate records. Forty-seven official links are accepted within their recorded scope; the superseded IBZ `/family` route is not relied upon. Twenty-nine local copies are accepted: nine federal Justel legal texts; thirteen official guidance, procedure and oversight snapshots; three IBZ form/specimen PDFs; the FPS Foreign Affairs visa-routing matrix; current Walloon and Flemish regional public-information laws; and the January 2025 French RvV/CCE selected-jurisprudence issue. The Federal Justice international-adoption guidance was accepted as a date-bounded administrative page snapshot after a current-page review. No legal topic is accepted as adequately covered, and Belgium review is not complete. Of eleven converted review artifacts, one is now accepted locally, seven are closed as link-only or superseded, and three remain open in the Monitor local-only archive.

For ordinary use, prefer a clearly identified current government consolidation. When the exact edition or scope is unclear, consult the official source directly; the whole official site/service is listed in `source-acceptance.json` for human review. The review does not require Gazette-fragment reconstruction, API registration, a custom capture/conversion rule, local hashes, replay, or a scheduled updater.

## Important scope decisions

- Justel’s French and Dutch records for the Immigration Law of 1980 and Royal Decree of 1981 are official consolidated text links. Read the displayed language, version and relevant future-effect notes on the source page.
- The older IBZ `/family` page is superseded by the current `/family-reunification` route. That current page is incomplete and marked work in progress, so accept it only as an official route, not as proof of legal scope.
- Annex 19ter is accepted only as the specimen linked from the current IBZ index. The legacy filename does not establish its current revision or classification.
- The native 12 October 2026 Foreign Affairs competence-matrix PDF is accepted as a dated routing aid after checking its structure and sample UK/UAE rows. The UK and UAE mission pages are examples, not a review of every mission.
- The visa GDPR page dated 24 February 2026 is the preferred current operational privacy page. The separate 2022 privacy statement is historical.
- Annexes 19 and 20 are accepted as exact native form PDFs linked from the current IBZ index; their publisher filenames identify 2025 specimens. Annex 19ter is preserved as the two-page specimen linked from the current IBZ index; its SPECIMEN watermark means it is not a blank operational form. The January 2025 RvV/CCE monthly issue is preserved under `documents/case_law/` as a selective historical publication. Its featured judgment item is not treated as a full judgment, and no broad court coverage is accepted.
- The 2018 Data Protection Act is now linked through official French and Dutch Justel locators. Federal government pages identify the statute and NUMAC; Justel is offered for direct review because its live record did not render in this browser check.
- Ostbelgien Live’s official legal database provides coordinated current German-speaking Community decrees and orders and some German-language federal texts. It expressly states that the Official Gazette original is legally binding. The exact current records for the 1995 access-to-documents decree and the 2022 information/complaints decree still need to be found in that database.
- The French Community’s 1994 publicity decree, COCOF operational access procedure, and the 2019 Brussels joint access decree are now linked. Keep their institutional scopes separate. The 1997 provinces/municipalities law is linked as a statute locator; its current regional/local application must be checked before use.
