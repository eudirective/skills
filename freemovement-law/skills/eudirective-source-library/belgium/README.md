# Belgium national-source candidates

This is the second alphabetical discovery installment in the 31-jurisdiction EEA-plus-Switzerland expansion. It reconciles the prior public-mirror Belgium inventory with the expanded review and records 39 distinct official candidates. A bounded capture pass now has exact responses and replay-verified conversions for all 39 records, stored in a separate `monitor-candidates/` folder. None is accepted, and the generic pass does not establish a sustainable source-specific update rule.

The candidates cover the Immigration Act and implementing decree, official forms and indexes, EU-family residence guidance, a distinct mobile-Belgian route, D-visa instructions, national consular routing and mission examples, administrative procedure, access to records at federal and regional levels, complaint and review channels, data protection, private international law, adoption guidance, and a selected court-material lead. The capture pass preserved source hashes and conversions for offline replay. A successful generic GET does not establish text completeness or legal currentness; Justel version history, changing form versions, reuse terms, regional competence, and full court coverage remain open.

The Danish EU residence regulation remains a comparison seed only. Belgium's 1980 Immigration Act is a broad national statute with provisions specific to EU-family residence; it is not treated as a legal equivalent. The 40ter guidance for Belgians who exercised free movement is kept distinct from the route for Belgians who did not. Earlier candidate files remain preserved under `candidates/`; the new generic capture outputs are under `monitor-candidates/`. None is accepted source text.
