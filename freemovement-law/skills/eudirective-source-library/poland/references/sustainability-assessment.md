# Poland source-route sustainability assessment

**Review date:** 21 October 2026

**Inventory:** 63 references; 54 current official links accepted within scope; 4 historical-only references; 5 exact routes not relied upon with whole-site human-review offers.

**Local copies:** 0 accepted. **Topics:** 0 accepted. **Country review:** incomplete.

This review separates use of official source links from an optional workflow for retaining local copies. A source link does not need API access, registration, capture, conversion, hashes or replay to be usable for ordinary research. Prefer a trustworthy government-published consolidation. If publisher, current version, scope or live procedure cannot be established confidently, offer the whole official portal for human review.

## Route assessment

- **ELI legal records — suitable for link-based research.** ELI records expose the official title, publication identifier, displayed status and version date. Current consolidated links are accepted for the 2006 EU-citizen residence Act, the Electronic Delivery Act, SIS/VIS implementation, free legal aid, administrative-court procedure, fees and other supporting instruments. Check a later amendment only when it may affect the issue. No routine API poll, API registration review or Gazette-chain reconstruction is needed for link use.
- **gov.pl and UDSC pages — suitable as scoped operational links.** Captured official pages identify their stated service and responsible office. Use parent pages for forms and attachments; verify a linked form and competent authority when it matters. Currentness and local voivodeship practice remain dynamic, so whole-site review is offered for direct inspection.
- **MOS and e-Konsulat — human-review routes for dynamic workflows.** These portals contain interactive, country-specific or changing procedures. A local text extraction is not a prerequisite to link use. The MOS EU residence-card specimen was not visually checked; the e-Konsulat capture was a dynamic shell. Whole-site review offers record exactly what should be inspected.
- **Court and oversight portals — useful discovery links with coverage limits.** NSA and RPO references are accepted only as search or selected-oversight sources. The pack does not claim complete publication or exhaustive case-law discovery.
- **UODO SIS procedure — official page, channel conflict unresolved.** Its recorded ePUAP instruction conflicts with the post-2026 e-delivery guidance. The relevant SIS/VIS Act, Electronic Delivery Act and special-law exceptions are identified; the precise route needs provision-specific review. Do not present either channel as universal.
