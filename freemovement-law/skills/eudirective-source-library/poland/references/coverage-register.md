## Status meanings

- **Partial:** useful official links exist, but the legal or operational issue map is unfinished.
- **Open:** repeatable discovery or direct inspection is still needed.
- **Unresolved:** a material legal-status, currentness, or procedure question is still open.

Whole-site human-review offers are recorded for ELI/ISAP, UDSC, MSWiA, MOS, MFA/e-Konsulat, UODO, NSA and RPO where exact live content or local practice needs direct inspection. An offer is a review route, not a claim that the source is absent or unusable.
