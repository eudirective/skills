Application residence document:  
residence with EU family member for 
family members other than partner 
and child 
About this form  
Your family member (who is not your parent or partner) has the nationality of a European 
Union (EU) or European Economic Area (EEA) country or of Switzerland, but not Dutch 
nationality. Your family member is an EU citizen. You yourself are not an EU citizen. To live 
with your family member in the Netherlands, you need an EU residence document. Use this 
form to apply for this document. Do you already have a document for residence with this 
EU family member? And is that about to expire? Use this form to apply for a new 
document. Make sure the IND has received the application before your document expires. 
But no more than 3 months before the expiry. 
 
Before filling in the form 
• Het Nederlandse aanvraagformulier voor verblijf bij een EU-familielid is te vinden op 
ind.nl/verblijf-bij-EU-familielid. 
• Check the requirements for this residence document at ind.nl/en/residence-family-EU.  
• You can only submit this application if you are in the Netherlands. Go to 
netherlandsworldwide.nl and check if you need a visa to come to the Netherlands. 
• Is the application for a child under 12 years of age? Then a parent with parental 
authority submits the application. Any reference to ‘you’ in this form means the child 
for whom the application is being made. You can read about parental authority at 
rijksoverheid.nl.  
• First, collect all required documents. Read about how to have official foreign 
documents legalised and translated at ind.nl/en/documents. 
• If you fail to submit sufficient documents and/or do not have official foreign documents 
legalised and translated, it will take longer for the IND to decide. The IND can also 
reject your application for that reason.

Filling in the form 
Fill in the form digitally and then print it. Or print the form and then fill it in with pen. 
Always use a black or blue pen. Answer the open questions in block letters. Choose a 
selection box by filling it completely. If you made a mistake, cross out the incorrect box.

Submitting the form 
Send the form and the documents to: IND, postbus 16, 9560 AA Ter Apel. 
 
After your application 
• This application requires payment. You will receive a letter explaining how to pay. View 
the costs of the application on ind.nl/en/fees. If a residence document is not issued to 
you, you will not receive a refund.  
• The IND usually issues a residence endorsement (sticker) to be placed in your passport. 
This shows that you are permitted to stay in the Netherlands, at least until the IND has 
made a decision. You will receive a letter explaining how to obtain this sticker. 
• The decision period for your application is listed on ind.nl/en/decision-periods. 
Sometimes it takes longer, for example if not all documents are included with the 
application. 
• The decision on your application is also set out in a letter from the IND.

 Copy of proof of being caring parent 
• Submit copies of documents showing that you currently care for your child and did 
so previously. In the Netherlands and/or abroad. For example, submit a letter in 
which you describe your care and upbringing tasks. And explain the family situation 
from the birth of your child until now. Include copies of as many documents as 
possible from independent and impartial individuals and organisations that support 
your statement. Also answer the following questions in your letter: What would 
change in the life of your child if you were not allowed to live in the Netherlands? Is 
there a special situation, for example regarding the family, school, support, social 
network, care, or health of the child? 
• Is there another parent? Then you also have to include a statement from that 
parent. In this letter, the other parent describes what your tasks are in the care and 
upbringing of the child. And the family situation from the birth of your child until 
now. Include copies of as many documents as possible from independent and 
impartial individuals and organisations that support the statement. Also submit a 
copy of the travel document of the other parent. Only copy the page with the 
identity details and signature. 
• Copies of photos of you with your child. From birth or from the moment you became 
part of the life of your child up to now. Give explanations for what is shown in the 
photos. Also submit photos that show your involvement in care and upbringing 
tasks.  
• Copies of documents or statements from, for example, a general practitioner or 
medical specialist that show you were present during medical treatments or check-
ups of your child in the Netherlands or abroad. And when this was. 
• Copies of documents or statements from, for example, the school of your child, 
sports club, daycare centre, sports coach, or maternity nurse in the Netherlands or 
abroad. And these should describe your care and upbringing tasks. And since when 
you have had these tasks.  
• Copies of documents showing that you are financially supporting your child. 
Financial support means that you regularly pay for something for your child. For 
example, include copies of receipts for school supplies or clothing. You have to be 
able to prove that you were the one who paid. You can also include copies of 
documents showing that you transferred money for the care of your child.

C. Parent, grandparent or great-grandparent 
You are the parent, grandparent or great-grandparent of the EU citizen. Or of the married

 Copy of proof of financial support 
Submit copies of documents showing that the EU citizen and/or the married/registered 
partner gave you money regularly in the last 12 months before you came to the 
Netherlands. This money was for your basic needs such as food, drink and/or clothing. 
Or for your fixed expenses such as your rent or mortgage, energy, insurance and/or 
subscriptions. Also submit copies of documents showing what you spent the money on. 
Or of documents showing that the EU citizen and/or the married/registered partner paid 
bills for you. 
 
 Copy of proof of financial dependency 
Submit copies of documents showing that without financial help from the EU citizen 
and/or the married/registered partner, you could not pay your basic needs and/or your 
fixed expenses. In the country where you are from or previously lived. For example, a 
statement from an official organisation from this country showing this. Also include a 
letter explaining that you were financially dependent. 
 
 Copy of proof of financial support in the event of ≥ 4 months in the 
Netherlands 
Have you been in the Netherlands for 4 months or more? Then you have to prove that 
the EU citizen and/or the married/registered partner also gave you money regularly in 
the Netherlands. This money was for your basic needs such as food, drink and/or 
clothing. Or for your fixed expenses such as rent or mortgage, energy, insurance and/or 
subscriptions. Submit copies of documents showing that the EU citizen and/or the 
married/registered partner gave you money and what you spent it on. Or of documents 
showing that the EU citizen and/or the married/registered partner paid bills for you.  
 
 Copy of proof of financial dependency in the event of ≥ 4 months in the 
Netherlands 
Have you been in the Netherlands for 4 months or more? Then you have to submit 
copies of documents showing that without financial help from the EU citizen and/or the 
married/registered partner, you cannot pay your basic needs and/or fixed expenses in 
the Netherlands. For example, a statement from an (aid) organisation showing this. Also 
submit a letter explaining that you were financially dependent.
