Application residence document:  
residence with EU family member for 
partner and child 
About this form  
Your partner or parent has the nationality of a European Union (EU) or European Economic 
Area (EEA) country or of Switzerland, but not Dutch nationality. Your family member is 
therefore an EU citizen. You yourself are not an EU citizen. To live with your partner or 
parent in the Netherlands, you need an EU residence document. Use this form to apply for 
this document. Do you already have a document for residence with this EU family 
member? And is that about to expire? Use this form to apply for a new document. Make 
sure the IND has received the application before your document expires. But no more than 
3 months before the expiry. 
 
Before filling in the form 
• Het Nederlandse aanvraagformulier voor verblijf bij een EU-familielid is te vinden op 
ind.nl/verblijf-bij-EU-familielid. 
• Check the requirements for this residence document at ind.nl/en/residence-family-EU. 
• Are you going to live with a Dutch national? Read about Dutch nationals and EU law at 
ind.nl/ nederlanders-en-het-eu-recht. Use the form for residence with EU family 
member (Dutch national) at ind.nl/verblijf-bij-EU-familielid if you and the Dutch 
national meet the requirements.  
• Is the application for a foster child of an EU citizen? Then use the form for residence 
with another EU family member at ind.nl/en/residence-family-EU. 
• You can only submit this application if you are in the Netherlands. Go to 
netherlandsworldwide.nl and check if you need a visa to come to the Netherlands. 
• Is the application for a child under 12 years of age? Then a parent with parental 
authority makes the application. Any reference to ‘you’ in this form means the child for 
whom the application is being made. You can read about parental authority at 
government.nl.  
• First, collect all required documents. Read about how to have official foreign 
documents legalised and translated at ind.nl/en/documents.

Filling in the form 
Fill in the form digitally and then print it. Or print the form and then fill it in with pen. 
Always use a black or blue pen. Answer the open questions in block letters. Choose a 
selection box by filling it completely. If you made a mistake, cross out the incorrect box. 
And then fill in the correct box. Print the form in black and white or colour, on A4 paper 
and in full size. 
 
Submitting the form 
Send the form and the documents to: IND, postbus 16, 9560 AA Ter Apel. 
 
After your application 
• This application requires payment. You will receive a letter explaining how to pay. View 
the costs of the application on ind.nl/en/fees. If a residence document is not issued to 
you, you will not receive a refund.  
• The IND usually issues a residence endorsement (sticker) to be placed in your passport. 
This shows that you are permitted to stay in the Netherlands, at least until the IND has 
made a decision. You will receive a letter explaining how to obtain this sticker. 
• The decision period for your application is listed on ind.nl/en/decision-periods. 
Sometimes it takes longer, for example if not all documents are included with the 
application. 
• The decision on your application is also set out in a letter from the IND.

IN THE EVENT OF A RELATIONSHIP 
WITHOUT MARRIAGE OR 
REGISTRATION  
You are not married and do not have a registered partnership. This application requires 
you to show that you are in a sustainable relationship. Whether your relationship is 
considered sustainable depends on several things. Because of that, the IND requires a 
great deal of information. 
 
You provide this information by answering questions about your relationship. And by 
telling us about your relationship yourself. At ‘Submitting documents’, you will see the 
documents you have to submit to show that you have a sustainable relationship. 
 
If you do not tell us much about your relationship or if you fail to submit enough 
documents or enough different documents, the IND can reject your application. This is 
because in that case, the IND is unable to properly determine whether your relationship 
can be considered sustainable.

Explanation of documents

     B. Married or registered partner

your country of origin.

properly assess whether you meet the requirements.

be in colour. The same applies for photos. The rest may be a black-and-white copy.

 Copy of proof of family relationship  
Examples include a birth certificate, deed of acknowledgement or copy of a decision 
from a family court. Alternatively, submit copies of other documents or statements that 
show the family relationship. Does the situation concern adoption? Then you have to 
submit copies of documents proving this. 
 
 Copy of proof of being part of family 
Submit copies of documents showing that you belonged to the family of the EU citizen 
and the partner in your country of origin.  
 
 Copy of proof of living with parent 
Submit copies of documents showing that you and your parent live at the same 
address. Are you registered at the same address as your parent in the Personal Records 
Database (BRP) of the municipality? Then you do not have to submit any documents. 
Do you not live with your parent? Then submit a letter in which you write why this is 
the case.

 Copy of proof of parental authority over child 
Submit copies of documents showing that your parent (i.e. the partner of the EU citizen 
partner) has authority over you. For example, an extract from the parental authority  
register or a court ruling. If your parent does not have parental authority over you, 
explain why in a letter. You can read about parental authority at government.nl.  
 
 Appendix: Declaration of consent of the parent remaining behind 
Do you have another parent who has parental authority over you? Is that parent not in 
the Netherlands and are they also not coming to the Netherlands? Then this parent has 
to fill in the appendix Declaration of consent of the parent remaining behind (pdf, 149 
kB). This can be found at ind.nl/declaration-consent. Submit this declaration with your 
application. Are you unable to obtain this declaration? Then download the appendix 
Declaration not submitted Declaration of consent of the parent remaining behind (pdf, 
145 kB) at ind.nl/en/absence-declaration. And submit it with your application. 
 
 Copy of identity document of the parent remaining behind 
Do you need to submit a declaration of consent of the parent remaining behind? Also 
submit a copy of the identity document of the parent remaining behind. This has to 
include their signature.
