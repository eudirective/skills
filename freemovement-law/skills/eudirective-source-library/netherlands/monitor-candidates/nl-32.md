Follow-up application residence 
document: after residence with EU  
family member 
 
 
About this form  
You currently have a residence document for residence with a family member or partner 
from an EU/EEA country or Switzerland. Or for residence with a Dutch family member or 
Dutch partner who comes under EU law (from now on: EU citizen). This residence 
document is about to expire. And your situation with the EU citizen has changed. Because 
the EU citizen has died or left. Or because your marriage, registered partnership or 
relationship with the EU citizen has ended. You want to continue living in the Netherlands. 
You can use this form to submit a follow-up application for your EU residence document.  
 
Before filling in the form 
• Het Nederlandse aanvraagformulier voor Vervolgaanvraag verblijfsdocument: na verblijf 
bij EU-familielid is te vinden op ind.nl/verblijf-bij-EU-familielid. 
• Check if you can stay in the Netherlands if you are no longer living with the EU citizen 
at ind.nl/en/changed-situation-EU.  
• You may be able to apply for a residence document that has more benefits for you. 
Read about the residence document for permanent residency for EU citizens at 
ind.nl/en/permanent-residence-EU. 
• Is the application for a child under 12 years of age? Then a parent with parental 
authority submits the application. Any reference to ‘you’ in this form means the child 
for whom the application is being made. You can read about parental authority at 
government.nl.  
• First, collect all required documents. Read about how to have official foreign 
documents legalised and translated at ind.nl/en/documents. 
• If you fail to submit sufficient documents and/or do not have official foreign documents 
legalised and translated, it will take longer for the IND to decide. The IND can also 
reject your application for this reason.

Filling in the form 
Fill in the form digitally and then print it. Or print the form and then fill it in with pen. 
Always use a black or blue pen. Answer the open questions in block letters. Choose a 
selection box by filling it completely. If you made a mistake, cross out the incorrect box.

Submitting the form 
Send the form and the documents to: IND, postbus 16, 9560 AA Ter Apel. 
 
After your application 
• This application requires payment. You will receive a letter explaining how to pay. Check 
the costs of the application on ind.nl/en/fees. If you don’t get a residence document, 
you will not get a refund. 
• The decision period for your application is listed on ind.nl/en/decision-periods. 
Sometimes it takes more time. For example, when not all required documents are 
submitted with the application.  
• You will receive the decision in a letter.

SUBMITTING DOCUMENTS  
Explanation of documents  
• Submit the documents appropriate that apply to your situation with this form. The 
documents are included in the list below. You do not have to submit the list with your 
application.  
• A. General: always submit  
• B. The EU citizen has left the Netherlands 
• C. The relationship with the EU citizen has ended 
• D. The EU citizen has died 
 
• The documents are an important part of your application. You can prove that you meet 
the requirements with documents. You will need to request many official documents 
from the government of your country of origin. 
• Have official foreign documents legalised and translated. Read about how to do this at 
ind.nl/en/documents. 
• If it says 'for example', you may choose which documents to include. The more 
different documents you submit, the better the IND can assess if you meet the 
requirements.  
• If you fail to submit sufficient different documents, it will take longer for the IND to 
decide. The IND can also reject your application. Because we cannot properly assess if 
you meet the requirements.  
• Always submit copies of original documents. Copies of certificates and passports must 
be in colour. The same applies for photos. The rest may be a black-and-white copy. 
 
A. General 
 Copy of passport or other travel document  
Copy the page with identity details from your travel document and the pages with travel 
stamps. So do not copy blank pages.  
 
 Authorisation declaration 
Do you have an authorised person? But have you not yet authorised this person? Fill in 
the Authorisation declaration (pdf, 165 kB) at ind.nl/en/authorisation and submit the 
form with the application.

 Statement and proof in case of special facts or situations 
Are there any special facts or situations that the IND needs to know about? About you, 
a family member or the EU citizen, or all of you? Then you have to submit a letter 
explaining what is going on, and copies of (official) supporting documents. 
 
B. The EU citizen has left the Netherlands 
 Copy of proof of departure of EU citizen 
Submit a copy of a document proving that the EU citizen has moved abroad, if you can 
get that. For example, proof of registration in a foreign municipality. Or a letter or other 
message from the EU citizen stating that they have left. 
 
 Copy of proof of education in the Netherlands 
Are you the child of the EU citizen who has left? And do you want to finish your school 
or degree programme in The Netherlands? For example, secondary school, vocational 
education (MBO), higher professional education (HBO) or university? Then submit 
copies of documents showing that you are enrolled in education in the Netherlands. For 
example, a proof of registration at the educational institution for this year.  
 
 Copy of proof of family relationship 
• Are you the child of an EU citizen who has left? And do you want to finish your 
education in the Netherlands? Then submit copies of documents proving the family 
relationship between you and the EU citizen.  
• Are you the caring parent of this child? Then submit copies of documents proving 
the family relationship between you and the child and between the child and the EU 
citizen. For example, submit a (combination of) birth certificate, deed of 
acknowledgement, adoption documents, or copy of a decision from a family court. 
 
 Copy of proof of caring parent  
You want to stay in the Netherlands because you are caring for the child of the EU 
citizen and you. This child wants to stay in the Netherlands to complete an educational 
programme. Submit copies of documents showing that you are the caring parent of this 
child. For example, submit:  
• a statement in which you describe your care and upbringing tasks at this moment. 
Include copies of as many documents as possible from independent and impartial 
individuals and organisations. These should prove that you are taking care of your 
child. Also answer the following questions in your statement: What would change in 
the life of your child if you were not allowed to live in the Netherlands? Is there a 
special situation, for example regarding the family, school, support, social network, 
care, or health of the child?

 Consent of the parent with parental authority who has left 
Do you have a minor child with the EU citizen? Does the parent who has left also have 
parental authority over the child? Then this parent must fill in the appendix Declaration 
of consent of the parent remaining behind (pdf, 149 kB) for every minor child. This can 
be found at ind.nl/declaration-consent. Are you unable to obtain this declaration? Then 
download the Appendix Declaration not submitted Declaration of consent of the parent 
remaining behind (pdf, 145 kB) at ind.nl/en/absence-declaration. And submit it with

 Copy of passport or other travel document of the parent who has left 
Are you required to submit the Declaration of consent of the parent remaining behind? 
Then also submit a copy of the identity document of this parent, if possible. Submit 
copies of the pages with identity details and travel stamps. So not the blank pages.  
 
C. The relationship with the EU citizen has ended 
Your marriage, registered partnership or relationship with the EU citizen with whom you 
were living has ended. And at least 1 of the following situations applies to you: 
1. The marriage, registered partnership or relationship lasted at least 3 years. You lived 
in the Netherlands for at least 1 year of the marriage or relationship.  
2. You have a child or children with the EU citizen. And you have parental authority over 
this child or these children.  
3. You have a minor child or minor children with the EU citizen. You have rights of access 
to this child or these children. And the court has ordered that you must be able to see 
your children in the Netherlands. 
4. There is a special or difficult situation. For example, you were a victim of domestic 
violence during your relationship. Or you are a victim of honour crimes. 
 
Submit copies of documents proving this. Submit supporting documents for all situations 
that apply to you.

 Copy of proof of beginning and end of marriage 
Were you married to the EU citizen? Submit copies of documents showing when you 
were married. For example, the marriage certificate. Also submit copies of documents 
showing when the divorce was filed. And copies of documents showing that the 
marriage ended and the date on which it ended. For example, a petition submitted to 
the court for a divorce and the divorce certificate. 
 
 Copy of proof of end of registered partnership  
Did you have a registered partnership with the EU citizen? Submit copies of documents 
showing that the registered partnership ended and the date on which it ended. For 
example, the termination agreement or the dissolution order of the court.  
 
 Copy of proof of end of relationship 
Were you not married and did you not have a registered partnership with the EU 
citizen? Then submit copies of documents showing that the relationship ended and the 
date on which it ended. And when the relationship started. For example, statements 
from yourself and/or others stating when the relationship ended. Or screenprints of 
contact between you and your partner via social media such as WhatsApp and Facebook 
showing that. 
 
 Copies of proof of income/sufficient resources  
The appendix Income documents for EU document (pdf, 197 kB) at ind.nl/en/proof-
income-EU lists which documents these are.  
 
 Copy of proof of parental authority 
Has it been determined that you have parental authority over a minor child or children 
of you and the EU citizen? Then submit copies of documents showing this. For example, 
an extract from the parental authority register or a court order. 
 
 Copy of proof of family relationship 
Do you have a minor child or minor children with the EU citizen? Then submit copies of 
documents showing that you and the EU citizen are the parents of the child or children. 
For example, a (combination of) birth certificate, deed of acknowledgement, adoption 
documents, or copy of a decision from a family court. 
 
 Copy of proof of distressing situation 
Was there a very special or difficult situation in your relationship with the EU citizen? 
Then submit copies of documents showing this. For example, a statement from you, the 
police report(s) or a statement from healthcare providers.

 Copy of passport or other travel document of the parent who has left

Submit copies of the pages with identity details and travel stamps. So not the blank

D. The EU citizen has died 
The EU citizen with whom you were living in the Netherlands has died. And at least 1 of 
the following situations applies to you: 
1. You were living in the Netherlands for at least 1 year when the EU citizen died. 
2. You are the child of the deceased EU citizen. And you want to finish your education in 
the Netherlands. 
3. You are the caring parent of the child (see 2) who wants to stay in the Netherlands to 
complete their education. You are a caring parent if you take care of your child every 
day.  
 
Submit copies of documents proving this. Submit supporting documents for the situation 
that applies to you. 
 
 Copy of proof of death of EU citizen 
Submit copies of documents showing that the EU citizen has died. For example, a death 
certificate.
