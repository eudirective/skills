An apostille is a stamp or sticker on a public document such as a birth certificate or diploma. The apostille confirms that the signature of the document is genuine and that the document was issued by an official body such as a school or municipality.

Sticker issued by a district court

In the Netherlands an apostille is a sticker issued by a district court. This sticker is placed on your document and bears the district court’s stamp and the signature of a court official. In other countries an apostille may be issued as a stamp, a sticker or a separate document.

Example of an apostille

E-apostille

An apostille can also be issued digitally. This is called an e-apostille. If you have a foreign document with an e-apostille, you can use your document in the Netherlands. The Netherlands does not yet issue e-apostilles. For a Dutch document you can only get a paper-based apostille.
