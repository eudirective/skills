Special Report | Equal treatment 2025

This report for the year 2025 provides an overview of the landmark cases that the Authority was called upon to handle. Furthermore, the investigation of this year’s complaints has highlighted legal and practical issues from the implementation of equal treatment legislation, with a particular focus on pregnant women, mothers and persons with disabilities, the fight against age discrimination, and the problems faced by persons with disabilities or chronic conditions in their dealings with private sector organisations.

Annual Report | Return of Third Country Nationals 2025

In taking a brief overview the year 2025, the Ombudsman oversaw all Joint European Return Operations (JRO) in which the Hellenic Police took part and were coordinated by Frontex to Pakistan, Georgia and Bangladesh, as well as most national forced return operations on commercial flights to Egypt, India and Côte d’Ivoire. Meanwhile, inspections continued at detention facilities for third country nationals awaiting return, pre-removal detention centres (PROKEKA), at migration management departments, police stations, border protection departments and police directorates, whilst all individual files of third country nationals awaiting return were reviewed.

Learn more

Annual Report | National Mechanism for the Investigation of Arbitrary Incidents 2025

The 2025 Annual Report of the National Mechanism for the Investigation of Arbitrary Incidents is structured similarly to the reports of the previous years, to facilitate the comparative and systematic review of the degree of compliance of the internal disciplinary bodies with the recommendations and findings of the Ombudsman’s Mechanism. At the same time, with a view to enhancing transparency, the 2025 report sheds light on groups of cases or independent incidents that have raised public concern. Special reference is made to cases involving the use of stun grenades, resulting in injuries to civilians.

The Greek Ombudsman Mr. Andreas Pottakis met with the European Border and Coast Guard Agency’s (Frontex) Executive Director Mr. Hans Leijtens in Warsaw on Monday, 20.10.2025.

Special Report | Sustainable Tourism Development: Framework - Infrastructure - Resources

The Special Report Sustainable Tourism Development: Framework - Infrastructure - Resources, attempts to approach critical issues, regarding tourism development and sustainability as implemented in Greece. It examines aspects of the framework for tourism development, assesses the state of infrastructure and the adequacy of natural resources, as well as the adequacy, readiness and effectiveness of the administrative mechanism. At the same time, it makes key proposals to ensure sustainable tourism development.

Press Release | Discriminatory refusal to provide services to Israeli citizens on grounds of nationality and/or national origin

The Greek Ombudsman, within the framework of its constitutional and statutory mandate as Greece’s national equality body, responsible for promoting and monitoring the implementation of the principle of equal treatment, expresses its serious concern regarding recurring allegations of refusal to provide services to Israeli citizens which, according to complaints received by the Authority, appear to be linked to the individuals’ nationality and/or national origin.
