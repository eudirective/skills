# Greece source-route sustainability assessment (updated 2026-10-19)

## Scope and evidence

The shared bounded capture rule attempted all 56 Greek candidates on 2026-10-19. Forty exact responses were converted and offline replay passed; 16 failed (14 HTTP 403 and 2 TLS certificate errors). Captures include legal texts, gazette issues, guidance, forms, court-search and oversight pages. No local copy is accepted. Human-reviewed official links are usable within their displayed scope and cutoff; no Greece-specific currentness adapter or full amendment inventory is needed for ordinary research.

The candidate manifest contains 56 records: 40 converted local-copy candidates and 0 local copies accepted. These counts define the bounded inventory and do not prove source-universe completeness. The 2018 amendment and many MFA, court, privacy and oversight URLs returned 403; two unrelated authority URLs failed TLS validation. PDF conversion emits a missing-fontTools warning for an embedded barcode font in the FEK 135/A issue, although the sample PD 106/2007 text is readable. Conversion quality still requires proportionate review for any retained copy.

## Summary

The generic retrieval/conversion rule is implemented and replayed for 40 records, but it is not a Greek currentness rule. The Gazette route is usable as a human-reviewed official reference for known records. Its recorded terms restrict local copying/conversion, so the library should link to the official publication unless a permitted basis for storing a conversion is established. This limits automated capture; it does not require a full amendment search or make the closest government-published text unusable. The direct `api.et.gr` endpoint's separate terms were not established in the recorded review.

## Repair priorities

1. **Use official texts directly for research.** Choose a government consolidation when available; otherwise use the nearest government-published edition, record its cutoff, and check only an identified material later change. Link to the official page when local copying terms are unclear.
2. **Build automation only if recurring monitoring is requested.** Before doing so, check that the chosen route permits the planned requests and storage. Do not require a documented API when a human-reviewed official page meets the research need, and do not reproduce the law from every Gazette amendment.
3. **Keep any conversion check proportional.** Confirm the publication identity and review only the text used. Use broader PDF/OCR comparison if the extraction visibly risks losing or duplicating relevant content; hashes and replay are optional when no repeatable capture pipeline is needed.
4. **For guidance, add parent-index rediscovery and visible-date capture.** Prioritize current MITOS and MFA records; treat service records as mutable content and verify all links and language variants. Acceptance condition: stable IDs, update signals, known local scope, and a review candidate rather than silent replacement.
5. **Resolve the Council of State public-search route and national adoption/foreign-judgment coverage.** Acceptance condition: first-party endpoint, public access model, corpus/search completeness limits and official process sources documented.

## Separate coverage limits

This review concerns proposed-source routes only. It does not rate mirror synchronization, establish current Greek law, decide legal equivalence, cover all Greek ministries/municipalities/police jurisdictions/consulates, or certify a complete courts/adoption source universe. EEA EFTA, Swiss, UK Withdrawal Agreement and Greek-national family routes remain distinct. Candidates remain unaccepted and no source conversion rule has been implemented.
