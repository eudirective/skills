# Greece source-route sustainability assessment (updated 2026-10-21)

## Scope and evidence

The shared bounded capture rule attempted the original 56 Greek candidates on 2026-10-19. Forty exact responses were converted and offline replay passed; 16 failed (14 HTTP 403 and 2 TLS certificate errors). Three records were added after review found Law 5275/2026 Article 53 amending PD 106/2007, Law 5226/2025 Article 28 setting return-procedure rules for EU-free-movement cases, and the limited transitional provisions in Law 4686/2020 Article 43. Six official legal references have now been reviewed by link, while no local copy is accepted. The evidence does not establish a government-published consolidated PD 106/2007 text or a complete amendment chain.

The candidate manifest contains 59 records: 40 converted local-copy review artifacts, three additional link-only legal sources, and 0 local copies accepted. These counts define the bounded inventory and do not prove source-universe completeness. The 2018 amendment and many MFA, court, privacy and oversight URLs returned 403; two unrelated authority URLs failed TLS validation. PDF conversion emits a missing-fontTools warning for an embedded barcode font in the FEK 135/A issue, although the sample PD 106/2007 text is readable. Conversion quality still requires proportionate review for any retained copy.

## Summary

The generic retrieval/conversion rule is implemented and replayed for 40 records, but it is not a Greek currentness rule. No government-published consolidation of PD 106/2007 was found in the reviewed routes: the National Codification Portal still describes implementation as in its final stage, and the Ministry legal index does not list Law 5275/2026. For specific points, use the closest official text and the targeted amendments actually checked, recording their dates and scope. The 2025 return law is directly relevant: Law 5226/2025 Article 28 routes EU-free-movement returns through PD 106/2007 Articles 22–24 and supplements these with rules on authorities, deadlines, service, guarantees and judicial protection. Law 5275/2026 Article 53 changes duration for specified Article 3(2) categories to a five-year Residence Card, effective from publication on 6 February 2026. Law 4686/2020 Article 43 applies only to identified legacy transitional permit situations. The Gazette records are usable as human-reviewed links; their copy/reuse terms do not need to be resolved for ordinary reference use. Do not treat the reviewed records as a complete consolidated decree or as proof of full legal coverage.

## Repair priorities

1. **Use official texts directly for research.** No public government consolidation of PD 106/2007 was found. Use the original decree plus targeted Articles 42, 32 and 53 amendments where they matter; record the cutoff and scope, and do not reconstruct every Gazette change. Link to the official page when local copying terms are unclear.
2. **Build automation only if recurring monitoring is requested.** Before doing so, check that the chosen route permits the planned requests and storage. Do not require a documented API when a human-reviewed official page meets the research need, and do not reproduce the law from every Gazette amendment.
3. **Keep any conversion check proportional.** Confirm the publication identity and review only the text used. Use broader PDF/OCR comparison if the extraction visibly risks losing or duplicating relevant content; hashes and replay are optional when no repeatable capture pipeline is needed.
4. **For guidance, add parent-index rediscovery and visible-date capture.** Prioritize current MITOS and MFA records; treat service records as mutable content and verify all links and language variants. Acceptance condition: stable IDs, update signals, known local scope, and a review candidate rather than silent replacement.
5. **Resolve the Council of State public-search route and national adoption/foreign-judgment coverage.** Acceptance condition: first-party endpoint, public access model, corpus/search completeness limits and official process sources documented.

## Separate coverage limits

This review concerns proposed-source routes only. It does not rate mirror synchronization, establish current Greek law, decide legal equivalence, cover all Greek ministries/municipalities/police jurisdictions/consulates, or certify a complete courts/adoption source universe. EEA EFTA, Swiss, UK Withdrawal Agreement and Greek-national family routes remain distinct. The six reviewed legal links are usable within their stated scopes, but no local source copy is accepted. The remaining candidates are not thereby accepted; no Greece-specific automated source rule or complete country coverage rule has been implemented.
