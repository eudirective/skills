# Greece source-route sustainability assessment (updated 2026-10-25)

## Scope and evidence

The original 56-source retrieval attempt on 2026-10-19 converted and replay-verified 40 texts; 16 requests failed (14 HTTP 403 and two TLS certificate errors). The 61-row source list now records 27 accepted official references by link, including six Greek MITOS procedures newly reviewed on 2026-10-25. Of the 40 published candidate files, 24 have accepted official-link dispositions and 16 have explicit duplicate, discovery-only, historical, out-of-scope, terms, or human-review dispositions. No local copy is accepted. The evidence does not establish a government-published consolidated PD 106/2007 text or a complete amendment chain.

The 61 source rows are a bounded inventory, not a complete source universe. The 40 converted bodies, candidate manifest, and retrieval ledger are preserved only in the Monitor local-only archive. The 2018 amendment and many MFA, court, privacy and oversight URLs returned 403; two unrelated authority URLs failed TLS validation. PDF conversion emits a missing-fontTools warning for an embedded barcode font in the FEK 135/A issue, although a sample PD 106/2007 text is readable. Because no local copies are accepted, these historical conversions do not become source documents.

## Summary

The generic capture/conversion method replays the 40 archived outputs but is not a Greek currentness rule. No government-published consolidation of PD 106/2007 was located in the reviewed routes. The acceptance register now covers 27 official links within narrow recorded scopes; it accepts no local copy. For legal propositions, use the closest official text and targeted amendment actually checked, with date and limits. Law 5226/2025 Article 28 addresses the PD 106/2007 return route and supplemental procedural safeguards; Law 5275/2026 Article 53 changes duration for specified Article 3(2) categories to a five-year Residence Card from 6 February 2026; Law 4686/2020 Article 43 is limited to named legacy cases. The National Printing House terms restrict copying/conversion on its search site; whether that applies to direct API PDFs remains unresolved. Do not describe the reviewed records as a full consolidation or proof of legal coverage.

## Current government procedure references and portal review

Eleven selected MITOS pages are accepted as dated link references: five EU-residence procedures (updated 21 or 28 September 2026), foreign-resident marriage licensing (2 September), Greek consular marriage registration (29 September), public-document access (14 October), legal aid (2 September), nationality following adoption (21 September), and nationality following child recognition (7 August). All are link-only; the archived English/Greek conversions remain unaccepted local artifacts.

Whole-portal human review is offered for the Gov.gr family-residence section because the Greek page bodies were not available in this research client and the English general TCN-family page is dated 28 November 2022; for the Ministry of Migration and Asylum family guidance because its EU-family page dates from February 2021 and the TCN page has no clear update date; and for Attica because its current Greek checklist still states a two-year duration for the stable-partner route despite Law 5275/2026 Article 53. These review offers do not block the dated MITOS links or independent source work.

## Repair priorities

1. **Use official texts directly for research.** No public government consolidation of PD 106/2007 was found. Use the original decree plus targeted Articles 42, 32 and 53 amendments where they matter; record the cutoff and scope, and do not reconstruct every Gazette change. Link to the official page when local copying terms are unclear.
2. **Build automation only if recurring monitoring is requested.** Before doing so, check that the chosen route permits the planned requests and storage. Do not require a documented API when a human-reviewed official page meets the research need, and do not reproduce the law from every Gazette amendment.
3. **Keep any conversion check proportional.** Confirm the publication identity and review only the text used. Use broader PDF/OCR comparison if the extraction visibly risks losing or duplicating relevant content; hashes and replay are optional when no repeatable capture pipeline is needed.
4. **For guidance, add parent-index rediscovery and visible-date capture.** Prioritize current MITOS and MFA records; treat service records as mutable content and verify all links and language variants. Acceptance condition: stable IDs, update signals, known local scope, and a review candidate rather than silent replacement.
5. **Resolve the Council of State public-search route and national adoption/foreign-judgment coverage.** Acceptance condition: first-party endpoint, public access model, corpus/search completeness limits and official process sources documented.

## Separate coverage limits

This review concerns source references and route sustainability only. It does not rate mirror synchronization, establish current Greek law, decide legal equivalence, cover all Greek ministries/municipalities/police jurisdictions/consulates, or certify a complete courts/adoption source universe. EEA EFTA, Swiss, UK Withdrawal Agreement and Greek-national family routes remain distinct. All 40 published candidate files now have documented dispositions: 24 are represented by accepted link-only references and 16 have explicit non-acceptance dispositions. No local source copy or Greece-specific automated source rule is accepted, and no complete country coverage claim is made.
