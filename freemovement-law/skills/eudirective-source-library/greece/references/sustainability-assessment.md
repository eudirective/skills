# Greece source-route sustainability assessment (updated 2026-10-19)

## Scope and evidence

The shared bounded capture rule attempted all 56 Greek candidates on 2026-10-19. Forty exact responses were converted and offline replay passed; 16 failed (14 HTTP 403 and 2 TLS certificate errors). Captures include legal texts, gazette issues, guidance, forms, court-search and oversight pages. No source is accepted. No Greece-specific currentness adapter or amendment-completeness rule exists.

The candidate manifest contains 56 records: 40 converted review candidates and 0 accepted. These counts define the bounded inventory and do not prove source-universe completeness. The 2018 amendment and many MFA, court, privacy and oversight URLs returned 403; two unrelated authority URLs failed TLS validation. PDF conversion emits a missing-fontTools warning for an embedded barcode font in the FEK 135/A issue, although the sample PD 106/2007 text is readable. Conversion quality still requires document-by-document review.

## Summary

The generic retrieval/conversion rule is implemented and replayed for 40 records, but it is not a Greek currentness rule. The Gazette PDF route works for known items, yet the National Printing House’s copying/conversion terms restrict use absent permission. The route is therefore not sustainable for operational monitoring until the publisher’s terms or an applicable legal basis are documented and amendment discovery is complete. The most material blocker is the Gazette search-site terms page, which restricts use to personal, informational, non-commercial purposes and prohibits reproduction/conversion absent permission. The direct `api.et.gr` endpoint may be treated separately, but this could not be established from reviewed publisher terms.

## Repair priorities

1. **Resolve National Printing House use terms before proposing automation.** Ask the publisher for terms covering api.et.gr downloads, non-commercial internal monitoring, retention of official Gazette bytes, and text conversion. Acceptance condition: written publisher terms or a documented license/API contract that explicitly supports the intended operations. If no permission is available, do not automate retrieval/conversion from that route.
2. **Establish legal-version discovery independently of a pinned Gazette URL.** Identify a publisher-maintained record or complete Gazette search/export that enumerates every amendment affecting PD 106/2007 and other chosen laws. Acceptance condition: a repeatable record-to-amendment chain with stable identifiers and a documented way to detect new issues, plus a reviewed current consolidated representation.
3. **Only then prototype capture and conversion.** Save exact authorized bytes, validate PDF signature and issue/law identity, preserve hashes and provenance, cap response size, replay conversion offline, and fail closed when text/identity differs. Acceptance condition: positive/negative official-response fixtures, comparison against source pages, and reproducible outputs.
4. **For guidance, add parent-index rediscovery and visible-date capture.** Prioritize current MITOS and MFA records; treat service records as mutable content and verify all links and language variants. Acceptance condition: stable IDs, update signals, known local scope, and a review candidate rather than silent replacement.
5. **Resolve the Council of State public-search route and national adoption/foreign-judgment coverage.** Acceptance condition: first-party endpoint, public access model, corpus/search completeness limits and official process sources documented.

## Separate coverage limits

This review concerns proposed-source routes only. It does not rate mirror synchronization, establish current Greek law, decide legal equivalence, cover all Greek ministries/municipalities/police jurisdictions/consulates, or certify a complete courts/adoption source universe. EEA EFTA, Swiss, UK Withdrawal Agreement and Greek-national family routes remain distinct. Candidates remain unaccepted and no source conversion rule has been implemented.
