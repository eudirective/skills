## Findings

- The National Printing House provides public search pages, and direct `api.et.gr` PDF URLs returned the original 2007 free-movement decree, the 2012 amendment issue, and administrative-procedure Gazette documents without an account in this research browser. This proves only that the selected records were reachable, not that an acquisition API is documented, complete or permitted for automated conversion.
- The official Gazette identifies PD 106/2007 as implementing Directive 2004/38. MITOS links Law 4071/2012 and Law 4540/2018 as amendments, but the complete amendment chain and a current consolidated text were not established.
- The `search.et.gr` terms page restricts site use to personal, informational, non-commercial purposes and says copying/conversion without permission is prohibited. Whether that restriction governs direct `api.et.gr` PDFs and proposed internal automated monitoring is unresolved; no rule should be based on this route until terms and rights are clarified.
- The National Codification Portal states that implementation remains in its final stage. MITOS provides recent, detailed process records, but they are dynamic administrative procedures rather than a complete consolidated legal source. Ministry guidance includes older, potentially stale fees and document requirements.
- MFA’s central visa hubs coexist with mission-specific material. The current missions directory path redirected to a landing page; one family-member page is dated 2019. Residence handling also varies by competent local authority.
- The Ombudsman, FRO, Hellenic DPA and NTA all expose official complaint routes with materially different scope. The FRO route concerns access to territory, reception and asylum; none is a general substitute for statutory/judicial review. Case-law coverage and intercountry-adoption recognition remain incomplete.

The [sustainability assessment](sustainability-assessment.md) evaluates only the proposed official routes. No Greek acquisition rule is implemented and no candidate is accepted.
