CONSULAR JURISDICTION

The Embassy of the Czech Republic in Ankara provides consular services to persons who reside in these provinces: Adana, Adıyaman, Afyonkarahisar, Ağrı, Aksaray, Amasya, Ankara, Antalya, Ardahan, Artvin, Bartın, Batman, Bayburt, Bingöl, Bitlis, Bolu, Burdur, Çankırı, Çorum, Diyarbakır, Düzce, Elazığ, Erzincan, Erzurum, Eskişehir, Gaziantep, Giresun, Gümüşhane, Hakkâri, Hatay, Iğdır, Isparta, Kahramanmaraş, Karabük, Karaman, Kars, Kastamonu, Kayseri, Kilis, Kırıkkale, Kırşehir, Konya, Malatya, Mardin, Mersin, Muş, Nevşehir, Niğde, Ordu, Osmaniye, Rize, Samsun, Şanlıurfa, Siirt, Sinop, Şırnak, Sivas, Tokat, Trabzon, Tunceli, Van, Yozgat, Zonguldak. Other Turkish provinces fall under the jurisdiction of the Consulate General of the Czech Republic in Istanbul.

OFFICE HOURS FOR PUBLIC

Monday - Thursday, 9 a.m. - 12 p.m. and 1 - 2 p.m.

Friday, 8 a.m. - 2 p.m.

To submit a long-term visa / residence permit application, schedule appointment.

To submit a Schengen visa application, contact the visa center in Ankara or Antalya – based on your place of residence.

Applicants submit Schengen visa applications in visa centers. Directly at consulate apply only family members of EU citizens, holders of diplomatic or service (official) passports, members of official delegations, or persons travelling in urgent humanitarian situations. These categories of applicants can still choose to submit their applications in visa centers. To submit a Schengen visa application directly at consulate, schedule you appointment by e-mail at Ankara.Consulate@mzv.gov.cz.

Family members of EU citizens can submit their applications every Wednesday, 9:30 a.m. - 12 p.m. – we prefer that you schedule your appointment by e-mail at consulate_ankara@mzv.gov.cz.

Legalization: during office hours based on appointment scheduled by e-mail: consulate_ankara@mzv.gov.cz

Matrimonial matters, travel documents, certified copy of a document: based on pre-arranged appointment via email only - ankara.consulate@mzv.gov.cz.
