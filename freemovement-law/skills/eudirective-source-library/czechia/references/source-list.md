# Czechia — national legal-source candidates for free movement and family members

**Inventory date:** 19 October 2026. **Status:** 43 candidate records: 17 current legal texts, 13 official guidance/index pages, and 13 forms, mission, court, and oversight discovery pages have saved unaccepted review candidates. All 43 conversions pass offline replay; 0 are accepted. **Scope:** Czech national legislation, official authorities, courts, and oversight materials relevant to EU/EEA/Swiss free movement, residence, EU-family visa processing, civil-status evidence, procedure, and remedies. EU legislation and CJEU judgments are excluded because they belong in the EU core. Primary legal texts are in Czech; official English guidance is marked separately.

Use e-Sbírka's date/version history for the temporal law applicable to a particular event. Its current-text pages label the consolidated text as informative. Keep publication, entry into force, applicability, end of validity, and operational commencement distinct. The inventory is a scoped starting list, not a claim of completeness. The legal adapter uses e-Sbírka's public page-data service to resolve each stable act record to the current effective version, capture its ordered XHTML fragments, and produce a replay-verified text candidate; it does not use the registration-gated REST API. Exact version metadata and response hashes are in `references/esbirka-open-data-captures.json`. These conversions remain review-only until the applicable version and official promulgation are reconciled.
