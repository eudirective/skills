# Czechia — national legal-source candidates for free movement and family members

**Inventory date:** 15 October 2026. **Status:** candidate acquisition list only. These URLs are official starting points, not evidence that source bytes were retrieved, converted, hashed, or accepted. **Scope:** Czech national legislation, official authorities, courts, and oversight materials relevant to EU movement, residence, EU-family visa processing, civil-status evidence, procedure, and remedies. EU legislation and CJEU judgments are excluded because they belong in the EU core. Primary legal texts are in Czech; official English guidance is marked separately.

Use e-Sbírka's date/version history for the temporal law applicable to a particular event. Its current-text pages label the consolidated text as informative. Keep publication, entry into force, applicability, end of validity, and operational commencement distinct. The inventory is a scoped starting list, not a claim of completeness.

## 1. Constitutional, free-movement residence and visa framework

- **`cz-esbirka-1993-1` — Act No. 1/1993 Coll., Constitution of the Czech Republic** — constitutional rights, public authority, and access to courts; source types: constitutional text. Topics: `FOUND-02`, `ADMIN-01`, `REMEDY-03`. [Official e-Sbírka record](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1993/1).
- **`cz-esbirka-1999-326` — Act No. 326/1999 Coll., on the Residence of Foreign Nationals in the Territory of the Czech Republic** — principal national entry, visa, residence, family-member, residence-document, and authority-competence statute; source type: national legislation. Topics: `RIGHTS-02`, `RIGHTS-03`, `VISA-01`–`VISA-08`, `RESIDENCE-02`–`RESIDENCE-09`, `ADMIN-01`, `REMEDY-02`–`REMEDY-03`, `MISSION-18`–`MISSION-29`. e-Sbírka search results identified a current version from 12 June 2026, incorporating Act No. 314/2025 Coll.; verify the current page and amendments at retrieval. [Official e-Sbírka record](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1999/326).

## Verification notes

The e-Sbírka pages for the Residence of Foreign Nationals Act and Administrative Procedure Code were located on the official host; search evidence reported Act 326/1999 Coll. current from 12 June 2026 and Act 500/2004 Coll. current from 1 July 2025. Act 301/2000 Coll. search evidence showed a future version from 1 January 2027 as well as a current version from 1 January 2025, so monitoring must keep effective dates distinct. Other legal candidates use the official e-Sbírka stable record pattern but still need retrieval-time identity/version verification.

Official English guidance was located for the MOI Information Portal, MFA, and Public Administration Portal. The older English MOI page at `mv.gov.cz/mvcren/article/eu-citizens-and-their-family-members.aspx` redirects to an archive and states that it was under revision; use the newer IPC portal targets above and preserve old pages only as historical guidance if needed.
