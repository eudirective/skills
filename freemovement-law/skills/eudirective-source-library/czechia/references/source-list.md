# Czechia — national legal-source candidates for free movement and family members

**Inventory date:** 21 October 2026. **Status:** 63 records: 62 official external references are accepted within their recorded scopes (17 dated e-Sbírka law links, the 26 previously inventoried official guidance/form/discovery links, 15 additional Czech-language government guidance/service pages, and 4 individual official court decisions); Decree 429/2010 remains pending whole-portal human review. No retained local copy or topic is accepted, and country review remains incomplete. The 43 existing local conversions pass offline replay, which establishes reproducibility rather than legal currentness or semantic accuracy. See the [source acceptance register](source-acceptance.json). **Scope:** Czech national legislation, official authorities, courts, and oversight materials relevant to EU/EEA/Swiss free movement, residence, EU-family visa processing, civil-status evidence, procedure, and remedies. EU legislation and CJEU judgments are excluded because they belong in the EU core. Primary legal texts are in Czech; official English guidance is marked separately.

Use e-Sbírka's date/version history for the temporal law applicable to a particular event. Its current-text pages label the consolidated text as informative. Keep publication, entry into force, applicability, end of validity, and operational commencement distinct. The inventory is a scoped starting list, not a claim of completeness. The legal adapter uses e-Sbírka's public page-data service to resolve each stable act record to the current effective version, capture its ordered XHTML fragments, and produce a replay-verified text candidate; it does not use the registration-gated REST API. Exact version metadata and response hashes are in `references/esbirka-open-data-captures.json`. The 17 official e-Sbírka source references are accepted within the dates and limits recorded in `references/source-acceptance.json`; their local Markdown conversions remain review-only. No Gazette history is required by default.
