What if the complaint relates to a child or person under 18 years?

If the complaint relates to a child or person under 18 years who has been adversely affected by
an action, or inaction, of the accommodation centre or the IPAS, you should complain to the
Ombudsman for Children’s Office (OCO).

If you are unsure – for example, the complaint might relate to a person who is now over 18 but
who was under 18 at the time the issue arose –  contact either the Office of the Ombudsman or
the OCO for advice.
