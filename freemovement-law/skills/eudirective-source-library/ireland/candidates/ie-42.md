Raising a concern with the commission

In today’s business world most organisations take data protection very seriously, and the majority of issues are resolved without ever needing to raise a concern with the DPC. However, if you have contacted an organisation about a personal data concern, in keeping with the guidance provided in our ‘Know Your Rights’ section and you are unhappy with the outcome, you can raise the matter with the Data Protection Commission through our online form .
