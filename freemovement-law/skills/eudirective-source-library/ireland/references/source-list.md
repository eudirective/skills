## Material review notes

- eISB local texts are exact made/enacted instruments, not a reconstructed consolidated code. The separate SI 548 amendment index and later 2021/2025 instruments are retained as distinct records.
- Current Law Reform Commission revised-act pages are linked for the Immigration Act 2004, FOI Act 2014 and Data Protection Act 2018; they are administrative consolidations with their own cutoff and disclaimer.
- ISD currently directs EUTR1/EUTR1A applicants to the Customer Service Portal; paper forms are requested there. The static 2023 EUTR1 PDF and August 2025 explanatory leaflet are not the current default workflow.
- ISD’s current Treaty Rights page says a decision can take up to six months. The gov.ie page updated 11 April 2025 says 12 months; it remains accepted only as a dated page, with the conflict open for department-level reconciliation.
- The 2026 ISD appeal page says ordinary short-stay refusal appeals ended on 1 June 2026 but EU Free Movement Directive cases remain appealable.
- All 26 original captured responses, all 26 deterministic candidate conversions, and the retrieval manifest are indexed in the Monitor local-only archive. No pack-local `candidates/` or retrieval manifest is retained.
- Open coverage items: mission-specific requirements, individual court judgments, precise current operative scope of the 1977/1997 EEA instruments, legal-aid eligibility in EU Treaty Rights/judicial-review matters, and foreign-status/adoption documentation. These are not certified by this bounded candidate review.
