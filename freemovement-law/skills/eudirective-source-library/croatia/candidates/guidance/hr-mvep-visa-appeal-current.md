## Current official-page summary

As displayed on the review date, the page says a visa refusal, annulment or revocation appeal is filed through the competent embassy/consulate within 15 days of service; it must be written in Croatian, with an English exception if no authorized Croatian court interpreter is available in the recipient country. It identifies the MVEP Visa Service as decision-maker, gives a 60-day decision period, lists an administrative appeal charge of €130 or USD 150.71, and identifies a 30-day court-claim period after the second-instance decision at the Zagreb Administrative Court. It cites Foreigners Act NN 133/20, 114/22, 151/22, 40/25 and 55/26, GAPA NN 47/09 and 110/21, and Visa Ordinance NN 109/21.

The Foreigners Act NN 133/20 Article 32(3)–(6) provides for delivery of reasons, a 15-day appeal through a diplomatic mission/consular post, decision by the foreign-affairs ministry, and no automatic suspensive effect. MUP's current `Propisi` index lists the amendment chain through NN 55/26.
