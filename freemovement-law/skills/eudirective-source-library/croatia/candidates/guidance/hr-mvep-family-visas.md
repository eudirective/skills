## Current official-page summary

MVEP says that a qualifying non-EEA family member with a valid passport and the relevant Union-family residence card may travel without a short-stay visa when accompanying or joining the EEA/Swiss family member. Where a C visa is needed for accompanying or joining, MVEP describes a priority appointment at the embassy/consulate, expedited handling, no visa application fee, and a minimum document set: visa form, valid passport, photograph, and proof of the family relationship. The Croatian counterpart makes the free-movement condition explicit. Neither page is a substitute for the EEA Act or Directive 2004/38.
