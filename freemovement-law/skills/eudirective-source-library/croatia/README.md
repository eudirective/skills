## Main findings

- The dedicated EEA citizens Act is the primary free-movement route. It expressly extends citizen provisions to Swiss nationals and includes a specific scope rule for EEA-national family members of Croatian citizens. Do not collapse it into the general Foreigners Act.
- The Interior Ministry’s current legislation index records the EEA Act and implementing regulation amendment chains. The EEA guidance page was last updated 9 October 2025 and links to forms 1b, 2b, 3b, and 6b.
- Narodne novine publishes stable ELI records, HTML and PDF, an XML sitemap, and metadata routes. Its documented API is for structured metadata, not full text; the publisher advises no more than three requests per second and states that content is copyrighted. This is promising for discovery but does not authorize mirroring or establish a retrieval/conversion rule.
- An official MVEP legacy FAQ still says Croatia is not in Schengen, while the current MVEP visa overview says Croatia joined on 1 January 2023. That stale page is recorded as a quality-control lead and must not drive a rule.
- General procedure, judicial review, Ombudsman, information-access, data-protection, legal-aid, and court-search routes are separate candidates with their own coverage and currentness limits.

All entries remain candidates. The generic bounded retrieval/conversion rule has now been exercised on 40 records; it does not validate amendment completeness or establish a reuse right. No source is accepted.
