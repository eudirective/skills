# Croatia source discovery register

**Skill:** author-source-pack-rules v1.0.1
**Research date:** 25 October 2026
**Target:** Croatia, EU Member State. Compared with earlier free-movement and source-taxonomy findings from Denmark, Sweden, Czechia, Hungary, and Romania; carried forward publisher, remedy, privacy, and sustainability issues from Austria, Belgium, and Bulgaria.
**Languages:** Croatian official sources; English official guidance where available.
**Scope:** legislation and guidance for free movement and third-country family members, visas, forms, general procedure, evidence, administrative remedies, privacy, oversight, legal aid, and courts. A located lead is not accepted and does not establish legal equivalence.

## Acquisition, archive and review outcome (source capture: 19 October 2026; link review: 25 October 2026)

The initial bounded HTTPS batch covered 41 seed records: 40 exact responses converted and replayed offline; `hr-e-predmet` returned an application/search surface without a stable text body. Twenty-five Narodne novine legal texts have selector-bound `.sl-content .doc` conversions with independent ordered-visible-text review, and four MUP EEA forms have page-by-page native-PDF review. Four SIS/VIS forms were checked by complete text-layer extraction, page counts and PDF properties; their page images were not independently visually inspected. The pack now has 43 accepted local documents: 25 Croatian legal-text documents, eight MUP/MVEP forms and ten guidance article texts. Nine source-scoped authority article bodies or subsections from the Monitor archive are accepted after source-specific page and reuse review, including two historical-only records; the AZOP SIS rights excerpt was rechecked on 25 October and excludes the remainder of its mixed SIS/VIS page. Seven have archived raw snapshots matching their reviewed source URLs; an earlier raw family-page response for retired ID 180456 is also preserved, while the later current-ID family capture and appeal capture are hash-only. The Monitor archive contains 113 indexed Croatia records: 25 source fragments, 51 HTML source responses, four direct image captures, eight discovery notes, 22 candidate notes, and three acquisition manifests. Archive paths and hashes are recorded in source-acceptance.json; no candidates, captures or acquisition manifests remain in this country pack. The 41 official references remain usable by link within recorded scopes; link acceptance remains separate from local-copy acceptance, topic coverage and country completion. Current MUP version indexes and legal-text cutoffs are recorded separately. Article 18(3) of Copyright Act NN 111/21 is applied to individually identified published official text and, narrowly, to the one unchanged MVEP VIS form. MUP’s Terms of Use permit attribution for the seven unchanged MUP forms and the identified EEA guidance body; AZOP’s terms separately allow attributed republication of the named complaint article and SIS rights subsection without changing meaning. The seven MVEP/Ombudsman/Commissioner article copies use page-specific statutory inferences under the official-publication clause, not a site-wide licence. Page shells, third-party material, forms, unreviewed site content and bulk/database-wide copies are excluded.

## Bounded-search limitations

- The closest official government-published versions and listed updates are recorded; use those versions with their dates and known limits. Do not reconstruct the full amendment chain merely to create a consolidated text.
- Third-country 2026 regulation records, MVEP family-specific visa guidance, the full missions/VAC set, current privacy and court coverage were not all directly inspected. Use the closest government text for each source; no complete amendment-chain compilation is required.
- No applicant-specific forms, visa submissions, complaints, or case materials were collected.
- “No candidate identified” was not used as a conclusion that a source does not exist.
