# Croatia source discovery register

**Skill:** author-source-pack-rules v1.0.1  
**Research date:** 18 October 2026  
**Target:** Croatia, EU Member State. Compared with earlier free-movement and source-taxonomy findings from Denmark, Sweden, Czechia, Hungary, and Romania; carried forward publisher, remedy, privacy, and sustainability issues from Austria, Belgium, and Bulgaria.  
**Languages:** Croatian official sources; English official guidance where available.  
**Scope:** legislation and guidance for free movement and third-country family members, visas, forms, general procedure, evidence, administrative remedies, privacy, oversight, legal aid, and courts. A candidate is not accepted and does not establish legal equivalence.

## Capture outcome (2026-10-19)

The shared bounded HTTPS rule attempted all 41 candidates. Forty exact responses converted and replayed offline; `hr-e-predmet` returned an application/search surface without a stable text body. No source was accepted. This confirms retrievability for the recorded responses, not currentness of every amendment chain or permission to reuse copyrighted Gazette text. Details are in [`croatia-capture-results.json`](croatia-capture-results.json).

## Bounded-search limitations

- The exact current consolidated wording of the EEA Act and implementing regulation was not reconstructed; the amendment chain is recorded.
- Third-country 2026 regulation direct records, MVEP family-specific visa guidance, the full missions/VAC set, Ombudsman amendment chain, current privacy-law chain, and full court corpus were not all directly inspected.
- No applicant-specific forms, visa submissions, complaints, or case materials were collected.
- “No candidate identified” was not used as a conclusion that a source does not exist.
