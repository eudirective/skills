# Croatia source discovery register

**Skill:** author-source-pack-rules v1.0.1
**Research date:** 21 October 2026
**Target:** Croatia, EU Member State. Compared with earlier free-movement and source-taxonomy findings from Denmark, Sweden, Czechia, Hungary, and Romania; carried forward publisher, remedy, privacy, and sustainability issues from Austria, Belgium, and Bulgaria.
**Languages:** Croatian official sources; English official guidance where available.
**Scope:** legislation and guidance for free movement and third-country family members, visas, forms, general procedure, evidence, administrative remedies, privacy, oversight, legal aid, and courts. A candidate is not accepted and does not establish legal equivalence.

## Capture outcome (source capture: 19 October 2026; link review: 21 October 2026)

The shared bounded HTTPS rule attempted the 41 seed candidates. Forty exact responses converted and replayed offline; `hr-e-predmet` returned an application/search surface without a stable text body. Twenty-five Narodne novine legal documents have since been replayed through `.sl-content .doc`, with page shell excluded and official-text fragments stored under `captures/official-texts/`. An independent ordered-visible-text audit passes for all 25. Seventeen of 76 source records now pass local-artifact acceptance; this does not establish topic coverage or country completion. The Free Legal Aid Act chain, EEA and Foreigners Act chains, General Administrative Procedure Act chain, and Administrative Disputes Act were accepted after their official authority indexes and Gazette sources were checked. The 2026 budget execution act was captured and independently replayed; the official Ministry of Finance page identifies it as the 2026 act, and the bounded exact-title search found no amending act. NN 142/25 was captured and accepted as a 2025-only provider-compensation regulation; it is not the 2026 rate. The MVEP family-visa and visa-appeal pages, three current MUP pages, five EEA/third-country legal source sets and the Constitutional Court decision NN 39/26 are now separately logged as eleven usable official references within recorded scopes. MUP’s live index links the listed official Gazette versions but does not provide an integrated consolidation; the relevant MUP, Narodne novine and Central Catalogue portals are offered for human review of any consolidated government version elsewhere. The local MVEP copies remain unaccepted; link review does not promote a local artifact. The copyright basis for individual officially published legal texts is recorded in [`reuse-policy.json`](reuse-policy.json); it does not cover page shells, third-party material, or database-wide reuse. Details are in [`croatia-capture-results.json`](croatia-capture-results.json) and [`capture-ledger.json`](capture-ledger.json).

## Bounded-search limitations

- The closest official government-published versions and listed updates are recorded; use those versions with their dates and known limits. Do not reconstruct the full amendment chain merely to create a consolidated text.
- Third-country 2026 regulation records, MVEP family-specific visa guidance, the full missions/VAC set, current privacy and court coverage were not all directly inspected. Use the closest government text for each source; no complete amendment-chain compilation is required.
- No applicant-specific forms, visa submissions, complaints, or case materials were collected.
- “No candidate identified” was not used as a conclusion that a source does not exist.
