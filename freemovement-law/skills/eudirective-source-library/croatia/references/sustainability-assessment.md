- **Current legal texts and index:** The Croatian MUP Propisi index was rechecked on 21 October 2026 and lists the identified versions/amendments for the EEA Act, EEA residence regulation, Foreigners Act through NN 55/26, third-country residence regulation through NN 43/26, and third-country procedure regulation through NN 116/26. Use a current government-published consolidation when available. The MUP index is a version-checking aid, not consolidated statutory wording. Do not manually assemble every listed Gazette fragment just to create a consolidated text; use the closest government-published text and record the resulting limit.
- **Conflicting index copy:** A separate older MUP page omits NN 55/26 and NN 116/26. This stale copy and the resolution are recorded in `currentness-review.json`; the current Propisi page is used as the live version index.
- **Copyright basis:** Article 18(3) of the Croatian Copyright and Related Rights Act NN 111/21 says officially published legislative, administrative and judicial texts cease to be protected by copyright when published for official public information. The Narodne novine terms apply a general copyright rule to its website content subject to applicable law. The pack therefore allows reuse of individual official text only and excludes the page shell, third-party material, non-text assets unless reviewed, and database-wide or unrestricted bulk reuse. Forty-five official legal-text records carry this scoped reuse basis.
- **Reproducible legal text:** Twenty-five Narodne novine documents have been converted using `.sl-content .doc`; the selected official document fragments are stored under `captures/official-texts/` with source and conversion hashes. An independent ordered-visible-text audit passes for all 25. The replay utility is deterministic from the captured fragments. Full publisher HTML responses stay out of Git. A prior replay-marker defect duplicated each section; it was corrected and all 25 candidates were regenerated and audited.
- **Semantic and attachment checks:** The twenty-five conversions pass ordered visible-text equivalence against their retained official fragments, along with checks for issue identifiers, Article headings and removal of known page-shell text. This verifies extraction fidelity, not source currency, legal classification or topic completeness. Seventeen sources also pass the separate full source-acceptance gate. Nineteen captured texts have no embedded image/file attachments. Six documents contain 160 embedded images; they are inventoried, but visual review remains open.

## Completion rule

A human-reviewed official source reference is usable within its declared scope after confirming publisher, identity, language, displayed version/cutoff, relevant content, and visible caveats. A locally retained or converted copy needs proportionate fidelity, attachment, and reuse checks; automation needs a suitable permitted route. Use the closest government-published text when no usable consolidation exists and state its limits; a full Gazette chain is not a default gate. A topic is covered only when relevant law, procedure, remedies, and practice have usable support or explicit gaps. Croatia is complete only when material topic gaps have final dispositions.
