## Findings

- The Federal Justice Ministry says the electronic BGBl at `recht.bund.de` has been the authentic federal promulgation organ since 1 January 2023. N-Lex’s Germany profile appears stale when it describes printed BGBl as the only official version.
- BGBl ELI/RSS and issue PDF/ZIP routes are promising, but no package replay, seal validation, extraction or conversion rule was implemented. Gesetze im Internet consolidations are useful but not authentic promulgation.
- FreizügG/EU shows an amendment dated 16 July 2026. AufenthG results show a 22 July amendment with at least one not-yet-fully-edited text caveat.
- The AA ended voluntary visa remonstration worldwide on 1 July 2025. Consular Services Portal availability depends on mission; residence-card practice depends on Land/local authority.
- Hesse and Bremen service pages are regional examples only. Swiss routes remain separate from EU/EEA. Adoption and civil-status sources are adjacent categories, not EU-family residence rules.

The [sustainability assessment](sustainability-assessment.md) records route feasibility and missing implementation evidence. A source URL is not an implemented retrieval rule and no candidate is accepted.
