An official website of the Federal Republic of Germany

Third-country nationals who are family members of an EU or EEA citizen require a residence card for their further stay in Germany no later than three months after entering the country.

Unfortunately this specification of service has not yet been completely translated.

Please select where you would like to apply for the service:

Information about the service depends on the selected region. You can change the region. In most cases, you can specify your place of residence or select the place where you want to use the service.
