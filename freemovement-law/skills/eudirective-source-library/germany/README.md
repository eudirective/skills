# Germany source library

**Status (24 October 2026):** The inventory contains 47 source records. Forty-two official references are accepted within recorded narrow roles. Eighteen accepted current-law source records correspond to 17 unique dated local statutory texts because the § 28 AufenthV locator (de-10) is represented by the full AufenthV text (de-09). Five proposals remain open for human review. The accepted-source count does not establish topic coverage or country completion.

The accepted local texts are under [`documents/legislation/`](documents/legislation/). Their paths, hashes, publisher build dates and limits are in [`references/source-acceptance.json`](references/source-acceptance.json) and [`references/candidate-manifest.json`](references/candidate-manifest.json). Currentness after the recorded publisher build dates has not been certified.

The source inventory and recorded scope for each official link are in [`references/source-list.md`](references/source-list.md). Five unresolved items are de-03 (publisher webservice instructions), de-34 (Hesse service record updated in 2020), de-36 (older BAMF guide), de-41 and de-42 (BfDI pages not directly reviewed). The de-31 handbook reference is accepted, but its extracted local text remains unaccepted and is retained only in the Monitor local-only archive.

No topic is marked covered and Germany review is incomplete. Mission and Land procedures, case-law discovery, dependencies, and operational evidence still require separate work.

The Monitor has no registered Germany pack action. Pack-level automated runtime checks and acceptance gates are therefore unsupported; this review records manual source decisions only.
