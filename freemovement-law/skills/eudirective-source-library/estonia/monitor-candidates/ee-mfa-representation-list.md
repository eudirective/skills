Countries where Estonia is represented by another country when issuing visas

In a country where Estonia does not have a foreign mission, another Schengen Member State may represent Estonia when issuing visas. Only applications for Schengen visas can be submitted to the representation of the Member State representing Estonia. For more information on the documents to be submitted for visa applications, please contact the Embassy of the Member State representing Estonia. In order to apply for long-stay visas, you must still contact the nearest Estonian foreign mission.

Azerbaijan (Baku)

Estonia is represented in issuing visas to Estonia by Latvia.

Persons that would like to travel to Estonia as main destination for private trips or business should present along with other required documents an original invitation letter certified by a notary public.

The invitation must be in English or Russian and contain the following information - name, surname, date of birth, number of passport, address in Azerbaijan of the person, who has been invited; place of stay in Estonia; scheduled time of the trip; main purpose of the visit.

For business trips a copy of the co-operation agreement should be presented if applicable.

Invitation letter from state institution should be on the official form of the institution duly signed and stamped (certification by notary is not needed).

For more detailed information concerning additional documents to be submitted for visa application please contact the Embassy of Latvia.
