## Remaining review

The official page for the [EU-citizen residence procedure regulation](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/akt/125042025013) presents its consolidated wording as current from 1 January 2026. A refreshed XML record has `dokumentStaatus=esitatud avaldamiseks`; that field’s status meaning is unresolved. Keep the official page available for human review and the local conversion unaccepted until the displayed version is reconciled.

The VSS also refers to the Administrative Procedure Act, except for entry-ban procedure. Current Estonian procedure/remedy provisions, forms, mission-specific visa instructions, residence guidance, oversight, legal aid, and relevant case law remain under review. The 42-record register is bounded and does not claim complete Estonian coverage.

For ordinary research, use the official publisher’s displayed consolidated version and record its date and limits. Full Gazette-chain reconstruction and a retained local conversion are not prerequisites for citing an official link.
