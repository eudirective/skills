# Romania national source pack

**Status (2026-10-19):** 79 monitored national-source targets in the main manifest; 71 produce reviewed Markdown, and eight are monitor-only. A separate guidance manifest tracks 10 additional authority sources. The pack is not an exhaustive inventory of Romanian law.

- Romanian EU-language mirror: [core/README.md](core/README.md)
- Reviewed national documents: [documents/README.md](documents/README.md)
- National inventory: [manifest.json](references/manifest.json)

- Human-readable source list: [source-list.md](references/source-list.md)

National legislation, Romanian court decisions, and Romanian authority material belong under `documents/`. The country-level `core/` contains only official EU-language representations. Successful retrieval or conversion does not establish legal force, currentness, completeness, or suitability for a particular question; check the responsible official publisher and version history before reliance.
