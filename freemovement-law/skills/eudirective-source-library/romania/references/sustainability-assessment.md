# Romania source sustainability assessment

**Reviewed:** 21 October 2026

**Result:** official consolidated references are usable within recorded dates and scope; legal-source discovery, topic adequacy, and country review remain incomplete.

## Source and reference state

The existing pack lists 79 national-source targets: 71 have reviewed Markdown conversions and eight are monitor-only. A separate guidance manifest has 10 authority sources. The repository contains 81 national and guidance document files. The pack's earlier notes call the conversions reviewed, but there was no current per-source acceptance register. This review therefore does not newly accept local copies; their source identity, currentness, legal classification, semantic fidelity and attachments must be reconciled if they are retained as accepted artifacts.

Eight official Portal Legislativ links are now recorded for scoped external use: the principal EU free-movement act, its implementing act and republished rules, the general foreigners act, administrative litigation, petitions, public legal aid, and one Constitutional Court decision. The Portal Legislativ records identify their instruments and show consolidation histories. The displayed cutoffs range from 7 June 2022 to 27 April 2026; they are usable government-published texts at those displayed versions, not a certification that no later relevant change exists. Check a specific later change only when it may materially affect the issue. No API, local copy, Gazette-chain reconstruction, hash, replay or conversion is needed for ordinary link use.

OUG nr. 51/2008 was missing from the existing Romanian source list. Its official consolidated page displays 3 November 2024; Articles 1–5 identify the access-to-justice purpose, qualifying applicants including residents of another EU Member State, administrative matters, and the financial-needs test. It is accepted as a link to the act, but no legal-aid eligibility or application topic is complete. CCR Decision nr. 664/2025, published 2 April 2026, is recorded narrowly as a Law nr. 554/2004 Article 9 decision; the challenge was rejected and the case is not migration-specific.

## Whole-site human review offer

Law nr. 544/2001 and HG nr. 123/2002 remain in the inventory, but the research viewer returned 502 for both individual Portal Legislativ records and timed out on the portal root. Other individual pages on that same portal loaded and exposed their title, content and consolidation history. The whole official Portal Legislativ site is therefore offered for human review of those FOI records, their displayed versions and the text used for current procedure. This is a bounded site review, not a reason to treat the sources as missing or to reconstruct every Gazette amendment.

## Sustainability of the existing workflow

The Romania skill already allows official-link use without the local update pipeline. Its batch retrieval, conversion, hash and replay steps apply only when maintaining retained local copies; they are not prerequisites for research. That is the sustainable split for these sources. The fixed 79-target inventory and the checks against its listed titles cannot discover every omitted law, new judgment, new mission instruction or changed administrative route. The issue-led coverage register now records those as open research rather than adding them to an automatic download queue.

The current manifest remains unchanged at 79 national targets plus the separate 10-source guidance lane. The two link-only additions (OUG nr. 51/2008 and CCR Decision nr. 664/2025) are documented outside the compiled manifest, so they do not create unnecessary local copies or recurring retrieval work.
