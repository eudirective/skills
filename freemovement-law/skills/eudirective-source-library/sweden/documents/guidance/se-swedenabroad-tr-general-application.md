How to apply?




If you want to visit Sweden and are a citizen of a country outside the EU, you may need a visa.

The list of countries whose citizens require a visa to visit Sweden

How to apply 

To improve the services to visa applicants, the Consulate General cooperates with an external service provider, VFS Global.

You can only submit your visa applications at VFS Global offices in Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir and Antalya. You can not submit visa applications directly from Consulate General.

Applicants should submit their applications in person if the applicant doesn't have a Schengen visa issued in the last 59 months.

If you have a Schengen visa issued in the last 59 months for which you gave biometrics, you may not need to apply in person. In that case, please pay more attention to have all the required documents, signatures and a biometric photo not older than 6 months. Otherwise, you may not be able to apply.

You need to book an appointment in VFS Global to hand in your application there. VFS Global can also provide you with information and other kinds of services.

The processing time of the applications are 15 days and when the decision has been made, you collect your passport at the VFS Global. The round-trip time for applications between VFS Global offices and the Consulate General is 4-6 working days, in addition to the processing time.

According to the Visa Code, visa applications must be submitted no later than 15 calendar days before the start of the intended visit. If you submit a visa application less than 15 days before the intended departure date, as a rule, it will be rejected by the Consulate. Exceptions can only be made in urgent situations.

VFS Global charges a service fee per application (over and above the visa fee) for their services. Starting from August 22, 2023 , you will pay this fee, when you book appointment from their website.

Check the VFS Global website for the application Centre’s address and opening hours.

How you proceed is described step-by-step at the VFS Global website.           

Avoid other people selling services

Please note that the Consulate General only cooperates with VFS Global. The Consulate has nothing to do with sellers outside the premises of the Consulate or the VFS Global offices who offer services for payment. No sellers can influence either the processing period or the decision.
