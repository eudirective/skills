# Portugal candidate source list (2026-10-18)

**Scope:** bounded discovery from the Danish EU free-movement residence law and official guidance seed, carrying prior country-pack findings forward as leads. Portugal is an EU Member State. The register covers official publication/version/reuse routes; EU, EEA EFTA, Swiss and distinct Andorra routes; third-country family/immigration processes; forms, visas and missions; procedure, remedies, access, privacy, oversight, legal aid, civil-status evidence and case-law discovery. Similarity is not legal equivalence.

**Inventory:** 60 official-source candidates; 0 accepted. The 19 October 2026 bounded retrieval pass saved exact responses for 46 records and converted/replay-verified 19; 27 responses were discovery shells without a stable text body and 14 failed retrieval. See the [capture ledger](monitor-candidate-manifest-2026-10-18-capture-results.json). DRE is the official publication route, but its consolidated text and legal-analysis views have no legal value. No official API documentation was found in the bounded search. AIMA permanent EU documents moved to the Portal de Renovações on 1 July 2026; older gov.pt service pages explicitly warn that they may be outdated. The pass is a review capture, not a sustainable recurring update rule.
