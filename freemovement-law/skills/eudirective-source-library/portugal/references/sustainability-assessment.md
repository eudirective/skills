## Usable official references

Nineteen sources in the 60-record monitor inventory have dated external-reference decisions in [source-acceptance.json](source-acceptance.json). These decisions accept official links only within the displayed identity, language, version/date, content and limitations. No local copy or converted Markdown file is accepted.

The Diário da República provides government-published consolidated pages for the principal EU free-movement law, its document-model regulation, the general immigration law and regulation, administrative procedure, administrative-court procedure, public-information access/reuse, and legal aid. Its pages show legal status, a selectable version date and change information. DRE also states the consolidation has no legal value and does not replace consulting the original acts. Under the repository policy, these pages are usable as official working references; do not rebuild Gazette amendment chains by default. Check a specifically relevant later change if the displayed cutoff or a source dependency makes it material.

Law 23/2007 is linked to its DRE consolidation, but the page displayed **“Em atualização”** on the review date. Preserve that warning and check the provision at issue before describing the link as current. Portaria 1334-D/2010’s consolidated page still refers to Regulation (EU) 2019/1157; check the current EU instrument for card-format questions. Its annex images were not visually checked.
