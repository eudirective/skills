DRE consolidated pages are government-published working references. They show version/change information and legal status, while DRE states the consolidation has no legal value and does not replace the original act. Use the displayed working text and caveat; check a specifically relevant later change only when material. Do not reconstruct Gazette chains by default.

## Dependencies recorded

Portaria 1334-D/2010 still cites Regulation (EU) 2019/1157. The Court of Justice declared that regulation invalid in C-61/22 while maintaining its effects until a replacement entered into force. Council Regulation (EU) 2025/1208 entered into force on 10 July 2025. Portuguese wording is preserved; the dependency register links the judgment and successor and leaves the card-specimen crosswalk open.

The dependency register separates Directive 2004/38 from general immigration and Andorra, EEA, and Swiss routes; ordinary GDPR processing from law-enforcement and SIS/VIS processing; and general administrative procedure from judicial-review remedies. These are dependencies mapped in part, not topic-adequacy findings.
