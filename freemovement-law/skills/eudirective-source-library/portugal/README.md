The pack records official national laws, operational guidance and procedural sources relevant to EU free movement, family members, civil status, adoption and remedies. EU law stays in the shared core. Direct HTTP retrieval from some official Diário da República URLs exposes a JavaScript application shell rather than legal text. Browser-reviewed DRE consolidated pages are now accepted as official government-published working references within their displayed status, date, content and caveats; DRE says these consolidations have no legal value and do not replace the original acts. Directly reviewed links do not require a local capture or conversion. The expanded monitor capture is a bounded ordinary HTTPS GET pass with exact source hashes and a separate output namespace; it is not an unattended sustainable update rule. For ordinary use, check source identity, language, displayed version/date, relevant content and caveats; extraction and reuse checks apply when retaining a local copy.
