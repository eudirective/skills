# Ministry immigration office and public contact directory

- **Source ID:** es-31
- **Publisher:** Ministry of Inclusion, Social Security and Migration, Spain
- **Official source:** [Ministry immigration office and public contact directory](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/es/web/migraciones/informacion-de-contacto)
- **Checked:** 25 October 2026
- **Version or cutoff:** Official contact page reviewed 2026-10-25; directory links are mutable.
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a link-only reference.

**Scope.** Official discovery directory for immigration offices and public contact points in Spain.

**Reviewed content.** The Ministry page links to provincial immigration offices, Police offices that process foreign-national procedures, and the Large Companies and Strategic Groups unit.

**Limits.**

- This directory does not determine territorial competence for a particular application.
- Police and immigration-office procedures are distinct; resolve the responsible office from the specific procedure.
- Contact details and office routes can change.
