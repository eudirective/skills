# Immigration appointment service FAQ

- **Source ID:** es-35
- **Publisher:** Ministry for Digital Transformation and the Civil Service
- **Official FAQ:** [Immigration appointment service questions](https://sede.administracionespublicas.gob.es/ayuda/faq/extranjeria)
- **Official help page:** [Appointment help for immigration procedures](https://sede.administracionespublicas.gob.es/pagina/index/directorio/ayuda_cita_previa)
- **Reviewed:** 25 October 2026
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; current operational content is link-only.

The official FAQ describes appointment enquiries, technical issues, cancellations, and office-dependent availability. Its indexed page says that not every Immigration Office uses the online appointment system and directs non-technical questions to the relevant office or the 060 service. A missing appointment slot does not establish that the legal filing route is unavailable.

The local capture is from 19 October 2026. The research client returned HTTP 503 when opening the live FAQ on 25 October; this does not establish that the publisher was down. The official search result was available but its indexed content was last crawled about three weeks before this review. Recheck the live FAQ and responsible office before relying on current contact or appointment details.
