# EU citizen registration certificate: National Police procedure

- **Source ID:** es-33
- **Publisher:** Spanish National Police

- **Reviewed:** 25 October 2026
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a dated route note, not a copy of the Police page.

## Route shown by the official page

The page identifies an in-person registration procedure for EU/EEA citizens planning to reside in Spain for more than three months. It says to apply within three months of entry at the provincial Immigration Office or, if unavailable, the corresponding Police station. The listed documents include EX-18, proof of the 790/012 fee payment, valid identity document, and evidence of the conditions in Article 7 of Real Decreto 240/2007.

The Police page also groups Swiss citizens in its operational description. Their legal basis is separate from EU/EEA free movement. The page links the current EX-18 model and fee service; verify office appointment and availability for the relevant province.

This note does not establish that every office has appointments, determine individual eligibility, or map the page’s stated remedy periods to a case. Consult the live procedure and applicable legal texts.
