# Electronic submission of immigration applications

- **Source ID:** es-30
- **Publisher:** Ministry of Inclusion, Social Security and Migration, Spain
- **Official source:** [Electronic submission of immigration applications](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/es/web/migraciones/presentacion-electronica-de-solicitudes-de-estancia-de-residencia-de-residencia-y-trabajo-y-de-tarjeta-de-familiar-de-ciudadano-de-la-union-europea)
- **Checked:** 25 October 2026
- **Version or cutoff:** Official Ministry page reviewed 2026-10-25; no stable revision date exposed.
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a link-only reference.

**Scope.** Official electronic submission route for listed residence and immigration applications, including EU-family cards.

**Reviewed content.** The Ministry page lists eligible application types and says access requires a digital certificate or DNIe. It also describes filing supporting documents and states that an electronic filing produces registry effects.

**Limits.**

- Supported procedures, login requirements and portal workflow can change independently of the explanatory page.
- The page does not establish that every procedure or applicant may file electronically.
- No application was submitted and no personal data was entered.
