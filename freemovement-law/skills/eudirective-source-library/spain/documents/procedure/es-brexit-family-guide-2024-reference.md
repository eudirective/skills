- **Publisher:** Ministry of Inclusion, Social Security and Migration, Spain

- **Language:** Spanish
- **Version or cutoff:** 40-page Ministry guide dated September 2024; linked from the Ministry's resident-in-Spain Withdrawal Agreement page.
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a link-only reference.

**Scope.** Dated question-and-answer guidance on Withdrawal Agreement eligibility, residence documents and family members of UK nationals resident in Spain.

**Reviewed content.** The cover and contents identify the publisher and date. Relevant sections describe existing and later-joining family members, document procedures and the Article 50 TUE residence card.

**Limits.**

- This is nonbinding Ministry guidance, not legislation; use it with the Withdrawal Agreement and the BOE-published instruction.
- Its EES and ETIAS timing statements are stale as of this review date; check current border-system rules separately.
- The September 2024 guide may not reflect later changes to law or administrative practice.
