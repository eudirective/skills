# BOE-A-2020-7276 joint instruction on Article 18.4 residence documents

- **Publisher:** Agencia Estatal Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE)

- **Version or cutoff:** Original Resolution of 2 July 2020, published in BOE No. 184 on 4 July 2020; no consolidated text is shown for this instruction.

**Scope.** The joint DGM/DGP instruction for issuing the Spanish residence document under Article 18.4 of the EU-UK Withdrawal Agreement, including covered UK nationals, family members and other persons, application routes and EX-20/EX-21 documents.

**Reviewed content.** The BOE record identifies the resolution and original instruction. The text states that Spain chose the Article 18.4 document model and sets out distinct procedures for UK nationals and qualifying third-country family members.
