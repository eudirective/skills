# Cita previa immigration appointment portal

- **Source ID:** es-32
- **Publisher:** Ministry of Territorial Policy and Democratic Memory, Spain; official route linked from the Ministry of Inclusion
- **Official source:** [Cita previa immigration appointment portal](https://sede.administracionespublicas.gob.es/pagina/index/directorio/icpplus/)
- **Checked:** 25 October 2026
- **Version or cutoff:** Official Ministry guidance reviewed 2026-10-25; the direct appointment endpoint returned HTTP 503 to the research client during this check.
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a link-only reference.

**Scope.** Official online appointment route for procedures and offices where appointment booking is enabled.

**Reviewed content.** The Ministry’s official online-procedure guide says the appointment service applies only to procedures and offices for which the option is enabled. It distinguishes immigration-office and Police services.

**Limits.**

- A research-client 503 response does not establish that the publisher service is unavailable.
- The appointment portal did not expose live availability in this check; province and procedure determine whether the route applies.
