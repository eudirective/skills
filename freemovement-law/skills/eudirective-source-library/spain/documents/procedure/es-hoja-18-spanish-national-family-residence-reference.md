# Hoja 18, family members of Spanish nationals

- **Source ID:** es-28
- **Publisher:** Ministry of Inclusion, Social Security and Migration, Spain
- **Official source:** [Hoja 18, family members of Spanish nationals](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/es/web/migraciones/w/18.-autorizacion-de-residencia-temporal-de-familiares-de-personas-con-nacionalidad-espanola)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-21
- **Language:** Spanish
- **Version or cutoff:** Page states last update April 2026.
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a link-only reference.

**Scope.** Official operational guidance for the separate temporary residence authorization for qualifying family members of Spanish nationals under LO 4/2000 and RD 1155/2024 Articles 93–98.

**Reviewed content.** The page identifies the route, qualifying categories, EX-24, filing channels and consular or domestic application routes, and says the law in force at filing controls.

**Limits.**

- Guidance is expressly orientative; the regulation controls.
- It does not resolve returning-Spanish free-movement rights or the EU/EEA/Swiss routes.
