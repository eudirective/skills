# Spanish Consulate in Brussels: EU-family short-stay visa route

- **Source ID:** es-36
- **Publisher:** Consulate General of Spain in Brussels, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, European Union and Cooperation (MAEC)
- **Official guidance:** [Visados de familiar de ciudadano de la Unión](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/bruselas/es/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/index.aspx?scca=Visados&scco=B%C3%A9lgica&scd=36&scs=Visados+de+familiar+de+ciudadano+de+la+Uni%C3%B3n)
- **Reviewed:** 25 October 2026, using the current official search result; the earlier direct retrieval was blocked by the research client.
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a mission-specific route note.

The page describes a preferential, free short-stay visa route for qualifying family members who accompany or join an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen. It identifies a stay of up to 90 days in any 180-day period, a family-evidence list, proof of residence in the Brussels consular district, and consular processing information. The page also describes a 15-day decision period that may extend to 45 days, written reasons for refusal, and review routes. Check the live page and applicable law before relying on any deadline or checklist item.
