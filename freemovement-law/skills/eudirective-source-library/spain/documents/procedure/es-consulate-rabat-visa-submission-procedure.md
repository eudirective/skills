The page says the Rabat Consulate handles applicants whose passports were issued within its consular district. For specified Schengen applications, it describes direct filing at BLS Rabat without an appointment; other applicants use BLS's appointment route. It distinguishes a short-stay visa for a family member of an EU/EEA/Swiss citizen from a residence visa for family members of Spanish nationals and gives different submission routes for those categories.

This note records the page's 23 October 2026 procedure only. Appointment slots, provider arrangements, opening hours and required evidence can change. Verify the live page and the applicant's consular district before use; do not infer the same process for another mission. No application was submitted and no applicant data was collected.
