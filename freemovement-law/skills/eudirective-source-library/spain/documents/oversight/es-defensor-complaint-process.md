# Defensor del Pueblo complaint process

- **Source ID:** es-43
- **Publisher:** Defensor del Pueblo (Spain)
- **Official guidance:** [Cómo te podemos ayudar](https://www.defensordelpueblo.es/el-defensor/como-te-podemos-ayudar/)
- **Reviewed:** 25 October 2026
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a dated reference note.

## Who may complain and how

The official page says any person, Spanish or foreign, may complain regardless of age or legal status in Spain; organisations may also complain. It describes the route as free and says no lawyer or procurador is needed. It lists online, post, fax and in-person channels and explains that the institution may contact the relevant administration, seek information and propose a solution.

## Limits

The page says the Defensor cannot annul or amend administrative acts and that filing does not suspend administrative or court appeal periods or execution. It also lists matters it will not investigate, including disputes without prior administrative action, private disputes, complaints made more than one year after learning of the facts, anonymous complaints, and matters pending before a court or disputing a judicial decision. Check competence and ordinary appeal routes separately. The page was checked on 25 October 2026; recheck live filing instructions before use.
