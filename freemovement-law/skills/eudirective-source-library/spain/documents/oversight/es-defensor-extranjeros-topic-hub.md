# Defensor del Pueblo: foreign nationals and migration topic hub

- **Source ID:** es-42
- **Publisher:** Defensor del Pueblo (Spain)
- **Official topic page:** [Extranjeros](https://www.defensordelpueblo.es/grupo-social/extranjeros/)
- **Reviewed:** 25 October 2026
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a dated reference note.

The official topic page says foreign nationals may approach the Defensor del Pueblo regardless of their documentation status in Spain or age. It describes supervision of Spanish consulates abroad, complaints about delays in nationality-by-residence files, and visits to foreigner detention centres. The page also links to individual actions, reports and news items with their own dates.

This is a topic hub and oversight route, not a statutory appeal or a complete catalogue of immigration remedies. The national Defensor del Pueblo and autonomous-community ombuds institutions have separate scopes. The page was checked on 25 October 2026; linked case records must be read individually and dated separately.
