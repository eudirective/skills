# CTBG complaints about access to public information

- **Source ID:** es-41
- **Publisher:** Consejo de Transparencia y Buen Gobierno (CTBG)
- **Official guidance:** [Qué debes saber para reclamar](https://consejodetransparencia.es/reclamaciones/como_reclamar/que-debes-saber)
- **Reviewed:** 25 October 2026
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a dated reference note.

## Route described by the CTBG

The CTBG says a person may complain when an information request has been denied, only partly granted, answered unsatisfactorily, or left unanswered. The complaint is optional and free; the page says a person may instead go directly to court. It states a one-month period from the day after notification for an answer through the state Transparency Portal. Where more than one month has passed without an answer, the page says a complaint may be filed at any time. It identifies the electronic office and, without electronic identification, in-person, public-register and postal channels. The page states a three-month decision period.

## Territorial and procedural limits

For a ministry or other state public-sector body, the CTBG identifies itself as the competent complaint body. For an autonomous-community or local authority, the CTBG says competence depends on an agreement transferring that function; the page lists Asturias, the Balearic Islands, Cantabria, Extremadura, La Rioja, Ceuta and Melilla. Elsewhere, use the competent regional transparency authority and check its governing rules and deadlines. These are the page's descriptions, not a substitute for checking the law and authority for a particular request.

The official page was checked on 25 October 2026 and its decisions index showed 2026 material through September. Recheck the live route and applicable deadline before relying on it. This complaint concerns access to public information, not the merits of an immigration decision.
