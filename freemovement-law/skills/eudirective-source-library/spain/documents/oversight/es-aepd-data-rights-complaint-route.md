# AEPD complaints about data protection rights

- **Source ID:** es-40
- **Publisher:** Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD)
- **Official guidance:** [AEPD FAQ-1303](https://www.aepd.es/preguntas-frecuentes/13-reclamaciones/FAQ-1303-puedo-interponer-una-reclamacion-respecto-al-ejercicio-de-los-derechos-de-proteccion-de-datos)
- **Reviewed:** 25 October 2026
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a dated reference note.

## Route described by the AEPD

The FAQ says a person may complain after first exercising an applicable data-protection right with the controller or processor if there is no timely answer or the answer is unsatisfactory. It identifies the AEPD electronic office and postal submission, and asks the complainant to include the prior rights request and any response. For rights requests involving telecommunications companies, the FAQ also describes an optional, free Autocontrol mediation route.

## Scope and limits

This page is guidance about complaints to the national AEPD. Check which data-protection authority is competent for the body concerned; regional authorities may handle complaints about regional public bodies. The note does not establish the merits of a complaint, preserve any statutory deadline, or replace the applicable data-protection law.
