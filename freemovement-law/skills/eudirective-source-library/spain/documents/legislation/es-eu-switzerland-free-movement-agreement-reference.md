# EU–Switzerland Agreement on free movement of persons

- **Source ID:** es-10
- **Publisher:** European Union, EUR-Lex
- **Official treaty record:** [Agreement between the European Community and Switzerland on the free movement of persons](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:22002A0430(01))

EUR-Lex identifies CELEX 22002A0430(01) as the EU–Switzerland Agreement, in force from 1 June 2002, with a consolidated version applicable from 1 January 2021. Spain is a contracting party. Annex I contains the Agreement's free-movement and family-member provisions.

This treaty is a distinct legal basis for Swiss mobility and family rights. It is not Directive 2004/38, and it does not by itself describe Spanish filing steps. Check later protocols and Joint Committee decisions when they could affect the issue. Read the current official EUR-Lex record; this note is a link reference, not treaty text or a conclusion about current Spanish practice.
