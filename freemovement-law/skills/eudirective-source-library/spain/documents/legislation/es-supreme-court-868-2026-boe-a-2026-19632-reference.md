- **Official source:** [Supreme Court judgment 868/2026 annulling specified provisions of RD 1155/2024 (BOE-A-2026-19632)](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-19632)

**Scope.** Direct judicial dependency for specified provisions of RD 1155/2024, including Spanish-national-family eligibility and criminal-record refusal grounds.

**Reviewed content.** The official BOE text identifies the decision, case number, disposition, affected provisions and correction. It nullifies part of Article 94.1(f), parts of Articles 97.4 and 98.1 for cases within Article 20 TFEU, among other provisions.
