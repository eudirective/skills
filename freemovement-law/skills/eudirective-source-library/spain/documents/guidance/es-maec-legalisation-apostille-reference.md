# MAEC document legalisation and apostille guidance

- **Source ID:** es-39
- **Publisher:** Ministry of Foreign Affairs, European Union and Cooperation (MAEC)
- **Official guidance:** [Legalización diplomática](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/es/ServiciosAlCiudadano/Paginas/Legalizacion-y-apostilla.aspx)
- **Reviewed:** 25 October 2026 through the official MAEC search result; direct retrieval was blocked by the research client.
- **Local source copy:** none accepted; this is a dated reference note.

The page distinguishes diplomatic legalisation from apostille and describes exemptions under international conventions and the EU Public Documents Regulation. It also separates foreign public documents intended for use in Spain from Spanish documents intended for use abroad, and links to country lists, procedures and sworn-translation information.

Authentication requirements depend on the document, issuing country, destination, treaty and intended procedure. Do not treat legalisation or apostille as universally required; check the applicable exemption and the competent authority. This page provides administrative guidance, not proof of the document's content or legal effect. The linked lists and service steps may change, so check the live MAEC pages before use.
