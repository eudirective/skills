# N-Lex Spain national-law discovery route

- **Source ID:** es-01
- **Publisher:** European Union Publications Office
- **Official route:** [N-Lex, Spain national legislation](https://n-lex.europa.eu/n-lex/legis_es/boe_form)
- **Reviewed:** 25 October 2026
- **Local source copy:** none; this is a discovery note, not a copy of the portal.

N-Lex presents a Spain national-legislation search route and links the Spanish destination to the Agencia Estatal Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE). Use it to locate a Spanish record, then verify the instrument and version directly at BOE.

N-Lex is a discovery gateway. It does not publish the Spanish legal text, prove the current version of an instrument, or provide complete guidance, court-decision or oversight coverage. A search result or gateway link does not establish source acceptance or topic coverage.
