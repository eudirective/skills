- **Source ID:** es-45
- **Publisher:** Consejo General del Poder Judicial (CGPJ), Centro de Documentación Judicial (CENDOJ)

**Scope.** Terms for consultation and reuse of the CENDOJ public case-law database.

**Reviewed content.** The notice permits personal consultation, prohibits commercial use and bulk downloading, and directs database or commercial reuse to CGPJ/CENDOJ procedures and conditions.

**Limits.**

- Do not use this page as an authorization for bulk collection or commercial reuse.
