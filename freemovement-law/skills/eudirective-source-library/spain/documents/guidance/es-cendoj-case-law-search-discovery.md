- **Publisher:** Consejo General del Poder Judicial (CGPJ), through the Centro de Documentación Judicial (CENDOJ)

The official CGPJ domain exposes a public jurisprudence search with court and decision-type filters, free-text search, dates, ROJ and ECLI fields. Official indexed CENDOJ result pages expose decision records and the site's reuse notice. That notice describes public access for personal consultation, bars commercial use and bulk downloads, and directs database reuse to CENDOJ's stated process.

The review client's direct request to the search landing page returned HTTP 403 on 25 October 2026. That is a research-client fetch result, not evidence that the publisher is unavailable. Official indexed CENDOJ pages were available, but no immigration-related query set was run and no filter, pagination, corpus-completeness or update behaviour was verified. Use this note only to locate official case records; it establishes no case-law topic coverage and no conclusion from an empty search. A portal-level review remains open to verify current search behaviour and coverage before designing repeatable case-law discovery.
