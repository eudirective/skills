# Spain official-source discovery register

**Research date:** 18 October 2026; retrieval check 19 October 2026; official-link review 21 October 2026. **Scope:** central first-party sources relevant to EU-family mobility/residence, EEA EFTA and Swiss routes, family members of Spanish nationals, official visas, procedure/remedies, access/privacy, oversight, legal aid, civil-status evidence and selected courts. **Seed:** Denmark's EU free-movement residence regulation and guidance. **Status:** the expanded register now has 48 candidates; 22 official external references are accepted within reviewed scope, with no local copies or topics accepted. The initial run saved 34 responses, converted and replay-verified 30 pages, found four dynamic pages without stable text, and logged 12 retrieval/conversion failures. Those failures do not bar public-link use. See the [monitor capture ledger](monitor-candidate-manifest-2026-10-18-capture-results.json), [official reference decisions](source-acceptance.json), and [issue-led coverage](coverage-register.md). The shared GET helper is optional for retained local copies; its output is isolated under `monitor-candidates/`.

Relationship means functional relationship to the Danish seed or a relevant supporting-source role. It does not mean legal equivalence. Verification and search outcome remain independent. The current link review checked responsible official publishers and preserves each displayed date and limitation. N-Lex is accepted only as a discovery gateway; BOE is the Spanish national-law publisher.
