# Legalize Italy source crosswalk

**Reviewed:** 29 October 2026  
**Repository snapshot:** [legalize-dev/legalize-it at `bd48fd8832e579d0e2cb7461f5fa4f2c67fc39b4`](https://github.com/legalize-dev/legalize-it/tree/bd48fd8832e579d0e2cb7461f5fa4f2c67fc39b4) (main commit dated 20 October 2026)

## Scope and decision

I screened the 13 Italian Normattiva act records already accepted in `source-acceptance.json`. This is a crosswalk of the library's registered legislation, not a claim that every file in the much larger Legalize repository was inventoried.

Legalize's README says the Italian files are derived from Normattiva and named by Normattiva's redaction identifier. Its [LICENSE at the pinned commit](https://github.com/legalize-dev/legalize-it/blob/bd48fd8832e579d0e2cb7461f5fa4f2c67fc39b4/LICENSE) says the legislative texts are public domain under Article 5 of Law 633/1941; the separate software/pipeline is MIT licensed. The same notice says the texts are automated reproductions, not official or verified legal texts, and directs users back to the official publisher.

**No Legalize file was added as an accepted local source and no Legalize retrieval method was assigned.** The candidates below are either older than the official current page, incomplete, not located by the bounded searches, or still need a full text comparison. The three accepted local copies already in this pack remain unchanged.

## Conversion rule

The candidate files are Markdown, but their YAML and body formatting do not meet this pack's article-level provenance format. See [Legalize conversion rules](legalize-conversion-rules.md). No conversion has been applied to an accepted source because no candidate passed the current-text gate.

## Attribution

If a later full-text comparison confirms a Legalize file, record the exact Legalize repository, pinned commit, path and blob SHA as its retrieval provenance, alongside the official Normattiva URL and date/version evidence. Normattiva remains the authoritative publisher. Do not treat Legalize's repository license or automated output notice as publisher verification.
