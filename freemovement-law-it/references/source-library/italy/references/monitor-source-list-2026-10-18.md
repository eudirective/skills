# Italy expanded official-source inventory (reviewed 24 October 2026)

Workflow: complete-national-source-pack v1.0.0; no registered Italy Monitor action, so the documented manual review was used.

**Scope:** bounded discovery of Italian legislation and official publication; EU/EEA/Swiss residence routes, UK Withdrawal Agreement rights, family entry and residence, visas and missions, administrative procedure and access, civil-status evidence, remedies, courts, privacy, oversight and legal aid. Italy is an EU Member State. EEA EFTA, Swiss, UK Withdrawal Agreement and static/mobile Italian-family routes remain distinct. This is not a complete-source claim or legal-equivalence opinion.

**Inventory:** 42 expanded records; 35 distinct official source records accepted through 37 links within narrow scopes, 4 links pending currentness or replacement review, 5 leads not relied upon, and 3 duplicate records. Three full Normattiva statutory representations (28, 80 and 92 article records) passed replay and semantic comparison and are accepted under `documents/legislation/`. The 33 wider-page conversions and their 34 exact responses remain unaccepted review evidence in the Monitor local-only archive; eight retrievals had no retained conversion. ## Findings

- D.Lgs 30/2007 is a close functional match to the Danish EU-residence seed because Italy identifies it as its implementation of Directive 2004/38. Article 23 distinguishes the family route for mobile Italian citizens from the route for family members of non-mobile Italian citizens; those routes must not be conflated.
- N-Lex's Italy connector currently reports that its database connection is not working and directs users to Normattiva. N-Lex also says its coverage excludes ministerial decrees and some dynamic amendment links are incomplete.
- Normattiva’s official current view now replaces the stale 2024 D.P.R. 394/1999 capture; the current record reported a 30 September 2026 update. The 2024 local conversion remains unaccepted.
- Normattiva’s Open Data/API material is optional technical documentation. No API, registration, automated retrieval rule, or live endpoint check is needed to use a human-reviewed official text link. Its access terms and live authentication were not validated and no API workflow is relied upon.
- Visa, residence and civil-status guidance is spread across MFA, Interior Ministry, Portale Immigrazione, prefecture and mission pages. The identified family visa PDF is dated, Rome Prefecture is one local example, and no mission-by-mission instruction review was completed.
- Italy's general administrative-document access route is separate from general civic access. The Commission for Access covers state/central bodies, while civic-defender recourse for local and regional authorities is geographically fragmented.
- Current court remedies depend on the challenged measure. Administrative-court and Cassation search tools were identified, but their corpus completeness and source-level retrieval terms were not reviewed.

The current full-act Normattiva links for D.Lgs 286/1998, D.P.R. 394/1999, Law 241/1990, D.Lgs 33/2013, the Administrative Procedure Code and D.P.R. 115/2002 are recorded in the table and source acceptance register. The TUI entry also records material links to its 2026 amendments. The [sustainability assessment](sustainability-assessment.md) and [source acceptance register](source-acceptance.json) record the link-level decisions. Three complete statutory local representations are accepted. Other saved page and PDF extracts remain unaccepted; no legal topic is adequately covered and country review remains incomplete.
