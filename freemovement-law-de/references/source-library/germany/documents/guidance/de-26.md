## What the FAQ says

The FAQ concerns a non-German EU citizen moving to Germany with a spouse who is not an EU citizen. It describes the spouse's entry and residence right as derived from the EU citizen's status under the German Freedom of Movement Act (FreizügG/EU), and says that this right applies whether the spouse is already in the EU or first enters the EU to join the citizen.

The FAQ states that no visa is required if the spouse holds a family-member residence card issued by another EU Member State under Article 10 or Article 20 of Directive 2004/38/EC. If the spouse does not have such a card and is a national of a visa-required country, an entry visa is needed, but the visa procedure is facilitated.

For further questions, the FAQ directs readers to the competent German mission in their country of residence and its website instructions.

## Scope and limits

This is a short official FAQ about accompanying or joining a non-German EU citizen who is moving to Germany. It does not set out all eligibility conditions, evidence, local residence-authority procedures, or treatment of other sponsor categories. It is distinct from the Federal Foreign Office's FAQ about a short visit to Germany. Consult the current FreizügG/EU, Directive 2004/38/EC, and the responsible mission for a specific case.
