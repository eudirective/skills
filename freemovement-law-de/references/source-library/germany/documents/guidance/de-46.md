## Verified scope

The official brochure identifies the BfJ as Germany’s Federal Central Authority under the 1993 Hague Adoption Convention. It describes the BfJ’s role as promoting international cooperation and coordinating communication between Germany and other states.

The brochure distinguishes that federal coordinating function from the handling of individual adoptions. It says that the Länder’s regional central authorities and accredited adoption bodies are authorized to conduct individual adoption work. It also identifies BfJ involvement in court proceedings concerning recognition of foreign adoption decisions and in issuing certificates of legal capacity for certain adoptions abroad.

This source concerns intercountry adoption and is adjacent family-law material. It is not an EU-family residence or visa source, and it does not provide an applicant checklist or complete adoption procedure.
