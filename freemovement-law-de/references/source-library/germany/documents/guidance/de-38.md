## Summary

The leaflet directs a person challenging a visa refusal to the legal-remedy instructions in the refusal notice. It says those instructions identify the legal action, competent authority and filing period. The Berlin court does not explain why the Federal Foreign Office refused the application and does not advise which legal action to take.

The leaflet describes the Berlin court’s filing office as a place where an action may be filed, while emphasizing it does not provide legal advice. It gives a telephone appointment route for that office. It says an attorney is not required to file there. It describes the application as being in German and lists identifying information and a copy of the challenged decision among the items to include. The leaflet distinguishes electronic filing through EGVP when a digital signature is available from filing by post or fax; it says ordinary email is not a filing route.

The leaflet points readers to the court’s contact page and fee information. It gives no general case-duration estimate, stating timing depends on the individual case.
