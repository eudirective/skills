## Scope and verified points

The Federal Foreign Office announcement says that its voluntary remonstration process for visa refusals ended worldwide on 1 July 2025. It describes remonstration as a review route the Office had offered voluntarily, rather than a remedy prescribed by statute.

The notice says that judicial review remains available and that an applicant may submit a new visa application after refusal. It also records the Office’s explanation for the policy change: a pilot suspending remonstrations in selected visa sections was evaluated as freeing staff capacity and increasing application processing.

The page is an announcement of the Office’s administrative policy. It is not a complete statement of German remedies, court jurisdiction, standing, filing method, or limitation periods. The refusal notice and applicable law must be checked for the particular case. Mission instructions and procedures may change.
