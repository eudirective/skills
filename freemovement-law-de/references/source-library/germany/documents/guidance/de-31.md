## Source identity and currentness

The live official PDF was opened on 29 October 2026. Its cover states “Stand: 15. Oktober 2026.” The handbook is a collection of administrative guidance for German visa processing. Individual chapters show their own “Stand” dates, so the cover date does not establish that every chapter was revised on 15 October 2026.

The handbook’s opening notice says that the worldwide visa-remonstration procedure was abolished from 1 July 2025 and that any remaining references in the handbook to remonstration are obsolete and have no validity. It says those passages are to be removed during ongoing revision. Treat any such residual passage as expressly disclaimed by the publisher.

## Use and scope

This source is a useful official guide to the Federal Foreign Office’s visa-administration approach and a route to chapter-level guidance. It is not legislation, a complete statement of an applicant’s rights, or proof that the handbook covers every visa category or mission practice. Each chapter’s own date and cited legal basis need to be checked before reliance. The applicable law and the instructions in an individual decision remain controlling.

The handbook addresses visa matters across distinct Schengen short-stay and German national visa contexts. A handbook entry must be read within the visa category and legal framework it addresses; it should not be generalized to EU free-movement, EEA, Swiss, or German-citizen family routes without separate authority.
