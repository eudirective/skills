## Scope and verified content

The live page concerns spouses joining an EU or EFTA citizen in Germany. It distinguishes a joining spouse who is an EU/EFTA citizen from a third-country national. For third-country spouses it describes visa requirements, an exception for certain holders of another European residence permit, visa-exempt nationalities and application routes. It directs users to the Consular Services Portal or a responsible German mission, and notes that additional documents depend on the mission.

The page states that a spouse joining an EU/EFTA citizen may live and work in Germany, and describes the residence-card/permit need as depending on nationality. Its summary wording groups EU and EFTA sponsors; the precise legal route differs among EU free movement, EEA law, Swiss agreements and other national rules. Read the guidance only within the sponsor and applicant categories it describes, and check the cited legislation and competent authority separately.
