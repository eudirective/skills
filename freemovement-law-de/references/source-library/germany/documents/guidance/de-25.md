# Federal Foreign Office FAQ — visa for a non-EU spouse visiting with a non-German EU citizen

## What the FAQ says

The FAQ addresses a non-German EU citizen who wants to visit Germany with a spouse who is not an EU citizen. It distinguishes three residence situations:

1. **Residence in a Schengen country:** The FAQ says that a spouse holding a residence permit issued by a Schengen country does not need a visa for a short stay in another Schengen country, including Germany, subject to the 90-days-in-any-180-day-period limit. It advises checking with the authorities in the country of residence whether the particular permit allows visa-free travel in the Schengen area.
2. **Residence in an EU country where Schengen does not apply fully:** The FAQ says the spouse does not need a visa for travel within the EU when the residence country has issued a “residence card of a family member of a Union citizen” under Article 10(1) of Directive 2004/38/EC.
3. **Residence outside the EU:** If the spouse is a national of a country subject to a visa requirement, the FAQ says an entry visa is required, with a facilitated visa procedure applying.

For further questions, the FAQ directs the reader to the relevant German mission or to contact that mission.

## Scope and limits

This is short-stay FAQ guidance. It does not cover moving to Germany, long-term residence, the procedure for obtaining the Article 10 card, or all national and mission-specific rules. Confirm the applicable document and route with the competent authority or German mission, and use Directive 2004/38/EC and relevant national law for legal analysis.
