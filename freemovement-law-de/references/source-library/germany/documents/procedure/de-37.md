# Berlin Administrative Court: visa-procedure information

**Source ID:** de-37  
**Source type:** Regional court information page / leaflet locator  
**Publisher:** Administrative Court Berlin (Verwaltungsgericht Berlin)  
**Official page:** <https://www.berlin.de/gerichte/verwaltungsgericht/service/visumsverfahren/>  
**Checked:** 29 October 2026  
**Language reviewed:** German page; links to German and English leaflets

## Verified role

The court’s page addresses people who have received a visa refusal and want to challenge it. It directs them to read the leaflet linked from the page. The page itself is a document locator, not an explanation of the remedy or deadline. It identifies the Administrative Court Berlin and links to the court’s official English and German leaflets.

The linked English leaflet says the refusal notice contains the applicable legal action, competent authority and filing period. That leaflet is summarized separately in [de-38](../guidance/de-38.md). The individual refusal notice and current law must be checked for the actual case.

Recheck that the official page still identifies the Berlin Administrative Court, links to current leaflets, and has not moved. Confirm the linked leaflet itself before relying on it. This is Berlin court information and must not be presented as nationwide or case-specific advice.
