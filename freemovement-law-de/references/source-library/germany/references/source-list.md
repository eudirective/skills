## Findings

- The electronic Bundesgesetzblatt is the authentic federal promulgation source; the Gesetze im Internet consolidations are the working text sources recorded here. Currentness after each accepted copy’s publisher build date is not certified.
- The accepted corpus contains 17 unique dated German statutory text copies for 18 current-law source records; de-10 is represented by de-09. Acceptance of a source does not establish topic coverage.
- The Foreign Office handbook is accepted as an official guidance locator dated 15 October 2026. Its extracted local text has not been accepted; the handbook itself warns that residual remonstration references are obsolete.
- Five source proposals remain in human review: de-03, de-34, de-36, de-41 and de-42. Hesse’s service record is dated 2020; the BAMF guide is old; the BGBl and BfDI pages need direct review.
- Residence routes and procedures vary by mission and Land. Swiss, EEA, EU and German-citizen family routes remain distinct. Adoption and civil-status references are adjacent categories, not EU-family residence rules.

No Germany topic is marked covered and country review remains incomplete. The Monitor has no registered Germany pack action, so runtime pack checks and acceptance gates are unsupported here; the documented source dispositions are manual review results.
