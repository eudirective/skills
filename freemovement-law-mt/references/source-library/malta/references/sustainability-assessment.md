## Normal use and maintenance

For normal source use, a human-reviewed official link is enough. Prefer a trustworthy government-published consolidation or the closest official text, record its displayed edition/date and caveats, and use the whole responsible official website for unresolved currentness or operational detail. Do not rebuild Gazette chains by default.

The earlier full-text captures were not accepted as local copies. Article 13 of the [Statute Law Revision Act, 2021 (Act XL of 2021)](https://legislation.mt/eli/slra/eng) restricts reproducing revised editions without Government permission; its publication exception excludes reproductions based on scanned or electronically reproduced revised editions. The previous statutory conversions were based on official publisher PDFs. Item-specific republication permission was not established for the captured guidance pages and forms. The source list therefore retains official links and their scopes, not copied text. Chapter 546 does not by itself settle the terms for each portal item.

Local captures, conversion scripts, hashes, replay and scheduled updates do not establish legal currency or coverage. Check a specific amendment, attachment or source condition when it may materially change the issue. A live link or a source age alone does not resolve topic coverage or country completion.
