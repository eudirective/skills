## Scope and limitations

Official issue identity was checked against its cover and colophon. Article 1 replaces the last sentence of Article 4(1) of YA 22343/2024. That paragraph addresses applicants appearing in person for application submission and the collection of photograph and fingerprints; the replacement exemption names newly posted diplomatic/consular mission personnel, notified staff of international and European organizations where their legal status is governed by relevant international agreements (including any headquarters agreement), and their spouses, registered partners and children up to age 23. Article 2 adds Annex I, the national-visa application form, and Annex II, the standardized medical-certificate form. Article 3 leaves the remainder of YA 22343/2024 in force; the amendment takes effect upon publication. The p.1–2 text was extracted using pypdf 6.21.2 and checked against the Gazette identity/date; embedded image-form pages 3–5 were decoded from the official PDF and visually reviewed. Annex I is a two-page image-based national-visa form containing fields 1–45, ending with family-relationship information; Annex II is an image-based medical-certificate form for a Greece long-stay D visa. The 2026 MFA English page-linked application form gr-156 has 52 fields and a 2011 PDF timestamp, so it is a distinct model whose relation to Annex I is unresolved. The separately linked medical form gr-157 visually matches Annex II in content, though exact file identity is not established. The 2026-10-25 MFA National Visas index lists YA 65381/2025 and separate national-visa application and medical-certificate links. Limits: This is a dated amending decision, not a consolidated version of YA 22343/2024 or a complete statement of current national-visa procedure.; No exhaustive search for later amendments was completed. The current wording of the general procedure and which application form missions require remain open; the MFA-linked gr-156 file differs from the 2025 Gazette Annex I.; The relationship between the Greek exemption wording and Visa Code Article 13 was not legally resolved; do not infer more than the amendment states.; The separate MFA English DOCX at gr-98 is a dated August 2025 summary whose biometric-exemption wording predates this amendment; it is not a current checklist.; Mission-specific checklists, appointment/provider arrangements, live submission behavior, and current timelines remain open.; Greek is the controlling language.
