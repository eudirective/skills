Description

The procedure concerns the issue of a certificate of registration to citizens of the European Union, countries of the European Economic Area and the Swiss Confederation, who are to reside in Greece for a period exceeding three months from the date of their arrival, if they are employed or engaged in independent economic activity.

2EstablishmentThe issue of a Certificate of Registration for Union citizens requires that Union citizens reside in Greece.

3Time limitUnion citizens who are going to reside in Greece for a period exceeding three months from the date of their arrival are obliged, after the expiry of the three-month period, to appear in person at the competent police authorities of their place of residence for registration.

NoNo

4LabourThe issue of a Certificate of Registration for Citizens of the Union presupposes that citizens of the Union, who reside in the Greek territory as employees or non-employees. According to Article 7 par. 3 of the Presidential Decree 106/2007, the status of an employee or a person exercising an independent economic activity is maintained for a citizen of the Union who is no longer an employee or no longer exercising an independent economic activity in the following cases:

(a) if the person concerned is temporarily unable to work due to illness or accident;

(b) if the person concerned is duly registered as involuntarily unemployed, having exercised a professional activity for more than one year, and is registered as a person seeking employment with the competent employment service,

(c) if the person concerned has been duly registered as involuntarily unemployed after the expiry of a fixed-term employment contract of less than one year or after having been involuntarily unemployed during the first twelve months and has been registered with the competent employment service as a person seeking work. In this case, the status of worker is maintained for six months; and

(d) if the person concerned is undergoing vocational training, the maintenance of the status of worker presupposes the existence of a link between the previous occupational activity and the training. This condition is not required in cases where the person concerned is involuntarily unemployed.

NoNo

5Conditions for non-commission of criminal offensesThe issue of a Certificate of Registration for Union citizens presupposes that Union citizens residing in the Greek territory as employees or non-employees are not subject to restrictions on their freedom of movement and residence for reasons of public order or public security.
