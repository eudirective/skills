# Reviewed Greek source documents

This directory contains a source-specific record for each accepted reference in the Monitor candidate manifest:

- Eighty-four source-specific Markdown files retain source text: 67 scoped official legislative or administrative excerpts under Greek Law 2121/1993 Article 2(5), sixteen complete or dated MITOS procedure-page copies under CC BY-SA 4.0, and one dated Gov.gr guidance-page snapshot under its lawful non-commercial reuse terms. The page copies are not legislation or current-procedure certification.
- Link-only locator/disposition notes remain for references without accepted local text; they do not reproduce source content.
- Candidate gr-06 is a duplicate locator for gr-05 and has no separate document.
- Five later accepted source-list references are outside the 207 accepted entries in the candidate manifest and remain external-only.

Unaccepted captures remain in the EU Source Update Monitor's indexed local-only archive. Link acceptance, retained text, legal currentness, topic coverage and country completion are separate states.
