## Scope and limits

Article 23 lists the third-country family-reunification approval file and national-visa-stage documents under Law 5038/2023 Articles 84–85. Article 59 lists residence-document evidence for PD 106/2007 family routes, including direct ascendants and selected extended-family categories. This is the original 2024 instrument, not a verified current checklist or consolidation; a bounded official-domain search found no later corrective Gazette text for the reviewed Article 59 paragraphs. The identified Gazette title, number, date, and selected text were checked.
