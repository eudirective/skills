## Official page text

### Residence of third country citizens who are family members of EU citizens

Information on the residence of third country citizens who are family members of EU citizens, in Greece.

Third country citizens who are family members of EU citizens must apply for a residence document within 3 months of entering Greece. The application must be filed with the migration service for the place where they are living (list of departments).

The interested party must produce an ID card or valid travel document. The application must be accompanied by proof of payment of the fee for printing a stand-alone document (EUR 16).

If the supporting documents are complete, the interested party will receive immediate acknowledgement of receipt of the supporting documents required for a residence card (blue confirmation letter).

Provided there are no objections on grounds of public policy, security or public health, a decision granting a residence card to the EU citizen's family members will be adopted within 6 months of the date of receipt of the application and all the necessary supporting documents.

The decision will include the name of the court or judicial authority to which the interested party can appeal and the deadline for an appeal (Article 24 of Presidential Decree 106/2007). If the application is refused, a deadline is set (usually no less than 1 month from the date of notification of the decision) by which the interested party must leave Greece.
