## Scope

Government training portal: issuing civil registry records.

## Maintained summary

The guide describes immediate electronic issuance through gov.gr for records issued from 2013 onward; older records amended or excerpted since 2018; and foreign events registered at the Athens Special Registry Office from 22 January 2018. If immediate issuance is unavailable, it describes a later KEP route and a myKEPlive video appointment where KEP cannot issue electronically.

## Limits

This is a government training/workflow guide, not a legal determination of eligibility, authentication, translation or cross-border acceptance. Dynamic portal availability and individual record data were not tested.
