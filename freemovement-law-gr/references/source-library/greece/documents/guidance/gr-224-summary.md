## Scope

Attica Decentralized Administration: Apostille service scope.

## Maintained summary

The page distinguishes e-Apostille processing for eligible digitally issued records through gov.gr from the Attica office’s handling of physical originals bearing signatures of Attica municipalities or the Athens Special Registry Office. It describes the local office’s submission route and scope.

## Limits

This is regional operational information, not a national statement that every document can be apostilled or that a destination state will accept it. The issuing authority, document form, territorial link and destination-country requirements matter. No service transaction was submitted.
