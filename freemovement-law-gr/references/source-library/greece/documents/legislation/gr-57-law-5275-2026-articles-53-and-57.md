## Scope and limits

Article 53 reproduces PD 106/2007 Article 3 as amended for five-year residence cards in specified other-family-member and stable-partner cases, including listed pending applications. Article 57 makes targeted changes to Law 5226/2025 Articles 4, 22(4) and 23(4), not Articles 13–15 or 28. This is not a consolidated PD 106/2007 or Law 5226/2025; effect of the revised Article 3(1) wording and present practice remain open. The identified Gazette title, number, date, and selected text were checked.
