## Official source text

Published: 25.10.2026.

Updated: 26.10.2026.

Due to technical reasons, the websites of several embassies and permanent representations are currently unavailable. Please follow our social media channels for the latest information and updates.

Assistance in emergency situations abroad

Consular services

Latvian Embassies provide consular services only to persons whose place of residence abroad has been registered in the Latvian Register of Natural Persons.

Before applying for the service at the Embassy, check that you have declared your residence abroad.

The declaration of the place of residence by Embassy is a consular service with a consular fee of EUR 90.00.

The Consular Section of the Latvian Embassies provide consular services on-site only by prior appointment.

Please arrive at the Embassy’s Consular Section at the exact time of your appointment and ensure that you bring all required documents.

If you are unable to attend your appointment at the Embassy, please cancel it in advance to allow others to make use of the available appointment slot.

Public holiday opening hours

The Embassies of Latvia and their Consular Sections are closed on Latvian public holidays.

Consular services are not provided on public holidays observed in the host country.
