# Latvia official-source review

This directory contains Latvia’s official-source register, scoped external-link decisions, 40 accepted local text snapshots, maintained dated PMLP family-document, family-unification, filing-channel, residence-document, identity-number/address, MFA legal-conflict, MFA entry-hub, and State Border Guard route-map notes, a complete field map for the current PMLP EU-family application PDF, and a retrieval/conversion prototype. The external links and local copies are accepted only within their recorded source scopes; no topic is accepted as adequately covered and the country review is incomplete.

Latvia is an EU Member State. The 52-record inventory distinguishes EU/EEA/Swiss free movement, UK Withdrawal Agreement beneficiaries, and third-country immigration. Forty-eight official links are accepted within narrow recorded scopes; four are pending and no records are currently classified as not relied upon. Forty of the accepted references also have a dated local text snapshot. Three additional primary laws cover address registration, population-register records, and civil-status documentation. The current free-movement Act took effect on 10 July 2026. Its transitional text, the displayed validity of Cabinet Regulation 675 through 2 December 2027, and the new rule-making deadline leave Regulation 675’s application to post-commencement filings unresolved. The MFA page still describes Regulation 675 as the governing route; the current PMLP overview points to the new Act. See the acceptance, coverage, and dependency registers before relying on operational instructions.

The 52-record register has 43 direct official-source captures: 41 HTML pages and 2 PDFs. Forty selected text copies are in `documents/` and accepted within their recorded scope after deterministic replay. Thirteen unaccepted candidate texts and the old PMLP PDF are preserved in the Monitor’s local-only archive; they are not kept under this country pack. The OP 2026/119.1 Gazette PDF is stored with its reviewed text extraction. Six records are discovery or case-law routes without an individual text. Local-copy acceptance remains separate from external-link acceptance, topic coverage, and country completion.

The source review covers legislation, agency guidance, forms, visa and mission pages, administrative procedure, privacy and oversight, legal aid, and complaint routes. It did not identify individual court decisions: the court entries identify repositories/search surfaces, not specific decisions. A signature check is not a routine gate for a government-published consolidation; verify it only if a conclusion depends on that Gazette file's seal. Confirm that the PMLP form is linked from its current parent page before using it.

Run `python3 sync_latvia.py verify` to check saved source hashes and replay accepted local text copies without modifying them. `reconvert` and `convert-pdfs` stage unaccepted conversions under the temp workspace; they do not replace or accept documents. PDF tooling is pinned in [`requirements.txt`](requirements.txt). These commands do not retrieve sources or establish source-monitor coverage.

The ordinary HTTPS route worked without API registration or publisher security challenges. The publisher's reuse terms and fees concern retained or recurring copies; they do not block ordinary links to the official consolidated texts. No scheduled mirror is proposed here. See the [retrieval capture manifest](references/latvia-captures.json) and [sustainability assessment](references/sustainability-assessment.md) for the separate optional capture and reuse assessment.
