## Separate route findings

1. **Keep three legal tracks separate.** The FNIA applies to foreign nationals subject to treaty exceptions; the AFMP and EFTA Convention govern distinct EU/EFTA routes. SEM's FZA guidance also identifies a fact-specific situation where a Swiss citizen may rely on the AFMP for a third-country family member after prior permanent residence in an EU/EFTA state. The title “family reunification” is not enough to merge these regimes.
2. **Treat Bilaterals III as a pending change.** The AFMP protocol was signed in March 2026 and the package is before Parliament. It is not an operative replacement for the current AFMP on the evidence reviewed. Capture signature, approval, referendum, domestic implementation and effective dates separately.
3. **Use dated government consolidations within their stated scope.** The 21 October link review found official German and French Fedlex references for AIG, VEV, VFP and VwVG. They are accepted only within the displayed date and inspected scope; the dated AIG and VwVG editions do not establish later currentness. English translations are informational and can lag. Do not reconstruct Gazette fragments by default; check the whole Fedlex site or offer it for human review when the current version or legal status is unclear.
4. **Cantonal and mission sources are operationally essential.** SEM says cantons decide permit and family-reunification applications. The Istanbul post has distinct C-visa and national-visa routes, VFS for short stay, and replaceable family-document checklists. A federal page or one post cannot establish complete local coverage.
5. **Federal remedies do not cover every authority.** FDPIC explicitly excludes cantonal and municipal bodies; cantonal data-protection regulators and administrative remedies need separate research. FOJ reports no federal ombudsman office, but its 2004 local-office list needs current verification.
6. **Court coverage is selective.** FAC publishes substantive judgments and only selected procedural rulings; the Federal Supreme Court database has its own historical and anonymisation boundaries. Do not label either corpus complete for migration matters.

The 26 former candidate conversions are retained only in the Monitor local-only archive; no local source copy is accepted. Thirty official external references from the source-lead inventory are accepted within narrow scopes, plus one supplemental EFTA status reference; their status is recorded separately in [source-acceptance.json](source-acceptance.json). The Monitor archive preserves the original candidate files and capture metadata; each former candidate artifact has an accepted-link, duplicate, out-of-scope, or human-review disposition below. This register does not establish topic adequacy or country completion and does not implement an updater.
