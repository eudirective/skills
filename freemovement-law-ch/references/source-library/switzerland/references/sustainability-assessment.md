# Switzerland source sustainability assessment

**Assessment date:** 25 October 2026
**Overall status:** Official link-level references are usable within narrow scopes. Legal-topic coverage and country review remain incomplete. No retained local copy or topic is accepted.

## Current source state

The 44-record source-lead inventory covers laws, treaty routes, government guidance, forms, mission practice, procedures, oversight and court discovery. The former capture baseline had 28 configured routes, 27 saved responses and 26 review conversions. All 26 candidate files and both tracking manifests are now preserved in the Monitor local-only archive; the 28 archive hashes matched the published mirror before removal. The register records 26 official external references, 25 of which have link-only source notes under `documents/`; no local source text is accepted. The register accepts a direct official link only for the version, date, language, legal status and passages actually checked. It does not mark a local copy, subject area, or country review complete.

The checked legal references include:

- AIG, German, dated 15 October 2023; Article 2 preserves applicable treaty-specific rules. This is a dated reference, not a claim that the edition includes every later amendment.
- VEV, German, displayed by Fedlex as in force with a 12 June 2026 status date.
- OLCP/VFP, French, dated 1 January 2026.
- VwVG, German, dated 1 July 2022; its scope is federal administrative authorities and does not establish cantonal migration procedure.
- FDFA's AFMP status and link page, and EFTA's institutional explanation that Switzerland is not a party to the EEA Agreement.
- SEM Directive No. 322.3-12, dated 4 December 2025, for specified third-country family-reunification procedures. It clarifies the foreign-representation, canton, and SEM handoffs and expressly excludes AFMP family cases.

The official Fedlex web interface did not render some full text bodies in the research client; their official records are still usable references for the displayed identity and version metadata. Open them directly for provision-level use and recheck the publisher for later versions. Full legal-text retrieval, OCR, replay, checksum comparisons and bulk/API registration are optional activities for a future retained-copy or automated-monitoring workflow, not source-acceptance conditions.

## Link sustainability by source group

| Source group | Sustainable link use | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Fedlex laws and treaty records | Stable ELI/record links and dated file-store URLs identify specific official texts; the checked links can be reopened directly by a human. | Version selection and later changes must be rechecked at the official portal when a legal question arises. The AIG and VwVG references are older dated editions; the treaty body was not rendered in this review. |
| SEM route guidance | Dated EU/EFTA guidance and a current visa-refusal FAQ state route boundaries and direct users to responsible authorities. | SEM pages do not establish each canton’s process or each mission’s instructions. Keep route, revision date and accepted passages visible. |
| Cantonal authority directory | SEM's official directory provides a practical route to local authorities. | A directory is not the forms, appointments, evidence, filing and remedy process for all 26 cantons. Review the relevant official canton site. |
| FDFA missions | The official directory provides a discovery route to Swiss representations. | Mission competence, appointment providers, checklists, fees and channels vary and can change. Istanbul remains one example only. |
| Swiss Parliament | Dossier 26.023 has a visible update timestamp and deliberation status, suitable for repeatable status checks. | A parliamentary docket is not the treaty or implementing law and does not prove a change is in force. Check official legislation and treaty records before using a proposed rule. |
| FAC and other courts | The FAC page links the published-decisions database and states its publication rules. | FAC publication is selective for procedural decisions, reference decisions do not cover all fields, and internet completeness is not guaranteed. Federal Supreme Court and cantonal court coverage remain open. |

## Source-rule sustainability

The former acquisition-oriented assessment described an exact Fedlex metadata/API path, reuse checks, archive reconciliation, local conversion and replay as steps toward source sustainability. These may be useful if the project chooses to retain local legal texts or automate updates. They should not block ordinary use of a trustworthy official consolidated page or document. For link-based research, record the publisher, exact URL, language, displayed version/date, legal status, relevant scope, checked passages, and specific limits; re-open the official source when those facts may have changed.

Where government publisher identity, current version, or legal scope is unclear, keep that question visible and offer the relevant whole official website for human review. Do not make one failed browser rendering or one stale translation a reason to reconstruct a Gazette chain automatically. Use the closest government-published text with a dated caveat when that is the best usable official source.

No registration-only API, automated updater, raw-source retention, conversion replay, or hash calculation is required for the 26 external link decisions. If an updater is later requested, design the simplest sustainable check for the chosen source: revisit the official parent page or versioned record, compare the displayed version/date and links, and route material ambiguity for human review. Do not build an updater for sources that only need an occasional direct check.

## Material open coverage

The link review does not establish that the 44 source leads form a legally sufficient source set. Important open work includes the full AFMP and EFTA Convention text and current version, current federal law and implementing provisions, issue-based mapping of remedies, canton-level procedures across all 26 cantons, mission-specific instructions, sponsor-specific family routes, and broader Federal Supreme Court and cantonal case-law discovery. Family-status, access-to-information, privacy, legal-aid and oversight source leads also need issue-led review. See [`coverage-register.md`](coverage-register.md).

The parliament docket and FAC publication page provide useful discovery and monitoring routes while expressly limiting what can be inferred from them. A search of published FAC decisions is not proof that all relevant decisions were found. Likewise, an official SEM directory does not establish the details of linked local processes.

## Conclusions

- **Official link use:** sustainable for the 12 accepted references within their recorded scope and dates, with a direct publisher recheck when currentness matters.
- **Local captures:** 0 accepted. The former 26 conversions remain local-only review artifacts and are not accepted source text.
- **Topic coverage:** 0 accepted as complete. A topic should be accepted only after its applicable legal route, procedure, competent authority and remedies are mapped or the remaining gap is explicit.
- **Country review:** incomplete. The source set is not a completeness certification.
- **Human review:** offer the whole official Fedlex, SEM, FDFA, canton, mission and court portals for unresolved questions of currentness, route, version or coverage.
