# Switzerland national-source review

The pack records 48 bounded source leads across Swiss federal law, guidance, procedures, forms, decisions, oversight and discovery routes. On 25 October 2026, all 26 published candidate conversions, the candidate manifest, and the capture-results manifest were preserved in the EU Source Update Monitor's local-only archive and removed from the public pack; their 28/28 archive hashes matched the synchronized source-library mirror before removal. The review now records 31 official external references accepted within narrow scopes: 30 from the source-lead inventory and one supplemental EFTA status reference. Twenty-five have link-only source records under `documents/`; Parliament dossier 26.023 remains a discovery-only reference in the acceptance register because the Monitor does not allow discovery material under `documents/`. **No retained official source text, topic, or country review is accepted as complete.**

Switzerland is an EFTA and Schengen participant but not an EEA Contracting Party. Keep AFMP/EFTA treaty routes, the FNIA route for family members of Swiss citizens, ordinary third-country routes, and UK acquired-rights cases distinct. The Parliament dossier records the Bilateral III package as a pending legislative process; a proposal is not operative law.

Government-published consolidated texts may be used as working references when the publisher, language, date/version, legal status and scope are clear. Link acceptance is separate from local-copy acceptance, topic coverage, and country completion. When authority, current version, or scope is materially unclear, use the open human-review offer recorded in the acceptance register.
