The live Federal Form Service route is linked from the Volksanwaltschaft complaint guide and offers anonymous use or sign-in with ID Austria. The interactive form was not submitted.

The initial HTML contained transient ASP.NET ViewState, event-validation and session values. Those values were deliberately excluded from the repository; this route record is a bounded operational locator, not a replayable copy of the interactive form.
