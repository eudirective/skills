# Datenschutzbehörde online complaint and ex officio review submission route

- Jurisdiction: Austria
- Source ID: `at-dsb-online-complaint-form`
- Official route: https://www.formularservice.gv.at/site/fsrv/user/formular.aspx?pid=5a37966ab26c4489adc20dc53162fa4d&pn=Bf10e1134f601464e9b3835170de668bd
- Linked official parent: https://dsb.gv.at/eingabe-an-die-dsb/beschwerde
- Checked: 2026-10-19
- Scope: route identity and initial submission capabilities only; no form data entered or submitted.

The live Federal Form Service route is titled 'Eingabe an die Datenschutzbehörde'. The first screen offers a complaint or a prompt for ex officio review. The DSB's linked complaint page points to this route and provides separate procedural/fee information. The interactive page was not submitted.

The initial HTML contained transient ASP.NET ViewState, event-validation and session values. Those values were deliberately excluded from the repository; this route record is a bounded operational locator, not a replayable copy of the interactive form.
