# What documents do you need for the application?

## You are an EEA citizen or Swiss citizen and apply for a registration certificate?
Then you need:
• a valid identity card OR
• a validpassport
AND
• documents to show why you want to continue living inAustria.
Depending on what you marked under Point B you will need the following documents.
You marked (35) Employee? Then you will also need: 5
• a work contract or
• a confirmaton of engagement from theemployer.
You marked (35) Self-employed? Then you will also need:
• proof that you are self-employed.
For example: a work contract, fee notes, your income tax assessment for the last year.

## You marked (37) Private individual / non-employed person? Then you will also need:
• proof that you have comprehensive sickness insurance cover in Austria
• proof that you have sufficient resources for yourself and your family members for your planned stay in Austria.
You can show sufficient resources through your income or savings. For example your pension.
You marked (38) pupil/student (education)? Then you will also need:
• proof of enrolment at an establishment accredited or financed by Austria
• proof that you have comprehensive sickness insurance cover in Austria
• proof that you have sufficient resources
You marked (39) family member?
As husband, wife or registered partner you also need:
• a wedding certificate or certificate on your registered partnership.
As a child or grandchild under the age of 21 you also need: Status: 08/2026 documentation
• a document to proof a family relationship.
If you want to prove that a person is related to you, you need to bring a confirmation. This is, for example, a birth certificate or adoption certificate.
As a child or grandchild above the age of 21 you also need:
• a document attesting to the existence of a family relationship. If you want to prove that a person is related to you, you need to bring a confirmation. This is, for example, a birth certificate or adoption certificate. AND
• proof that you need maintenance AND
• proof that you are receiving maintenance from the EEA citizen or Swiss citizen, her husband, his wife or his/her registered partner. That means you have to show that you are getting money or other assistance from a EEA citizen or Swiss citizen covering your basic needs. 6
As a mother, father, grandmother or grandfather you also need:
• a document to proof a family relationship.
If you want to prove that a person is related to you, you need to bring a confirmation. This is, for example, a birth certificate or adoption certificate. AND
• proof that you need maintenance AND
• proof that you are receiving maintenance from the EEA citizen or Swiss citizen, her husband, his wife or his/her registered partner. That means you have to show that you are getting money or other assistance from a EEA citizen or Swiss citizen covering your basic needs.
As a life partner in a durable relationship you will also need:
• proof of a durable relationship with a EEA citizen or Swiss citizen.
As another family member you also need:
• proof that you are receiving maintenance from the EEA citizen or Swiss citizen in your country of origin. That means you have to show that you are getting money or other assistance from a EEA citizen or Swiss citizen covering your basic needs OR
• proof that you have lived together with the EEA citizen or Swiss citizen in the same household in your country of origin OR
• proof of the existence of serious health grounds which strictly require the personal care of the family member by the EEA citizen or Swiss citizen Status: 08/2026 documentation

# You are an EEA citizen or Swiss citizen and apply for a certificate of permanent residence?
Then you need
• a valid identity card
OR
a valid passport
• a proof that you have lived in Austria for the last 5 years and that you are still living in Austria
• a proof that your reason of residence in the last 5 years was as follows (several points are possible):
o employee/employee
o self-employed
o private individual / non-employed person
o pupil or student (education)
o family member 8
Evidence includes employment contracts, income tax returns, school reports, enrollment confirmations, bank statements, marriage certificate, partnership certificate or birth certificate.
You will find additional information on pages 5 to 7.

# Are you a third-country national applying for a permanent residence card?
Then you need
• a valid identity card
OR
a valid passport
• a proof that you have lived in Austria for the last 5 years and that you are still living in Austria
• Proof that you have been a family member of an EEA citizen or Swiss citizen for the last 5 years.
You can find more information about the family members on pages 6 and 7. Status: 08/2026 documentation

# ATTENTION:
Even if you have already submitted all the listed documents, the authority may still require more information/documents from you. This may be necessary if not everything is clear for the authority yet. Therefore, please send these documents to the authority quickly. 9
If you do not pay the fees, you cannot get a residence permit. Please pay the fees as soon as possible. If you pay later, your procedure will take longer. Status: 08/2026 documentation
