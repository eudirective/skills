# Austria Legalize source crosswalk — 29 October 2026

## Scope and verified addition

Reviewed the Legalize Austria repository at pinned main commit `30b7ded29545baa1079dc2ab8b2eef9193df3f59`. The repository describes its legislative content as automated reproductions of RIS federal consolidations and distinguishes the source-data license (CC BY 4.0) from its MIT-licensed pipeline. RIS remains authoritative.

One overlapping complete statute was verified and copied into the source library: the Informationsfreiheitsgesetz (IFG), RIS Gesetzesnummer 20012537. Every section body §§ 1–20 matched existing accepted RIS captures; the 23 repeated “Abkürzung IFG” display labels were the only removed source material. The RIS current-consolidation page was checked 29 October 2026 and listed BGBl. I Nr. 52/2025 as the latest amendment, matching the Legalize snapshot. See [the consolidated text](../documents/legislation/at-ifg-consolidated.md), [conversion rule](legalize-conversion-rules.md), and [acceptance record](source-acceptance.json).
