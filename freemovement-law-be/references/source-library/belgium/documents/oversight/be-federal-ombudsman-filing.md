Filing a complaint

Body

Your complaint must involve a federal public service. First you will need to have reported your problem to the federal public service concerned and to have asked them to sort it out. Read some of the types of complaints we can deal with.

As we are bound by professional secrecy. Your personal data are treated with complete confidentiality and processed in the context of your complaint only.

Not too sure whether we can help you? Contact us.

Complaint form

The quickest way to file a complaint is by using our online complaint form. After you have sent the form, you will receive an automatic confirmation.

By e-mail:

Email us at contact@federalombudsman.be. In the e-mail, you describe your complaint, you give your contact details and join all useful documents.

By post

Use our printable complaint form and
Post it to Rue de Louvain 48 letter box 6, 1000 Brussels Belgium

Printable complaint form.pdf(190.19 KB)"pdf"

By appointment

Make an appointment with a member of our team if you wish to lodge your complaint in person. You can meet us in our office in Brussels or during one of our clinics near you.
