## Article 40ter §1 route described by the source

The page describes a Belgian who actually resided in another EU Member State under Article 21 TFEU and then returns to settle in Belgium. It says the family members covered by Articles 40bis §2 and 47/2 follow the EU-family-member provisions when all three conditions apply:

1. the family life was created or continued during the Belgian and family member's actual residence in another EU Member State;
2. the family member accompanies or joins the Belgian in Belgium; and
3. the family life has not ended before the family member's entry into Belgium or before the residence application is filed.

The page says that family members who do not meet those conditions fall under the rules for family members of a Belgian who did not exercise free movement. This is a summary of the page and the cited provision, not an assessment of a particular applicant's eligibility.
