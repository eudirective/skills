# Belgium national-source library (partial)

Review date: 24 October 2026. This is a bounded source register, not a claim that Belgian law or procedures are complete. The review now accepts 47 official external links within their recorded scope; one superseded IBZ route is not relied upon. Thirty local copies are accepted under `documents/`: nine Justel legal texts; fourteen official guidance, procedure and oversight snapshots; three IBZ form/specimen PDFs; one FPS Foreign Affairs visa-routing matrix; two regional legislation texts; and one monthly selected-case publication. The related Federal Justice adoption guidance is a dated local snapshot; no update date is displayed. No legal topic is marked covered, and Belgium review is incomplete.

Official government links can be used directly when publisher and source role are clear. If the current consolidated record or exact source scope is unclear, the whole official legal website/service is offered for human review. Eight converted review artifacts remain only in the Monitor local-only archive; nine additional official-link records have no local capture. One superseded IBZ route is not relied upon. One French APD complaint snapshot is accepted as a dated 2026-10-19 copy; the direct page returned maintenance text on 2026-10-24. All candidate and capture copies have been removed from this pack; accepted source text is held under `documents/`, and unaccepted research artifacts are held only in the Monitor archive. Local PDF forms and the visa-routing matrix are preserved as native files under `documents/forms/` and `documents/guidance/`; their binary hashes match the downloaded originals. This does not itself establish topic coverage.
