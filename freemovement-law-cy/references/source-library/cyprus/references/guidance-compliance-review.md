## Evidence boundary

This review uses only recorded EGR passages and source records S23–S30 and S34–S40. It adds no source batch and does not claim that indexed passages are complete page text. The English Directive consolidation and original Greek Directive publication have different edition scopes; the Greek original does not establish later amendments. No claim here substitutes a secondary CyLaw text for primary law.

The indexed official pages remain useful discovery and bounded-reference leads even though direct automated access failed. A local copy is not required for ordinary research. Before relying on an operational requirement, a reviewer should open the official publisher link, identify the live wording and attachments relevant to the question, and record the review date and any visible version limits. If the page remains inaccessible, keep the point unresolved rather than reconstructing the entire legal regime from Gazette fragments.

## Handling rule

Keep guidance as guidance and legislation as legislation. Record the publisher, language, review date, relevant wording, applicable category and limits. Mark each issue supported, potentially overbroad, contradicted, unclear or unresolved only after comparing the actual wording with the relevant legal rule. Do not turn an unverified page excerpt into applicant instructions.
