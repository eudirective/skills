## Verified index scope

The current official page is titled **E.U./E.E.A. Citizens**. It says its links concern EU/EEA citizens and directs family members of EU/EEA citizens to a separate page. Its listed content includes:

- registration of EU citizens and family members who are also EU/EEA citizens (MEU1); and
- permanent residence for EU/EEA citizens and family members who are also EU/EEA citizens (MEU3).

The two routes are distinct from the page for third-country family members of EU/EEA citizens. The related current MEU1 page describes registration certificates; this index alone is only a navigation source and does not establish MEU1 or MEU3 eligibility, evidence, fees, deadlines, or procedure.
