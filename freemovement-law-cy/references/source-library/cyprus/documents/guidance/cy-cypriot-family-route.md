## Scope

The Migration Department page concerns entry and residence of third-country-national family members of Cypriot citizens under the Aliens and Immigration Law, Cap. 105 and Cyprus's stated facilitation policy. It separately identifies EU/EEA-family and UK free-movement cases. This summary does not merge those routes.

## Family categories described by the page

The page lists the following third-country-national categories:

- a spouse or civil-union partner of a Cypriot citizen;
- a direct ascendant of a minor Cypriot citizen, subject to the stated parental-care and contact-rights condition;
- a direct descendant of a Cypriot citizen or the citizen's spouse who is under 21 or dependent; and
- a dependent direct ascendant of the Cypriot citizen or the citizen's spouse.

It also identifies EU/EEA citizens and UK citizens with free-movement rights as separately facilitated categories. The definitions, relationship evidence, and any conditions must be checked against the current governing text and route-specific instructions.

## Entry, application and residence steps

The page says that a third-country family member may enter with a visa issued by a Cypriot consular authority or an entry permit requested by the Cypriot sponsor. For an existing valid residence permit, it describes re-entry with that permit and a valid passport where absence from Cyprus has not exceeded three months. For a pending application, the stamped payment receipt may support re-entry; if it is lost, the page describes a separate re-entry-permit request and says such permits are generally granted only for serious reasons.

For the residence application, the page states that the Cypriot citizen must attend, supporting documents must be officially translated and certified, applicable fees must be paid, and the applicant's biometrics and signature are collected. It describes a written notice after approval and a reason letter after refusal.

## Fees and timing stated on the page

The page lists:

- Remunerated single-permit residence and work issue/renewal: €80; first foreigner-register entry: €70.
- Non-remunerated employment residence/work issue/renewal: €50; first register entry: €70.
- Residence without employment issue/renewal: €50; first register entry: €70.

It states that complete applications are examined within four months, with longer processing where an application is incomplete. It says employment residence permits may be issued for up to five years; family members may remain without a fixed time limit only while the conditions continue to be met and a valid permit is held. The page states that specified non-dependent spouses/partners and parents of minor Cypriot citizens have labour-market access after obtaining a residence-and-employment permit.
