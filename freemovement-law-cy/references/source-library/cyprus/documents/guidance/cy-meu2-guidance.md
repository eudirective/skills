## Verified scope

The indexed page says that entry and residence rights of third-country family members of EU/EEA and Swiss citizens are provided by Cyprus's Union-citizen free-movement law. It lists spouses or civil-union partners, direct descendants under 21 or dependent, and dependent direct ascendants, including those of the sponsor's spouse or partner. It directs other family categories to separate residence routes.

The indexed guidance also describes:

- entry with a visa unless exempt, and a visa exemption for a valid residence card issued by Cyprus or another Member State;
- residence up to three months for a qualifying family member holding a valid passport and accompanying or joining the Union citizen;
- an application within four months of entry for a longer stay, with the Union citizen present and required to provide evidence of sufficient resources for themselves and the third-country family member;
- supporting documents, official translation and certification, a €20 fee, biometrics, a stated six-month examination period for complete applications, and possible interviews or additional evidence;
- a residence card ordinarily valid for five years or the sponsor's shorter intended residence period, renewal where the card is shorter and residence continues, and specified absences that do not affect validity;
- free access to employed and self-employed work; and
- personal retention conditions following the Union citizen's death or departure, and following divorce, annulment, or termination of a registered partnership.

## Wording and retrieval limits

The indexed text contains a terminology mismatch: the heading describes a **Residence Card (MEU2)**, but one application bullet calls it a “Registration Certificate.” This note preserves that discrepancy rather than assuming it is legally interchangeable with the MEU1 certificate.
