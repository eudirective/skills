## Verified scope

The official Greek page identifies entry and residence of third-country nationals who are family members of Cypriot citizens as governed by the Aliens and Immigration Law (Cap. 105), alongside a facilitating policy. The indexed text identifies these categories:

- a third-country national spouse or civil-union partner of a Cypriot citizen;
- a third-country national direct ascendant of a minor Cypriot citizen, provided a court has not removed parental care and contact rights;
- direct descendants of a Cypriot citizen or their spouse who are under 21 or dependent; and
- dependent direct ascendants of a Cypriot citizen or their spouse.

The page also describes personal retention of residence following divorce, annulment, or death, subject to factors including the length of marriage, a shared long-term legal or financial commitment, parental responsibilities, and immigration history. It states that employed family members may receive permits for up to five years; continued residence without a time limit depends on the residence conditions continuing to be met and a valid permit. It says non-dependent spouses/partners and parents of minor Cypriot citizens may access the labour market, including self-employment, after obtaining a residence and employment permit; a change of employer requires an application to renew that permit.
