## Verified scope

The official indexed MEU1 page describes registration of EU/EEA and Swiss citizens, and accompanying or joining family members who are also citizens within the stated “Union” grouping. It states:

- residence of up to three months without additional formalities beyond a valid passport or identity card;
- for residence beyond three months, the page lists employment/self-employment or sufficient resources together with comprehensive sickness insurance;
- a MEU1 application by the Union citizen and qualifying family members within four months of arrival;
- supporting documents, official translation and certification, fees, collection of a photograph, postal issue of an approved certificate, and a reasoned rejection letter;
- a stated €20 application fee, a one-month decision period for duly completed applications, and no expiry date for the registration certificate; and
- free access to employment, including employed and self-employed activity.

The page also states that failure to apply for the registration certificate may attract a financial penalty up to €2,500.
