## Publisher and content verification

The official OLC PDF route was checked against the Office of the Law Commissioner’s publication notice, which identifies the Greek consolidation of Cap. 105 as GEN-N.72. The searchable official PDF text identifies the instrument as the Aliens and Immigration Law (Cap. 105) and lists its sections, including entry, passports and visas, expulsion, immigration offences, family reunification and related regulation powers.

The complete PDF could not be retrieved and checked page by page in this review. Accordingly, this record does not accept its full text, article wording, amendment history or completeness as a validated transcription.

## Version and currency limits

The OLC publication is from 2024, not a current-as-of-2026 consolidation. The official Gazette published Law 4(I)/2025 amending Cap. 105 on 14 February 2025. Its section 29 states that commencement depends on a Cabinet notice in the Official Gazette; the notice and operative date have not been established in this review. See the separately maintained [bounded section 29 record](cy-law-4i-2025-s29.md).

Do not rely on this 2024 consolidation alone for current law or assume the 2025 amendment has commenced. Check the current OLC consolidation and Gazette for the commencement notice before resolving affected provisions. The direct OLC PDF endpoint timed out in the research browser; that access failure is not evidence that the official document is unavailable.
