## Confirmed publication scope

The official OLC index lists the Law 7(I)/2007 English publication. The indexed official PDF front matter identifies the instrument as the right of Union citizens and certain United Kingdom nationals and their family members to move and reside freely in Cyprus, Laws 2007 to 2023, and lists amendment laws 181(I)/2011, 8(I)/2013, 67(I)/2013, 77(I)/2015, 28(I)/2017, 193(I)/2020 and 96(I)/2023. It describes the document as an English translation and consolidation prepared by the Office of the Law Commissioner in Nicosia, May 2024.

The Migration Department's legislation page, published 19 December 2025, continues to list Law 7(I)/2007 among the laws it administers and describes its linked consolidations as unofficial. This confirms the law's continuing place in the Department's published legislation index; it does not confirm that the English OLC PDF is the latest text.
