Comparison seed: Denmark's EU free-movement residence regulation and official guidance. Findings from other country reviews were carried forward as search leads and acquisition-risk checks only. Slovenia’s GOV.SI expressly says that the Foreigners Act provisions applying to EU citizens also apply to EEA nationals and their family members; Swiss citizens and family members are described in a separate service route based on the EU–Swiss free-movement agreement. The EU, EEA EFTA, Swiss, returning Slovenian national, and UK Withdrawal Agreement routes must remain distinct unless a verified source itself groups them.

## Proposed route and disposition

For legal text, use the government-published PISRS consolidated version and retain its displayed edition and informational label. Do not reconstruct laws from Gazette issues. Consult a particular publication only for a known material change, commencement, or discrepancy. The PISRS API gate applies only to API use; a manual source link is enough for ordinary research. Copying or republication can be checked separately if required. OPSI's CC BY 4.0 notice applies to its listed basic-metadata dataset, not to full legal text.

For GOV.SI guidance, use stable topic or service pages as discovery parents and rediscover attachment links during each review. Mission pages, forms, fees and appointment instructions are mutable. For courts, record named collections, court, date/query limits and selective publication; the current search surfaces do not establish completeness.

As of 25 October 2026, 17 official references are accepted for their dated link-only scopes; PISRS legal records remain offered for direct human review because the review client cannot render their JavaScript pages. The 23 former candidate captures and two tracking files are archived locally, no local publisher copy is accepted, and no topic is complete. The PISRS API remains unused. The 19 captured shell/discovery responses are retained as source-specific discovery limits, not as a reason to reconstruct consolidated law from Gazette fragments.
