## Scope and relationship to predecessor procedures

The order approves the procedure for issuing temporary or permanent residence cards to a family member of a Union citizen. Its title covers both card types in one procedure. The official order preamble cites the Aliens Law, the Residence Declaration Law and Directive 2004/38/EC.

The official e-TAR chronology identifies these amendments to the order:

| Date | Change |
|---|---|
| 2019-07-01 | Order entered into force |
| 2020-06-23 | Amended by Order 1V-630 |
| 2022-11-18 | Amended by Order 1V-702 |
| 2023-04-04 | Amended by Order 1V-192 |
| 2023-04-05 | Consolidated edition effective from this date; e-TAR displayed no end date and marked the order in force when checked 2026-10-29 |

This combined procedure replaced the separate temporary-card Order 1V-290 and permanent-card Order 1V-369 from 1 July 2019. The library retains those predecessors as historical records only.
