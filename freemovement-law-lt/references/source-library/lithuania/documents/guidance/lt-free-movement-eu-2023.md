## Source record

The official page gives a short institutional overview of Lithuanian residence documents for EU citizens and their family members. It identifies territorial migration divisions of police institutions under the Migration Department as issuing temporary and permanent residence documents and states that the Department also withdraws those residence rights.

The page does not provide a current application checklist, form, fee schedule, processing timeline, or detailed decision procedure. It is retained as dated evidence of the Department's stated role and the scope of this page, not as current applicant instructions.
