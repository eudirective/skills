The page says that, once at the applicant's choice, a foreigner who completed studies or research and experimental development work in Lithuania is not charged the listed general-order fee for processing and issuing a temporary residence permit. This is a permit-specific statement; the page does not clearly extend it to family-member cards.

## Fee exemptions and scope

The page places a separate list of exemptions beneath the temporary residence permit section. It names categories including resistance/occupation-status holders, children under 16, people granted asylum and family members joining them, certain minors under Lithuanian guardianship, people documenting Lithuanian origin or restoration/preservation of citizenship rights and their family members, people who cannot be returned or deported, people unable to leave for dangerous health reasons, specified students or trainees, certain trafficking/illegal-labour victims cooperating with authorities, and UK citizens and family members whose residence rights were acquired before the end of the transition period.

A second list under the permanent-residence-permit section repeats only the first three categories shown on this page: resistance/occupation-status holders, children under 16, and people granted asylum and family members joining them.

The page does not provide a separate exemption table expressly tied to the EU-family residence-card charges. Do not assume that an exemption listed under the temporary or permanent residence-permit headings also applies to a Union-citizen family-member card. Verify any claimed exemption against the legal basis and the current service instructions.
