## Visa categories described

The page distinguishes:

- **Airport transit visa (A):** valid for transit through international transit areas of one or more Schengen Member State airports.
- **Short-stay visa (C):** valid for transit through or an intended stay in the Schengen area for no more than 90 days in any 180-day period.
- **National visa (D):** may be issued for a foreigner's long-stay purpose in Lithuania, for no longer than 12 months. The page says the Migration Department under the Ministry of the Interior decides national-visa applications.

The MFA page links an online visa application form and Migration Department information. It says that from 1 July (the page does not state a year in this sentence), foreign nationals outside Lithuania apply for national visas through the external service provider selected by the Migration Department, rather than through Lithuanian diplomatic missions or consular offices. The linked Migration Department announcement dates that change to 1 July 2023.

## Currentness and operational limits

The MFA page itself displays a renewal date of 9 September 2026. Its category definitions are recorded as shown on that page; this note does not establish every national-visa ground, an individual's eligibility, or the conditions attached to a visa.

The national-visa submission route is not a fixed list of consulates. The Migration Department's current guidance directs applicants to MIGRIS and an external-service-provider appointment workflow; provider locations and application restrictions can change. Check the live [services for persons abroad](https://migracija.lrv.lt/en/structure-and-contacts/information-for-visitors/services-for-persons-abroad/) and relevant application instructions before booking. The MFA page's general D-visa description does not enumerate all grounds or explain exceptions.

- [Migration Department announcement dated 14 June 2023 on national-visa applications from abroad](https://migracija.lrv.lt/en/news/the-procedure-for-issuing-national-visas-is-being-changed-this-will-be-taken-care-of-by-the-migration-department/)
- [Migration Department current services for persons abroad](https://migracija.lrv.lt/en/structure-and-contacts/information-for-visitors/services-for-persons-abroad/)

Do not conflate short-stay Schengen visas with national D visas or residence permits. This page is an overview; detailed filing rules, competent authority, supporting documents, fee, prior consultation, decision and complaint routes are set out on separate linked pages.
