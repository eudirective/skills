# Lithuania: residence permits since 2020

**Source ID:** `lt-13`  
**Source type:** Official document-identification and specimen guidance  
**Publisher:** Migration Department under the Ministry of the Interior of the Republic of Lithuania  
**Official page:** [Residence permits since 2020](https://migracija.lrv.lt/en/activities/identity-documents/residence-permits-documents/residence-permits-since-2020/)  
**Language reviewed:** English  
**Page last updated:** 9 October 2025 (publisher display)  
**Checked:** 29 October 2026

## Source content

The Migration Department page says that the Identity Documents Personalisation Centre began issuing a new form of Lithuanian residence permit on 17 September 2020. It describes three document groups:

1. **Temporary residence permit:** a permit for an alien with a stated period of temporary residence. The page says the card stores personal data, a facial image and fingerprints.
2. **Family member of a Union citizen:** a separate residence card for a non-EU family member of an EU citizen, with temporary or long-term residence wording. The page associates card-title changes from 2 August 2021 with Article 7(2) of Regulation (EU) 2019/1157.
3. **Long-term resident and permanent residence material:** the page describes a Lithuanian long-term resident permit as confirming permanent-resident status and separately describes a family member's permanent residence card.

The page also displays specimen image links for temporary permits, family-member cards, long-term resident permits and cards for UK citizens after Brexit. Link labels include `IT_2020`, `IT_SP_2020`, `IT_SP_2021`, `IR_2020`, `IR_SP_2021`, and `IT_50str_2021` / `IR_50str_2021`. The page links a two-page 2020 bilingual Lithuanian/English leaflet, “Residence Permits in the Republic of Lithuania.” The leaflet is published by the Identity Documents Personalisation Centre under the Ministry of the Interior and describes the temporary permit, long-term resident permit, and EU-citizen family-member card. It also describes security features of the 2020 card format. This Markdown summarizes those items; it does not reproduce or embed the leaflet or images.

## Version and legal dependencies

The page is a dated document guide, not a current entitlement or application procedure. It cites Regulation (EU) 2019/1157 for the family-member card naming rule. EUR-Lex records that regulation as no longer in force from 9 July 2025, declared invalid in Case C-61/22 and replaced by Council Regulation (EU) 2025/1208, applying from 10 July 2025. The page's 2019/1157 citation is therefore historical.

- [Council Regulation (EU) 2025/1208, official ELI record](https://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/1208/oj)
- [Regulation (EU) 2019/1157, EUR-Lex status record](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32019R1157)
- [Case C-61/22, EUR-Lex case record](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A62022CJ0061)
- [Council Regulation (EC) No 1030/2002, uniform format for third-country-national residence permits](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32002R1030)

Regulation 1030/2002 concerns the uniform format for third-country-national residence permits and excludes EU free-movement family members from its scope; the distinct EU-family card format is addressed by the relevant free-movement instrument. EUR-Lex showed the latest consolidated 1030/2002 text as 21 November 2017 when checked. Confirm the current Lithuanian legal basis and applicable EU instrument for the specific card type and decision date.

## Use and limits

Accepted as dated official identification guidance for the document types and specimen links the page presents. Do not use it alone to establish current eligibility, entitlement, application procedure, card validity, security requirements, or post-Brexit status. The EU, EFTA/EEA, Swiss and UK Withdrawal Agreement routes remain distinct. The page's images were not visually inspected, and their labels do not establish their present validity.

The page uses older legal references and does not provide a complete process, document checklist, fees, processing time or remedies. Its stated 2020, 2021 and 2022 specimen dates are not evidence that the pictured format is the one currently issued.
