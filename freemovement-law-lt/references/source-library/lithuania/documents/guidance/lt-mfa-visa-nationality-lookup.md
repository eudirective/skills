## What the official page provides

The page is a nationality-based visa lookup under the Lithuanian MFA consular-information site. It presents a searchable country selector and an alphabetical set of nationality names, followed by visa guidance pages for visa types, competent authorities, application lodging and supporting documents, fees, prior consultation, decisions, external service providers, EU/EFTA family members, complaints, visa holders, and personal-data processing.

This is a dynamic lookup rather than a stable country-by-country text list. The text rendering checked on 29 October 2026 exposed the selector and nationality labels but not the selected-country result. This record therefore confirms the official lookup route and page version, not the visa requirement for any particular nationality.

## Use and limits

For a nationality-specific result, use the current selector on the official page and record the nationality, result, displayed conditions, and date checked. Follow any linked exceptions and the current Schengen rules. Do not infer a country's visa-free status from its appearance in the selector, or treat this Markdown as a cached result list.

A nationality-level visa requirement does not determine permission to work or reside, length or purpose of stay, border admission, or individual eligibility for a visa exemption. Verify travel-document type, purpose, duration, territorial scope, and applicable exceptions against the current official guidance.
