## Form contents

The PDF is a combined issue/replace form for temporary or permanent residence cards for family members of Union citizens. The applicant enters identity, nationality, Lithuanian personal code if assigned, former and Lithuanian residence addresses, contact details, travel-document information, and the Union or Lithuanian citizen they join or with whom they reside.

The grounds section contains checkboxes for:

- spouse and registered partner;
- direct descendants under 21 or dependent, including specified descendants of a spouse or registered partner;
- a dependent adopted child and a dependent direct ascendant;
- other persons under EU free-movement rules, including a durable partner (the form says a continuous relationship for the preceding three years), dependent person, household member, or a person needing personal care for serious health reasons;
- retained residence circumstances after death, departure, annulment, divorce or partnership dissolution;
- permanent residence after a five-year period and the form's listed situations for permanent residence in less than five years.

The remaining sections ask for the reason for replacing a card, the intended temporary-card term where relevant, state-charge amount and date, email for the decision notice, attached documents, and submission by the applicant or an identified representative. The form includes a declaration concerning responsibility for false information and consent to processing personal data, including criminal-record data, for residence-related decisions.

This is a summary of the form fields and categories, not a legal restatement of the listed eligibility grounds. Use the controlling Lithuanian legislation and current procedure for eligibility and evidence.

## Identity, currentness and limitations

The PDF itself identifies Order No. 3K-92 and Annex 9. The direct file is on the official Migration Department domain and was opened as a six-page PDF. No revision date or version history is visible in the reviewed PDF.

The English title says “temporary residence card,” while the Lithuanian title and bilingual body provide temporary or permanent options. Some English wording is awkward or ambiguous. The file must not be silently translated into a more precise eligibility rule.

The official Migration Department service list currently distinguishes issuance/change of temporary and permanent EU-family cards: [official services list](https://migracija.lrv.lt/lt/paslaugos/visas-paslaugu-sarasas/). A separate Lithuanian-language three-page permanent-card application form was also opened on the official Ministry domain on 29 October 2026: [permanent residence-card application form](https://migracija.lrv.lt/uploads/migracija/documents/files/Paslaugos/Pra%C5%A1ym%C5%B3%20formos/LLG%20ir%20LNG/%C5%A0eimos%20nario%20LNG%20i%C5%A1duoti.pdf). Its form structure and statutory references differ from this combined 2019 PDF. Its presence does not establish which temporary-card form or which version any individual service currently requires; check the current service page and its linked attachment. Do not use it as a current filing form without checking the specific live service and its linked attachment.

The form's basis includes Article 101 of Lithuania's Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and refers to EU-citizen family members and Lithuanian citizens who exercised EU free movement. This document does not itself establish that all the form's categories remain current, that an attached document list is complete, that a Lithuanian-citizen route always applies, or that EU, EEA/EFTA and Swiss routes are interchangeable.
