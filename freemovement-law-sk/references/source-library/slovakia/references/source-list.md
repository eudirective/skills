# Slovakia national-source register

Research and source-note review updated: 2026-10-29. Scope remains bounded official-source discovery for EU/EEA/Swiss free movement, third-country family residence, visas and mission practice, administration and remedies, family evidence, privacy, oversight, legal aid, and court-search routes. This is not a legal-equivalence opinion or a complete national inventory.

Comparison seed: Denmark's EU free-movement residence regulation and guidance. Findings from other jurisdictions were used as discovery leads only. EEA EFTA, Swiss, EU, returning-Slovak-national, and UK Withdrawal Agreement routes remain distinct unless the cited Slovak source expressly groups them.

Status: all 40 original source records have final notes under [documents/references/](../documents/references/), and SK-41 has been added as a 41st maintained reference. Thirty-eight official external references are accepted within recorded scope; three have a not-accepted or pending-verification disposition. Four current statutory transcriptions (Acts 71/1967, 404/2011, 365/2004 and 36/2005; versions effective 2018-09-01, 2026-10-01, 2025-07-01 and 2026-09-01 respectively) and zero topics are accepted; country review remains open. The other 31 converted candidate texts and captured source inputs are preserved in the Monitor's indexed local-only archive. Eight earlier MZV capture attempts returned HTTP 403; selected current pages were independently reviewed through official browser routes.
