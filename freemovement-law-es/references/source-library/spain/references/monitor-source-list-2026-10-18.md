# Spain official-source discovery register

**Research date:** 18 October 2026; retrieval check 19 October 2026; official-link review through 25 October 2026. **Scope:** central first-party sources relevant to EU-family mobility/residence, EEA EFTA and Swiss routes, family members of Spanish nationals, official visas, procedure/remedies, access/privacy, oversight, legal aid, civil-status evidence and selected courts. **Seed:** Denmark's EU free-movement residence regulation and guidance. **Status:** the expanded register has 52 source records; 54 official references are accepted within reviewed scopes; 20 local source documents in this expanded register are accepted (13 BOE laws, three Ministry forms, three BOE technical/reuse documents, and the SEM 2/2025 instruction); no topic is accepted. Nine additional treaty, oversight, consular and document-authentication records are link-only notes; raw HTML captures are archived and not accepted as source texts. CENDOJ is accepted only as a discovery route: no immigration query batch or case-law coverage is recorded, and portal-level search review remains open. The initial run saved 34 responses, converted and replay-verified 30 pages, found four dynamic pages without stable text, and logged 12 retrieval/conversion failures. Those failures do not bar public-link use. Raw responses, converted candidates, and retrieval/capture manifests are retained in the Monitor local-only archive. See [official reference decisions](source-acceptance.json) and [issue-led coverage](coverage-register.md). The shared GET helper is optional for retained local copies. For es-06, es-07, and es-09, raw captures and exact pre-promotion candidates are archived locally; accepted texts are under `documents/legislation/`.

Relationship means functional relationship to the Danish seed or a relevant supporting-source role. It does not mean legal equivalence. Verification and search outcome remain independent. The current link review checked responsible official publishers and preserves each displayed date and limitation. N-Lex is accepted only as a discovery gateway; BOE is the Spanish national-law publisher.
